# Pulse 230 — Wave F transportation deterministic calibration

Adds the FY2025-FY2035 transportation calibration. Eleven annual rows exercise
baseline, transition, provisional-productivity, and adverse paths. Deterministic
checks recompute primary outlays, net cash, fund-balance change, debt, and
subsequent net interest.

Incomplete access, quality, equity, resilience, and delivery floors block the
modeled productivity reduction, leaving target cost and rate outputs null.
