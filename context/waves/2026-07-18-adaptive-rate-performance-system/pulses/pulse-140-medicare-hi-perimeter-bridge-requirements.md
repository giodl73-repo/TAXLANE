# Pulse 140 — Medicare HI perimeter-bridge requirements

Pulse 140 defines the evidence and modeling requirements needed before Medicare
HI payroll context can become a matched assigned receipt base.

It preserves Medicare HI trust-fund separation and keeps the diagnostic
source-yield-to-payroll ratio blocked from statutory-rate, effective-rate,
solver, public-rate-card, tax-proposal, savings, waste/fraud, technology-savings,
and balanced-budget claims.
