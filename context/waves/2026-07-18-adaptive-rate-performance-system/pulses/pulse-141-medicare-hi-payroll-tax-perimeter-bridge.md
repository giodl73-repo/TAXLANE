# Pulse 141 — Medicare HI payroll-tax perimeter bridge

Pulse 141 partially evidences the CMS Medicare HI payroll-tax-yield perimeter
from the captured 2026 Medicare Trustees Report.

It identifies FY2025 CMS payroll taxes and separately identifies income from
taxation of OASDI benefits. It does not complete the OMB Hospital Insurance
anchor bridge and does not publish assigned bases, rates, solver inputs, public
rate cards, tax proposals, savings, waste/fraud findings, technology-savings
claims, or balanced-budget claims.
