# Pulse 163 — Defense outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 163 extends the outcome-floor definition packet pattern to the national
defense lane.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and defense-specific floor
concepts: treaty commitments, readiness, personnel safety, strategic reserve,
and force-structure/procurement feasibility.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, force-structure plan,
procurement schedule, federal score, target cost, savings estimate, solver
input, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget
claim is populated. Missing values remain null and blocked gates remain false.
