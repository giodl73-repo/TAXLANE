# Pulse 167 — Payment-integrity outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 167 extends the outcome-floor definition packet pattern to the
payment-integrity overlay.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and payment-integrity floor
concepts: due process, timely payment, access, false-positive rate, and causal
prevention or same-cohort collection lineage.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, fraud finding, waste
finding, recoverable savings credit, federal score, savings estimate, solver
input, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget
claim is populated. Missing values remain null and blocked gates remain false.
