# Pulse 98 — Transportation trust-fund accounting boundary

Pulse 98 adds
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/transportation_pilot_trust_fund_accounting_boundary.v1.draft.json`.

It uses the already-local OMB Appendix Chapter 13 funds PDF to lock the
transportation trust-fund accounting boundary:

- trust funds remain separate;
- general-fund transfers must be explicit;
- borrowing from the general fund is financing, not a receipt;
- repayment of borrowing is not an outlay;
- future rows must preserve primary outlays, net cash requirement, and fund
  balance change identities.

The pulse does not capture Table 13-4, does not publish annual trust-fund
values, does not complete reconciliation, and does not open rate, savings,
solver, technology-savings, waste/fraud, department-cut, or balanced-budget
claims.
