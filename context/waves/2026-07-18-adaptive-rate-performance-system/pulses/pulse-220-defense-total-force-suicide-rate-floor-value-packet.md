# Pulse 220 — Defense Total Force suicide-rate floor-value packet

Captured the official CY2024 Annual Report on Suicide in the Military and added one draft personnel-safety no-regression ceiling: 23.2 deaths per 100,000 Service members. The packet preserves the report's non-statistically-significant year-over-year boundary and leaves readiness, force structure, procurement, policy/stress values, pass/fail evidence, solver inputs, rates, savings, and balanced-budget claims blocked.
