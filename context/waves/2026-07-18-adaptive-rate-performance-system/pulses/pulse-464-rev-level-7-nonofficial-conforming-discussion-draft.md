# Pulse 464 — REV Level 7 nonofficial conforming discussion draft

Added a narrow discussion draft keyed to the current 26 U.S.C. §1(j)
structure. It covers all four individual filing-status tables, substitutes the
seven modeled rates, preserves current-law thresholds and indexing, excludes
estates and trusts, and includes effective-date and administration provisions.

The draft closes a controllable preparation gap but not the external gate. It
is not Legislative Counsel text, an introduced bill, an official proposal,
enacted law, an official score, or a certified rate or balance determination.
