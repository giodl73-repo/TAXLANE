# Pulse 221 — Disaster life-safety floor-value packet

Captured the official NOAA/NCEI 1980–2024 event report and added one draft life-safety ceiling: 568 deaths associated with 27 qualifying 2024 disasters. The packet explicitly records the missing exposure and event-severity normalization and leaves preparedness, response/recovery, mitigation, policy/stress values, pass/fail evidence, savings, and balanced-budget claims blocked.
