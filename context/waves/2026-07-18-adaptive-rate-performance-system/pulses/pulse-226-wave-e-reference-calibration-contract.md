# Pulse 226 — Wave E reference-calibration contract

## Outcome

Defined the Wave E completion boundary as one role-reviewed, current-policy
continuation reference calibration per lane.

## Contract

- Central performance values are identity projections of source-custodied Wave D
  anchors.
- Stress values move exactly one reported increment across the no-regression
  boundary in the adverse direction.
- Central results must pass and stress results must fail.
- The calibration does not create reform effects, fiscal scores, target costs,
  rates, savings, or policy recommendations.

## Boundary

This pulse defines reproducible scenario and comparator semantics. It does not
claim actual policy performance or admit lower-cost scenarios.
