# Pulse 232 — Wave G official current-law solver spine contract

Wave G is the official FY2025-FY2035 current-law federal topline solver spine.
It precedes real reform scoring because reform deltas need one fixed baseline
horizon, projection vintage, sign convention, financing residual, debt
rollforward, and net-interest lineage.

Acceptance requires official raw custody, eleven complete annual rows, one
FY2026-FY2035 projection vintage, an explicit FY2025 actual bridge, exact
topline and debt identities, eight-role review, and deterministic validation.

Wave G does not fill missing 17-row or fund allocations, choose reserve or
receipt-base parameters, score a reform, select a target cost, calculate a
rate, claim savings, or claim a balanced budget.
