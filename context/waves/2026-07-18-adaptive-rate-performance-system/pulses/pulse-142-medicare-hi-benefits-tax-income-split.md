# Pulse 142 — Medicare HI benefits-tax and income split

Implemented `medicare_hi_benefits_tax_income_split.v1.draft.json` and public
reader.

This pulse uses already-captured CMS Medicare Trustees FY2025 HI trust-fund
operations to split payroll taxes, taxation of OASDI benefits, and other
non-payroll income categories. It keeps the OMB Hospital Insurance receipt-row
mapping incomplete and keeps every assigned-base, rate, solver, tax proposal,
savings, fraud, waste, technology-savings, and balanced-budget claim blocked.
