# Pulse 139 — Medicare HI receipt-base reconciliation

Pulse 139 reconciles captured FY2025 CMS Medicare HI taxable-payroll and
payroll-tax-yield context against the existing OMB Hospital Insurance receipt
anchor.

The pulse computes a diagnostic source reconciliation ratio and the CMS-vs-OMB
receipt-anchor difference. It does not publish an assigned receipt base, a
statutory rate, an effective rate, solver input, public rate card, tax proposal,
savings, waste/fraud finding, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget
claim.
