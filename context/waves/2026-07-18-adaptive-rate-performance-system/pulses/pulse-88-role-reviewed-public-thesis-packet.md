# Pulse 88 — Role-Reviewed Public Thesis Packet

## Scope

Create the public explanation for what the adaptive rate and performance system
can and cannot prove.

## Artifacts

- `data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/public_thesis_packet.v1.draft.json`
- `data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/public_thesis_packet.schema.md`
- `docs/reading/public-thesis-packet.md`
- `reviews/2026-07-18-public-thesis-packet-role-review.md`

## Boundary

This pulse does not publish statutory rates, effective rates, a public rate
card, a tax proposal, savings estimate, waste finding, fraud finding,
department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, solver result, pilot
selection, or balanced-budget claim.

## Acceptance coverage

- Public language says "overspending risk" rather than unsupported "waste."
- Technology is explained as a transition path with timing, costs, measured
  productivity, stress cases, and floors.
- Blocked and not-calculated rates are explained as valid public outcomes.
- The packet includes all eight role-review findings and preserves P1 blockers.
