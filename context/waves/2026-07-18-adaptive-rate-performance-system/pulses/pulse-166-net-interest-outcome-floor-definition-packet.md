# Pulse 166 — Net-interest outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 166 extends the outcome-floor definition packet pattern to the net-interest
lane.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and net-interest-specific
floor concepts: full and timely debt service, endogenous interest formula,
debt/maturity/rate path, primary-balance feedback, and stress resilience.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, direct cut amount, debt
path, maturity path, rate path, federal score, target cost, savings estimate,
solver input, solver run, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim,
or balanced-budget claim is populated. Missing values remain null and blocked
gates remain false.
