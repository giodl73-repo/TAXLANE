# Pulse 165 — Revenue-solvency outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 165 extends the outcome-floor definition packet pattern to the
revenue-solvency overlay.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and revenue-specific floor
concepts: distributional progressivity, revenue stability, administrability,
growth sensitivity, and matched base/behavior/incidence readiness.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, receipt-base model,
incidence model, distributional score, statutory rate, effective rate, tax
proposal, savings estimate, solver input, department-cut instruction,
technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget claim is populated. Missing values
remain null and blocked gates remain false.
