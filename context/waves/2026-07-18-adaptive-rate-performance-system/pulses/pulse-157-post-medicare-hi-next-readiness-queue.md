# Pulse 157 — Post-Medicare HI next-readiness queue

Pulse 157 publishes the next-readiness queue after the Medicare HI closure
blocker series.

The queue preserves that Medicare HI rates and solver inputs remain blocked. It
orders the next work across source custody, fund reconciliation, outcome floors,
policy-specific scores, receipt bases, payment-integrity lineage, net-interest
feedback, and deterministic solver dry run.

No values, solver run, rates, public rate cards, tax proposals, savings,
waste/fraud findings, technology-savings claims, or balanced-budget claims are
populated.

No FOIA request, records request, form, email, phone call, or agency/person
contact was submitted.
