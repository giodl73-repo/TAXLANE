# Pulse 169 — Transportation/infrastructure outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 169 extends the outcome-floor definition packet pattern to the
transportation/infrastructure lane.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and lane floor concepts:
asset condition, fatalities, reliability, access, climate resilience, and
asset-inventory/maintenance-gap delivery feasibility.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, asset inventory,
maintenance-gap estimate, federal/state/local translation, simulator run,
federal score, target cost, savings estimate, solver input, department-cut
instruction, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget claim is populated.
Missing values remain null and blocked gates remain false.
