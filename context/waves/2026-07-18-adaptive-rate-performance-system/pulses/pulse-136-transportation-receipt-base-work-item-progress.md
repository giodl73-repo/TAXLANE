# Pulse 136 — Transportation receipt-base work-item progress

Pulse 136 records source-custodied FY2025 transportation and airport-and-airway
excise receipt-yield context from existing local OMB Historical Table 2.4
custody.

It deliberately does not convert those receipt rows into legal or economic
assigned receipt bases. Rates, solver inputs, public rate cards, tax proposals,
savings, waste/fraud findings, technology-savings claims, and balanced-budget
claims remain blocked.

No external request was submitted and no agency or person was contacted.
