# Pulse 161 — Health outcome floor definition packet

Pulse 161 converts the Pulse 160 outcome-floor threshold blocker into the first
lane-specific floor definition packet for the health/Medicare lane.

The packet defines the five mandatory floor classes and the health lane's
contract-specific floor concepts: coverage, access, quality,
risk-adjusted outcomes, and rural/safety-net capacity.

No threshold value, observed value, pass/fail result, lower-cost scenario
admissibility, target cost, federal effect, savings estimate, solver input,
rate, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget
claim is populated. Missing values remain null and blocked gates remain false.
