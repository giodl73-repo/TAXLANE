# Pulse 233 — Post-F Wave G through L roadmap

The post-F frontier is divided into six independently closable waves:

1. G: official current-law topline spine;
2. H: fund, reserve, transfer, offset, and endogenous-interest substrate;
3. I: first real transportation reform admission;
4. J: receipt-base, incidence, distribution, and administration bridge;
5. K: integrated internal candidate solver and rate calculation; and
6. L: first public pilot card and closure review.

The dependency chain is strict for output admission even when discovery work
overlaps. One pilot does not establish all-lane readiness. No public rate,
savings estimate, technology-savings claim, or balanced-budget claim is opened
by this roadmap.
