# Pulse 120 — Current-law source-custody packet template

Pulse 120 defines the exact future custody packet shape required before
current-law annual values may be populated.

Artifacts:

- `data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/current_law_source_custody_packet_template.v1.draft.json`
- `data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/current_law_source_custody_packet_template.schema.md`
- `docs/reading/current-law-source-custody-packet-template.md`

Boundary:

This pulse creates a template only. It captures no source, publishes no
current-law value, submits no external request, contacts no agency or person,
runs no solver, calculates no rates, publishes no target costs, scores no
savings, identifies no waste or fraud, issues no department-cut instructions,
claims no technology savings, and makes no balanced-budget claim.
