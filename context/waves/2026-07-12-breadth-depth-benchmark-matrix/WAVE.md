# Wave: Breadth, Depth, And Benchmark Matrix

## Goal

Create one controlled scoreboard showing which fiscal areas TAXLANE covers,
which have current and benchmark values on matched definitions, and which still
need depth. Keep efficiency comparisons, improper payments, fraud, and
recoverable savings as distinct evidence classes.

## Status

Active in depth mode. Breadth is closed: all 17 questions across 13 lanes have a
sourced current top line and zero remain Tier 3 gaps. Five questions (29.4%)
have a matched benchmark; 12 (70.6%) have a federal top line but still need a
scope- and outcome-matched expected value.

Retained depth work includes the headline-basis crosswalk; veterans,
transportation, education, disaster, justice, science/energy/environment,

baskets; and the distribution screen. The public scoreboard is canonical for
coverage status and the prioritized depth queue.

The K–12 outcome baseline attaches national public-school NAEP 2024 grade 4
and grade 8 mathematics and reading distributions plus the school year 2021–22
adjusted cohort graduation rate and selected subgroup rates. The periods are
explicitly mapped and are not treated as the same cohort; the result is a
descriptive baseline, not a causal spending-efficiency or savings score.

The attendance/access extension adds school year 2022–23 chronic absenteeism:
13.407M reported chronically absent students over 48.268M membership, or 27.8%,
with subgroup distributions. National aggregation can duplicate mobile
students, membership and absence have different collection windows, and state
definitions vary, so it is not a unique-student count or comparable state rank.

The PISA 2022 extension supplies the first definition-matched K–12 outcome peer
comparison: U.S. mathematics was close to the OECD average, while reading and
science were higher. U.S. school participation and exclusion missed PISA
technical targets. PISA Level 2 is not crosswalked to NAEP Proficient, and the
OECD outcome average is not treated as a spending target.

Education at a Glance 2025 adds a definition-matched 2022 resource peer:
government expenditure per full-time-equivalent student from primary through
post-secondary non-tertiary education was $14,603 PPP-adjusted in the United
States versus $12,438 across the OECD. Sharing a reference year with PISA does
not assign spending to tested students, so no joint causal benchmark is scored.

The WIOA PY2024 extension adds national administrative outcomes for Adult,
Dislocated Worker, Youth, and Wagner-Peyser participants. It is a partial
program-level view relevant to subfunction 504, not an account crosswalk or a
counterfactual evaluation. Differing outcome cohorts and the Youth education-
or-training definition are preserved; cost per outcome and savings stay blocked.

The BLS CPS 2024 extension supplies population, labor-force participation,
employment-population, unemployment, and median weekly earnings context by

earnings use different universes, and the cross-sectional population record is

The training-and-employment account bridge reconciles all 13 nonzero FY2025
DOL/ETA rows exactly to $5.434B. DOL budget activities identify the Adult,

The WIA Gold Standard extension adds national randomized evidence from 28
local areas and 2011–13 enrollment. Intensive-service access raised cumulative
earnings in both survey and administrative data; the training-access contrast
was inconclusive because training take-up differed by only nine percentage
points. These are historical WIA study effects, not PY2024 WIOA impacts or
FY2025 account savings.

The Census CPS October 2024 extension adds national age-enrollment denominators

age 15–24 reported as graduating that year, 2.088 million were enrolled in a
listed college, graduate, or vocational category. The derived 64.246% uses
rounded survey estimates; it is not an ACGR cohort, public-school membership,

The experimental-Pell extension adds randomized evidence from 46 volunteer
schools and 2,684 primary education-analysis students. Offers increased study-
school enrollment by 26.0 and 14.6 percentage points and completion by 16.7 and
9.3 points across the two experiments. Neither experiment detected a medium-
to long-term employment or earnings effect; those nulls do not prove harm,
equivalence, or zero effect. Grant amounts among users are not full program
costs and cannot be joined to FY2025 outlays.

The FY2024 Federal Student Aid extension adds current Title IV delivery scale:

## Design rules

- Never invent an expected value for a policy-dependent lane.
- Match unit, scope, and period before computing a comparison.
- Treat peer averages as comparisons, not automatic targets.
- Never infer fraud from an international cost difference.
- Never convert improper payments into recoverable savings without separate
  reviewed evidence.
