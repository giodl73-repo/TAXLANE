# Pulse 16: WIA Gold Standard Impact Evidence

## Result

Added a historical randomized-control-trial layer for WIA Adult and Dislocated
Worker services. Intensive-service access produced positive earnings impacts;
the marginal training evidence remains inconclusive because training receipt
differed by only nine percentage points and both earnings contrasts were not
statistically significant.

## Decision gate

Pass for causal inference within the randomized historical WIA service-access
contrasts and for preserving the study's society, customer, and taxpayer
benefit-cost perspectives. Fail for automatic transport to current WIOA,
joining the impacts to FY2025 subfunction 504 outlays, current cost per outcome,
fraud, or recoverable savings.

## Next bounded action

Find a current WIOA impact evaluation with a defined counterfactual and a
cohort-compatible program expenditure boundary before attempting a current
cost-effectiveness or fiscal estimate.
