consolidated public K–12 frame: $994.922B revenue, split 11.57% federal, 45.23%
state, and 43.20% local; and $983.713B total expenditure.

OMB subfunction 501 remains a separate federal public-purpose view. Its

which already includes $115.085B received from federal sources.

## Decision gate

Pass for school-system federalism scale and published current spending per
pupil. Fail for an expected spending level, learning-efficiency finding, fraud,
or recoverable savings.
