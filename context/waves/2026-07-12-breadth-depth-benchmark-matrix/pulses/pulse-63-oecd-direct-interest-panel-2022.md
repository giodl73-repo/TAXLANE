# Pulse 63: OECD Direct Interest Panel, 2022

## Result

- Captures 11 direct calendar-2022 general-government D.41 interest-payable
  observations from OECD annual government accounts.
- Uses the already captured same-year OECD GDP values to derive interest as a
  share of GDP without currency conversion.
- Combines that observed ratio with the separately labeled IMF revenue ratio
  to show interest as a share of general-government revenue.
- Leaves Singapore missing and rejects primary-minus-overall balance as an
  interest proxy.
- Labels D.41 as gross interest payable, not net interest after receipts or
  United States federal net interest.
- Keeps ranking, target, efficiency, and savings gates blocked.

## Coverage

The 11 core peers are complete. Interest payable ranges from 0.51 percent of
GDP in Sweden to 4.19 percent in the United Kingdom. As a share of revenue, the
United States and United Kingdom exceed 10 percent in this 2022 observation;
that is descriptive fiscal-pressure context, not evidence of an efficient peer
target or an available saving.
