# Pulse 10: K–12 Attendance Access Baseline

## Result

Attached the Department of Education's school year 2022–23 chronic-absenteeism
baseline: 13,406,900 reported chronically absent students over 48,268,200
membership, or 27.8%, with national race/ethnicity, disability, and English-
learner distributions.

## Decision gate

Pass for a descriptive attendance/access indicator and subgroup distribution.
Fail for a unique-student national count, comparable state ranking, causal
spending efficiency, fraud, or recoverable savings. National aggregation can
duplicate mobile students, membership and absence use different collection
windows, and state definitions vary.

keeping the peer average descriptive rather than an automatic target.
