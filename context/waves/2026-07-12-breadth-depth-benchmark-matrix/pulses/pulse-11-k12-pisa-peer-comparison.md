# Pulse 11: K–12 PISA Peer Comparison

## Result

Attached a definition-matched PISA 2022 U.S.-to-OECD-average outcome
comparison. U.S. scores were 465 versus 472 in mathematics, 504 versus 476 in
reading, and 499 versus 485 in science, with matched Level 2 and Level 5/6
shares.

## Decision gate

Pass for a same-assessment descriptive peer comparison. Fail for a spending
target, causal efficiency, or a crosswalk from PISA Level 2 to NAEP Proficient.
OECD's U.S. sampling caveat is mandatory because school participation and
student exclusion missed technical targets.
