# Pulse 04: VA PLTSS Recovery Bridge

## Result

Tested the projected-overpayment-to-recovery bridge and retained a structured
failed match. The Q4 2025 PLTSS scorecard has three program recovery rows, but
their FY2025-quarter and FY2023–FY2025 operational periods do not match the
October 2022–September 2023 sample behind the $218.30M FY2024 projection.

The FY2024 annual recovery table is VA-wide rather than PLTSS-specific. Neither
source provides debt- or claim-cohort lineage from projected error to
receivable, disposition, and collection.

## Decision gate

Pass for reporting the estimate and recovery rows separately with their own
periods and bases. Fail for a recovery percentage, recoverable-dollar amount,
fraud amount, prevented loss, or net savings.

## Next bounded action

Move to a source that can expose transaction lineage, or select another Tier 2
depth row. Do not spend another slice manipulating the unmatched aggregate
amounts unless a PLTSS debt-cohort or receivables source is found.
