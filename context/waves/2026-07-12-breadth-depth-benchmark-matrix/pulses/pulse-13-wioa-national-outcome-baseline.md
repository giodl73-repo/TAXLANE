# Pulse 13: WIOA National Outcome Baseline

## Result

Attached DOL ETA's PY2024 national administrative performance summary for
WIOA Adult, Dislocated Worker, Youth, and Wagner-Peyser programs. The record
preserves each indicator's numerator, denominator, cohort period, and program-
specific definition where available.

The underlying PDF reports 121,531 Youth participants served. DOL's results-at-
a-glance webpage reports 125,531; the artifact uses the PDF total and records
the conflict explicitly.

## Decision gate

Pass for a descriptive WIOA Title I and III participant-outcome baseline. Fail
for causal training effects, population employment outcomes, a full function-
500 measure, an account crosswalk to subfunction 504, cost per job, fraud, or
recoverable savings.
