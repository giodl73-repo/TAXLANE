# Pulse 40: Medicare Part D Sample-Design Evidence Ceiling

## Objective

Test whether the captured same-period CMS sources disclose enough of the
FY2024 Medicare Part D sample design to close that methodology field.

## Evidence

The CY2022 submission guide identifies individual PDE records as the sampled
unit. CMS assigns PDE IDs, routes selected records by sponsor contract through
HPMS, supplies ten identifying elements, and binds the reviewed record to the
June 29, 2023 reconciliation cutoff. The FY2024 Improper Payments Fact Sheet
describes a statistically valid stratified random sample of PDEs. The FY2024
findings report says the Part D measure complied with OMB sampling and
estimation-plan guidance and used a statistically valid methodology. It also
marks FY2024 as a baseline following FY2023 methodology changes and bars
comparison with reporting years before FY2023.

## Decision

Create a component-level evidence ceiling but do not close `sample design`.
The sources do not publish the actual national sample size, sampling-frame
definition or size, stratum definitions, allocation, within-stratum selection
procedure, inclusion probabilities, randomization implementation, replacement
or nonresponse rules, or the relationship between the PDE review and the
separately described beneficiary simulation. They also do not connect sample
weights to projection, variance, or confidence limits.

The guide's examples showing two or five PDEs are upload illustrations, not the
national sample size. A statistically valid designation is not a reproducible
design specification.

## Status and boundary

No full field closes. Medicare Part D remains three fields closed and five
open, with sample design still among the residual gaps. All methodology-scoring,
public-claim, fraud, waste, debt, recovery, and savings gates remain blocked.
The next source target is a same-period FY2024 sampling-method disclosure with
the missing frame, sample-size, allocation, probability, and selection details.
