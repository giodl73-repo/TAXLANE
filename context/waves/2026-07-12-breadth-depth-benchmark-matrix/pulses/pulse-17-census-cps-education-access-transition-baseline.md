# Pulse 17: Census CPS Education Access and Transition Baseline

## Result

Attached the October 2024 CPS School Enrollment Supplement's broad enrollment
denominators for the civilian noninstitutionalized population age 3 and older
and its transition snapshot for high school graduates age 15 to 24 reported as
graduating this year. Published counts remain in thousands, different table
universes remain separate, and derived transition rates expose their formulas.

## Decision gate

Pass for a descriptive national education-access and recent-graduate
transition baseline. Fail for attendance, quality, completion, unmet-demand,
or long-run outcome claims; WIOA, WIA, or other program attribution; a treated
versus untreated comparison; joining the survey populations to education or
training spending; cost per outcome; fraud; or recoverable savings.

## Next bounded action

Attach a current longitudinal or quasi-experimental education-to-employment
design with explicit program exposure, a defined counterfactual, and
cohort-compatible spending before attempting causal or cost-effectiveness
claims.
