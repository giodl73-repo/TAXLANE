# Pulse 70: Family Support Target Rubric Application

## Result

- Applies the Pulse 69 international comparator target rubric to the
  income-security-family lane.
- Uses the bounded 2022 SOCX family-spending panel and the age relative-income
  poverty panel as separate inputs.
- Records that the spending panel has six observed non-US peers, below
  TAXLANE's median-claim and favorable-quartile minimums.
- Keeps the result at display-only descriptive status even though the United
  States is lower than all observed non-US display peers on public family
  spending and higher than those same peers on child relative poverty.
- Blocks a family-spending target, favorable-quartile scenario, causal
  spending/outcome interpretation, efficiency rank, fraud, waste, or savings
  claim.

## Decision

```text
family spending input = display-only descriptive comparison
child poverty outcome = separate outcome context
six observed non-US spending peers < median-claim minimum
mixed outcome years = no robust target
spending gap + outcome gap != causal efficiency or recoverable savings
```
