# Pulse 67: Pension Replacement Panel, Model Entry 2024

## Result

- Captures OECD Pensions at a Glance 2025 gross and net replacement-rate rows
  for all 11 core peers; Singapore remains absent.
- Uses one exact scenario: male, entry age 22 in 2024, full career, average
  earnings, mandatory public and private schemes.
- Keeps gross and net denominators distinct and excludes voluntary schemes.
- Labels every value as a theoretical future entitlement rather than an
  observed current-retiree outcome.
- Refuses a spending-efficiency ratio against the unmatched 2022 SOCX panel.
- Keeps adequacy, poverty, ranking, target, efficiency, and savings gates
  blocked.
