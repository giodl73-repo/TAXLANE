# Pulse 20: Pell Bachelor-Recipient Outcome Baseline

## Result

Attached NCES Baccalaureate and Beyond descriptive outcomes for the 2015–16
bachelor-completer cohort four years after completion. Published Pell/non-Pell
rows cover employment and enrollment, federal student-loan debt and repayment,
worker hours and annualized earnings, and financial well-being, with standard
errors and denominator boundaries retained.

## Decision gate

Pass for a longitudinal descriptive baseline among bachelor's degree
completers grouped by lifetime Pell receipt through 2015–16. Fail for a Pell
effect on completion, persistence, employment, earnings, debt, repayment, or
financial well-being; transport to current FY2024 recipients or noncompleters;
joining worker, borrower, and repayment subuniverses; ignoring COVID-19 or
CARES administrative forbearance; a complete cost or outlay crosswalk; fraud;
or recoverable savings.

## Next bounded action

Find a current student-aid cohort with recipient-linked completion, debt,
repayment, employment, and earnings outcomes plus cohort-compatible full
incremental costs, or a credible causal design, before attempting a program
effect, cost-effectiveness ratio, or fiscal claim.
