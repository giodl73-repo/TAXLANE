# Pulse 38: Medicare Part D Sponsor-Documentation Dependency Evidence Ceiling

## Objective

Reconcile the official Part D scorecard and test whether the remaining
state-data dependency field can close without inventing a state role.

## Evidence

The captured Q4 2025 scorecard reports $3,053 million in FY2024 overpayments at
3.16%. This corrects the stale 1.02% and $2,403 million transcription. The
scorecard assigns the full rounded amount to failure to access data or
information needed and describes plan-sponsor drug, pricing, and documentation
issues. The annual workbook's exact amount is $3,052.65 million. CMS findings
split the 3.16% into 2.70% documentation and 0.46% drug/pricing components.

## Decision

Record a field-reframing evidence ceiling. `State-data dependency treatment` is
not supported for Part D; the evidence instead supports `sponsor-data and
documentation dependency treatment`. Do not close the full field until a
same-period method source explains how missing or corrected documentation flows
through review and estimation. Part D remains two closed and six open.

## Boundary and next action

Outside-agency-control taxonomy is not a legal nonrecoverability finding, and
insufficient documentation is not fraud, waste, debt, collectibility, recovery,
or savings. The next source target is the CY2022 Part D IPM submission guide and
FAQ, which can test the sponsor-documentation treatment without changing the
aggregate by assumption.
