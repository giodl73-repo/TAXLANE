# Pulse 62: Fiscal Country Panel, 2022

## Result

- Captures a pinned IMF October 2025 Fiscal Monitor vintage and explicitly
  filters its broad response to 2022.
- Captures OECD 2022 tax revenue for 11 countries and 66 tax-mix observations.
- Separates tax revenue from total revenue and gross debt from net debt.
- Covers overall and primary balances for all core peers; Singapore retains
  explicit tax, primary-balance, and net-debt gaps.
- Tests the direct IMF GFS interest route; it returns no observations, so no
  primary-minus-overall interest inference is published.
- Keeps every fiscal peer value descriptive rather than a policy target or
  savings score.
