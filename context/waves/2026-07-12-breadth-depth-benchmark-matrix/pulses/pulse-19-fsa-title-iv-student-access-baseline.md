# Pulse 19: FSA Title IV Student Access Baseline

## Result

Captured the Federal Student Aid FY 2024 Annual Report as a descriptive
Title IV access and disbursement baseline. Table 4's six program rows
reconcile exactly to the published grant subtotals and grand totals at the
displayed precision. The record also preserves the report's FAFSA, student,
institution, recipient, award, Pell average, and Pell maximum measures,
including every “more than” or approximate qualifier used in the selected
access measures. It retains two printed percentage-sign contradictions in
Table 4 and records the positive arithmetic direction separately.

## Decision gate

Pass for reporting fiscal-year Title IV disbursements and separately reported
award-year access counts. Fail for treating those periods as one student
cohort; deriving an exact average from rounded or bounded measures; treating
the Pell average or maximum as full cost; inferring an educational or labor
outcome, causal effect, OMB account allocation, improper payment, fraud,
recoverable amount, or savings estimate.

## Next bounded action

Find a recipient-level or program-level source that aligns award-year aid,
student characteristics, completion or labor outcomes, and a complete cost
measure before attempting a cost-per-outcome or fiscal benchmark.
