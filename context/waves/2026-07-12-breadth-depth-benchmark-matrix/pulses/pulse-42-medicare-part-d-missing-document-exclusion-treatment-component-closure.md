# Pulse 42: Medicare Part D Missing-Document Exclusion-Treatment Component Closure

## Objective

Determine whether CY2022 documentation defects remain in review or are excluded,
without treating that narrow result as the complete Part D exclusion rule set.

## Evidence

The CY2022 guide says timely submitted documentation undergoes Part D IPM review
even when incomplete or otherwise problematic. It requires a Missing
Documentation Form when specified evidence cannot be obtained and permits
corrected missing or invalid evidence before the April 19, 2024 final deadline.

The CY2022 FAQ states that the Missing Documentation Form is not substitute
evidence, so the PDE remains failed while the defect is unresolved; evidence
found before the deadline may still be submitted. The FY2024 findings report
2.70% for insufficient documentation, defined as invalid or missing evidence,
within the overpayment error categories.

## Decision

Close one narrow internal component: timely incomplete or problematic
documentation remains in review, unresolved missing evidence retains failed
status, and the cycle provides a predeadline cure window. Keep full `exclusion
rules` open.

The FY2020/CY2018 findings historically excluded 27 of 4,526 sampled PDEs when
requested evidence could not be obtained. That is comparison-only evidence, not
a CY2022 rule. The report’s separate COVID-era request suspension and deadline
extension do not prove COVID-19 caused those exclusions.

## Boundary and next action

The current sources do not disclose the complete exclusion taxonomy, current
counts and decision stages, no/late/invalid/incomplete submission distinctions,
post-deadline or appeal treatment, replacement and weights, estimator effects,
or the reason for any departure from FY2020.

Reviewed does not mean automatically erroneous or estimator-included unchanged;
failed status is curable before the deadline; and the aggregate documentation
category does not prove identical treatment for every defect. One component
closes, but no full field closes. Part D remains three closed and five open, and
every scoring, public-claim, fraud, waste, debt, recovery, and savings gate stays
blocked.
