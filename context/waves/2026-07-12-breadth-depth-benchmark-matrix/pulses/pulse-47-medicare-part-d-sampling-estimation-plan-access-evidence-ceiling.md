# Pulse 47: Medicare Part D Sampling and Estimation Plan Access Evidence Ceiling

## Objective

Record the strongest defensible next Part D evidence target without slicing
already-closed public facts into another narrow component.

## Evidence

The locally captured OMB M-21-19 requires statistically valid estimates to
produce point and confidence-interval estimates and directs agencies to upload
the Sampling and Estimation Methodology Plan and OMB checklist to the agency's
secure MAX page.

The existing validated Part D bridges show the public evidence ceiling across
sample design, payment universe, estimation method, and exclusion rules. They
are inventory inputs only, not new source custody or closure evidence.

## Decision

Record a zero-closure access evidence ceiling and exact acquisition target for
the CY2022/FY2024 Part D S&EMP, checklist, appendices, technical attachments,
version history, and the missing reproducible method detail.

Do not close any component or field. Secure MAX identifies a controlled plan
location; it does not prove exemption, withholding, nonexistence, public
unavailability, or Part D-specific content.

## Program Impact

This pulse adds zero component and zero full-field closures. Part D remains
three closed and five open, with three closure decisions and five residual
gaps. Recoverable-amount lineage remains a separate target. All ten claim and
scoring gates remain false.

## Outbound Boundary

No request was drafted or submitted, no external message was sent, and no owner
authorization or outbound state change is claimed.
