# Pulse 12: K–12 OECD Resource Comparison

## Result

Attached a definition-matched Education at a Glance 2025 resource comparison
using 2022 data. U.S. government expenditure per full-time-equivalent student
from primary through post-secondary non-tertiary education was $14,603 PPP-
adjusted, versus the OECD average of $12,438.

## Decision gate

Pass for a same-definition descriptive resource peer. Fail for causal linkage
to PISA, an automatic OECD spending target, a crosswalk to OMB function 500,
fraud, or recoverable savings.

## Next bounded action

Move to the worker, employment, and earnings side of function 500 or build a
research design capable of relating matched resources to outcomes over time.
