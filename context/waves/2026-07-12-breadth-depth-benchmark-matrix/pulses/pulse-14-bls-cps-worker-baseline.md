# Pulse 14: BLS CPS Worker Baseline

## Result

Attached BLS's 2024 education, earnings, unemployment, population, and
employment measures for people age 25 and older. The record preserves the
distinct earnings, labor-force, and civilian-noninstitutional-population
universes and the parent-child structure of CPS Table 7 attainment columns.

## Decision gate

Pass for a descriptive population worker baseline alongside WIOA participant
outcomes. Fail for a causal education or training effect, a WIOA
counterfactual, a subfunction-504 account crosswalk, cost per worker or
outcome, fraud, or recoverable savings.

## Next bounded action

Attach a longitudinal or quasi-experimental employment and earnings design,
or build a program-account bridge before attempting cost-effectiveness.
