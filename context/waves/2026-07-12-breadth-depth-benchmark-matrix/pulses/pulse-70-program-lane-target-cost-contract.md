# Pulse 70 — Program-Lane Target-Cost Contract

## Decision

Define the common bridge required to turn comparison evidence into target costs
and balanced rates without treating peer spending gaps as savings.

## Added

- A machine-readable contract covering all 15 comparison lanes.
- Exact target-cost methods, policy levers, outcome floors, federal translation,
  candidate financing bases, and integrated-solver treatment for every lane.
- Separate receipt-share and statutory/effective-rate equations.
- A ten-year balance identity with separate trust funds, endogenous net interest,
  explicit reserves, offsets, deficit gap, and rounding treatment.
- Required current-law, central-reform, and stress scenarios.

## Boundary

Every numeric readiness flag remains false. The contract does not select a
country target, quantify a new lane cost, enact a receipt dedication, score
savings, or publish a statutory rate. A country spending gap alone cannot unlock
any of those claims.

## Next Gate

Populate matched-year baseline quantities and low/central/high—or
current-law/central/stress—numeric scenarios, beginning with health while the
aggregate receipt-base and debt-interest solver is built in parallel.
