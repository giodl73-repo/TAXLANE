# Pulse 36: VA PLTSS Documentation-Recoverability Policy Boundary

## Objective

Run the pending VA policy search and determine whether current classification
and recovery rules close the PLTSS documentation-defect-versus-recoverable-
overpayment field.

## Evidence

Captured checksum-verified official HTML for VA Financial Policy Volume VII,
Chapter 03 and Volume III, Chapter 30. Chapter 03 distinguishes proper,
unknown, monetary-loss overpayment, underpayment, technically improper, and
returned-funds recovery states. Chapter 30 requires actual funds certified
recovered, credited, or realized for recovery-audit compensation and accounting.

Cross-checked those rules against the FY2025 AFR's PLTSS causes, payment
categories, post-payment reviews, and bills-of-collection language.

## Decision

Record a narrow agency-policy classification and certified-recovery boundary.
Do not close the full documentation-defect-versus-recoverable-overpayment field.
The sources do not map PLTSS defects through payment category, established bill,
dispute, write-off, collectibility, collection, and returned funds. PLTSS stays
at two closed and six open fields.

## Claim firewall and next action

A documentation defect is not automatically an overpayment. An estimated or
identified overpayment is not automatically a bill, collectible debt, or
recovery. A bill is not certified collected cash. Current policy postdates the
tested payments and cannot assign historical PLTSS dispositions by inference.

Keep scoring and public debt, collectibility, recovery, fraud, waste, and
savings claims blocked. The next useful evidence is PLTSS-specific cohort or
aggregate disposition data mapping defect cause through category, debt action,
dispute outcome, write-off, and certified collection.
