## Evidence locator

The RMA FOIA Fees page describes requester fee categories. An all-other
requester pays search and duplication costs after the first two hours of search
time and first 100 pages. USDA does not collect fees totaling less than $25,
while search fees may apply even when no responsive documents are located. A
fee-waiver request must document a public-interest basis and show that the
disclosure is not primarily in the requester's commercial interest.

## Boundary

The page provides general fee and waiver rules. It does not establish the
owner's requester category or entitlement to a fee waiver, and no fee
commitment has been executed.
