## Evidence locator

Section 142E.02, subdivision 6(b), defines child care assistance program
payment data for a specified period as data showing that a payment was made
and the amount paid to a child care center. It classifies data that may
identify a specific assistance recipient or benefit paid on that recipient's
behalf as private, while making payment-related data public for a licensed or
license-exempt center that receives more than $100,000 in a year or less or
meets a listed program-integrity condition.

## Boundary

The provision supports a privacy-aware request for provider payment facts and
amounts. It does not itself prove that DCYF maintains every requested
transaction, adjustment, cutoff, or calculation field; require a new ledger
to be created; or make recipient-identifying data public. It is not evidence
that a payment was improper, fraudulent, owed, recovered, prevented, or saved.
