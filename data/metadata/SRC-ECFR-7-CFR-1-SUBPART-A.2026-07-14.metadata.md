## Evidence locator

7 CFR §§ 1.3(a)(2), 1.3(b)(1), and 1.3(c) address electronic submission,
requester contact information and fee commitment, and reasonable description
of requested records. Sections 1.5(d) and 1.5(f) address preferred production
format and release of reasonably segregable nonexempt portions. Section 1.12
sets out fee provisions.

## Boundary

These provisions govern request construction, processing, format, segregation,
and fees; they do not guarantee responsive records or disclosure. The eCFR is a
continuously updated, unofficial editorial compilation rather than the official
legal edition of the Code of Federal Regulations.
