## Evidence locator

Section 13.03 presumes government data public unless another authority
classifies it otherwise. Subdivision 3 addresses inspection and copies,
electronic copies of public data reasonably reproducible from the maintained
computer format, allowable costs, and the requirement to cite the specific
legal authority when access is denied.

## Boundary

The section supplies general access rules, not a field-by-field classification
of child care records. It does not require an agency to create a new record or
different electronic format, establish that responsive records exist, or
override private-data classifications applicable to identifiable recipients.
