## Evidence locator

7 U.S.C. § 1502(c)(1) protects information furnished by a producer under the
Federal Crop Insurance Act. Section 1502(c)(2)(A) authorizes disclosure of
statistical and aggregate information in a form that does not identify the
producer.

## Boundary

The statute creates a disclosure boundary: protected producer information is
not interchangeable with authorized non-identifying statistical or aggregate
disclosure. It does not itself show whether a particular requested record can
be released or whether aggregate material can preserve case-level lineage.
