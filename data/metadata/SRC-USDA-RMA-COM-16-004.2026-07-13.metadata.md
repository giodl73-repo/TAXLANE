## Evidence locator

PDF file pages 1-2 state that an AIP must request a final administrative
determination within 45 days after receipt of a Final Finding. Receipt is the
date RMA uploads the finding to CARS. The request must identify disputed
provisions, explain the asserted error, and include rebuttal evidence.

## Boundary

An uploaded Final Finding is appealable. The memorandum does not establish that
the finding is affirmed, becomes debt, is collectible, or is collected.
