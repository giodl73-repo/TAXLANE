PDF file pages 1-2 describe an AIP request for a final administrative
determination when it disputes a Regional Compliance Office Final Finding. The
preferred submission path is the case-policy document library in CARS, where
RMA also uploads an acknowledgement.

affirmed or reversed dollars, established debt, collection, or sample-specific
FY2024 dispositions.
