## Evidence locator

PDF file pages 1-3 describe the Minnesota Government Data Practices Act
presumption, written-request contents, DCYF routing, and the three general
response paths: no responsive data, responsive data classified as not public
with the governing law identified, or collection, review, and disclosure of
responsive public data. The guide says public-data requests are fulfilled
within a reasonable amount of time.

## Boundary

The guide establishes DCYF's general public-data workflow. It does not promise
that the requested provider-level records exist, classify every requested
field as public, or establish a production timetable for a particular request.
No request was submitted and no response, fee, production, or denial exists.
