# Spend Category Accountability Question Handoff

## Purpose

These questions route the largest FY2025 spend categories to deeper evidence.
They are not findings.

## Common questions for every large category

- What legal obligation or public purpose does the category serve?
- Which agency, account, program activity, or benefit program owns the largest
  pieces?
- Is the spending mandatory, discretionary, interest, grant, procurement,
  transfer, direct benefit, provider payment, payroll, or administration?
- What metric would show cost, coverage, timeliness, quality, solvency, or
  readiness?
- Which source can answer the metric question?

## Category-specific handoff

| Rank | Category | First safe question | Next source family |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Social Security | What part of current outlays is benefit obligation, and how does solvency change under current law? | SSA trustees; OMB mandatory programs |
| 2 | Gross Treasury interest | How much of the cost comes from debt stock versus rates? | Treasury debt data; CBO long-term outlook |
| 3 | Medicare | Which cost growth comes from enrollment, prices, utilization, and provider payment rules? | Medicare trustees; CMS; CBO; health price literature |
| 4 | Health care services | Which programs dominate the subfunction and what outcomes/cost drivers are measurable? | HHS/CMS budget justifications; GAO; CBO |
| 5 | DOD-Military | How do personnel, operations, procurement, R&D, and readiness account for the outlays? | DOD budget justification; CBO; GAO |
| 6 | Other income security | Which programs are inside the residual category, and which are cyclical? | OMB mandatory programs; agency budget justifications |
| 7 | Veterans income security | How much is compensation, pensions, education, or other benefit obligation? | VA budget justification; GAO/IG |
| 8 | Federal employee retirement/disability | What share reflects legacy benefit formulas and workforce history? | OPM; OMB mandatory programs |
| 9 | Food and nutrition assistance | What are benefit adequacy, eligibility, participation, and payment-error metrics? | USDA; CBO; GAO |
| 10 | Veterans medical care | What do access, wait time, quality, and cost per enrollee show? | VA/VHA; GAO/IG |
| 11 | Ground transportation | Which programs and trust-fund flows dominate the outlays? | DOT; Highway Trust Fund; USAspending for awards |
| 12 | Housing assistance | What do rent burden, waitlist, targeting, and payment-integrity metrics show? | HUD; GAO; CBO |
| 13 | Elementary/secondary/vocational education | How much is federal grant support versus state/local education finance? | Education Department; NCES; grant data |
| 14 | Disaster relief and insurance | Which disasters, supplemental appropriations, or insurance losses explain the year? | FEMA; SBA; HUD disaster recovery; GAO |
| 15 | Health research and training | Which research portfolios, grants, and training programs dominate the category? | NIH/HHS budget justifications; grant portfolio data |

## Claim guard

Do not convert these questions into public claims until the deeper source is
recorded, the source grain matches the claim, and the accountability brief's
guardrails are satisfied.
