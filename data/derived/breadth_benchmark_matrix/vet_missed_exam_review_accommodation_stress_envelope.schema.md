# VET Missed-Exam Review and Accommodation Stress Schema

This record executes a normalized missed-examination cohort through
`taxlane-vet-claims`.

The funnel preserves notice reach, accommodation offer, reschedule acceptance,
exam completion, existing-evidence review, sufficient evidence, unresolved
claims, and follow-up separately. Workload converts accommodation and review
minutes into FTE and cost; completed examinations retain claimant time burden.

Procedurally reviewable means only that an examination was completed or the
existing record was sufficient for adjudication. It does not mean granted,
corrected, timely, equitable, or appeal avoided. Inputs have no population
weight and supply no admissible saving.
