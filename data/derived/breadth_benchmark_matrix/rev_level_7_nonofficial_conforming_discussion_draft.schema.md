# REV Level 7 nonofficial conforming discussion draft schema

The record must cite the current official structure of 26 U.S.C. §1, identify
the four individual filing-status tables, state all seven rate substitutions,
preserve bracket thresholds and indexing, exclude the estate-and-trust table,
state the effective date and transition directions, and retain explicit
questions for Legislative Counsel.

`nonofficial_discussion_draft_complete` may be true when those fields and the
companion draft text are complete. `legislative_counsel_conforming_text_complete`,
`authorized_submission_completed`, `statutory_schedule_ready`, official-score
fields, and formal certification fields must remain false or null until the
corresponding external event occurs.
