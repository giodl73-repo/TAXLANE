# EDU-A Education/Workforce Perimeter and Source Inventory Schema

This record closes `EDU-A-01` by freezing federal budget function 500 and its
six subfunctions as the controlling federal perimeter. It preserves grants,
administration, refundable-credit outlays, credit-subsidy costs, negative
subsidies, reestimates, liquidating accounts, and offsetting receipts with their
published signs.

State/local school finance, private resources, loan cash collections, and
education or workforce populations remain non-additive context. Inventory
completion permits `EDU-A-02`; it does not establish an annual baseline,
federal/state/local translation, causal outcome, savings, rate, or B-stage
start.
