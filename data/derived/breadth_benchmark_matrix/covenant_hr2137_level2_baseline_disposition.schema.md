# COVENANT H.R. 2137 Level 2 baseline disposition schema

## Product question

Does COVENANT's current claims, appeals, and rural-context snapshot complete
H.R. 2137 Level 2 or change Taxlane's fiscal result?

## Contract

`producer_input` preserves the exact producer commit, replay command, pack
hash, candidate identity, fourteen-section contract, readiness, and denied
authorities. `current_system_floor_snapshot` keeps inventory, backlog,
accuracy, docket waits, and rural context separate.

## Invariants

- `69,481 / 600,878` truncates to 1,156 basis points.
- `870 - 212 = 658` days; the gap does not waive hearing rights.
- Issue accuracy is not whole-claim accuracy.
- VHA rural enrollment is not a matched VBA claims cohort.
- Current performance is not an effect of an unenacted candidate.
- No savings, track reopening, target change, or rate recomputation occurs.
