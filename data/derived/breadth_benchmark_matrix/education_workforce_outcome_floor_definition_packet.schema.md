# Education/workforce outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for
`education_workforce_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the education/workforce lane, target-cost
  contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, transportation packet precedent,
  education depth card, and lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving federal/state/local translation,
  negative-higher-education accounting boundaries, null missing values, false
  blocked gates, and no international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six education/workforce-specific floor
  definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

Federal/state/local translations, program-to-actual-outlay allocations, cohort
timing models, floor thresholds, pass/fail findings, target costs, federal
effects, savings, solver inputs, department-cut instructions, technology-savings
claims, and balanced-budget claims remain blocked.
