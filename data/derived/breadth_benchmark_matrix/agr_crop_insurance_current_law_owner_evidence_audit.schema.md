# AGR crop-insurance current-law and owner-evidence audit schema

This record audits the selected AGR crop-insurance option against post-vintage
law, the current CBO baseline, USDA operating evidence, distribution and
participation evidence, insurer economics, disaster substitution, resilience,
food security, and current-law score availability.

The source set must contain nine official CBO, GovInfo, USDA, RMA, ERS, and GAO
records. Eight gates must distinguish current evidence, bounded context,
historical comparisons needing current response evidence, joint causal work,
and scorekeeper work. Indemnity shares cannot stand in for the incidence of a
premium-subsidy reduction, aggregate underwriting gains cannot become
recoverable savings, and a GAO matter for congressional consideration cannot
be treated as enacted law.

The bundled option may be split into narrower successor analysis components.
Such a split selects evidence work only: all admitted effects remain zero, no
solver runs, and no rate changes.
