# REV Level-1 individual-income rate-candidate start schema

This progress record identifies one real current-law revenue instrument using
official OMB receipt-category and IRS tax-return context. It must preserve the
fiscal-year versus tax-year mismatch and may not treat either context value as
a matched legal or economic base. It links the definition-only REV-L1-02
perimeter and the REV-L1-03 bounded timing reblock.

Behavior, compliance, administration, incidence, distribution, and
candidate-selection work packages remain explicitly owned. Candidate
selection, assigned rates, solver results, rate cards, tax proposals, and
balanced-budget claims remain null or false.
