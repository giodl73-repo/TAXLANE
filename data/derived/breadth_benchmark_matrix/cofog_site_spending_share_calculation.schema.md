# COFOG site spending-share calculation

This record makes the local website's six-country composition table
reproducible from the captured OECD COFOG country panel.

For each country, the denominator is the sum of non-null first-level COFOG
function amounts in current national-currency millions. Each displayed share is
the function amount divided by that denominator and rounded to one decimal.

Germany, France, Japan, Sweden, and the Netherlands contain all ten first-level
functions. The United States lacks GF05 in the captured OECD response, so its
column is normalized over nine captured functions and is not fully comparable.
Missing must never be rendered or interpreted as zero.

The result describes spending composition. It is not an income-tax allocation,
efficiency or fairness ranking, U.S. savings estimate, rate-model input,
official score, or public-release authorization.
