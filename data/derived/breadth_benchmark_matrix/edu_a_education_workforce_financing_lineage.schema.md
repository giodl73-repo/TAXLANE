# EDU-A Education/Workforce Financing Lineage Schema

This record closes `EDU-A-03` by separating federal appropriations, grants,
nongrants, mandatory and discretionary outlays, credit subsidies, negative
subsidies, reestimates, collections, offsets, and recipient-side state/local
finance.

Known K-12, higher-education, and training/employment bridges retain their
periods, signs, and reconciliation boundaries. State/local resources are
non-additive, and credit subsidy is not cash principal. Missing account
allocations remain null. Completion permits `EDU-A-04` only.
