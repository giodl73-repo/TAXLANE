# TRN-B Accounting Schedules Schema

TRN-B-05 must distinguish a computational adapter zero from a substantive
evidence-backed zero. Unsupported general-fund transfer, credited-offset, and
reserve schedules remain null. OMB combined balance adjustments and derived
rounding lines may be populated at displayed source precision.

Rounding may reconcile displayed components to displayed closing balances but
must never be relabeled as a transfer, offset, savings estimate, or policy effect.
