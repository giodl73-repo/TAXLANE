# ENVOY Nepal result-chain audit disposition schema

## Contract

`producer` preserves the exact ENVOY replay. `indicator_census` records the
complete public corpus by level and readiness. `process_trajectory` preserves
the only baseline-target-actual join without promoting design to construction
or outcome. `portfolio_result` states the fiscal and rate consequence.

## Invariants

- Readiness counts must preserve all 186 indicators and their four levels.
- Process trajectory is not completed work, service quality, or outcome.
- No candidate effect exists when zero outcome rows join baseline, target, and
  observation and zero compact-specific rows contain an actual.
- No INT reopening or rate recomputation occurs without an admissible fiscal
  effect and all applicable floors.
