# NET OMB PBD gross-to-net interest bridge schema

The bridge must retain exactly five OMB BEA net-interest subfunctions: gross
Treasury interest (901), on-budget trust-fund interest receipts (902), off-
budget trust-fund interest receipts (903), other interest (908), and other
investment income (909). Signed components must sum exactly to OMB net interest
for FY2025–FY2031.

OMB and CBO values remain separate. The bridge establishes source-backed
interest-receipt treatment semantics, but it cannot populate the CBO baseline
formula input until matching-vintage gross and receipt values reconcile.
