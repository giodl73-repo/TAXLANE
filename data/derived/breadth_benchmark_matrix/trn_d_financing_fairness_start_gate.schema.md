# TRN-D Financing and Fairness Start Gate Schema

TRN-D starts only after TRN-C closes. Its six work packages must resolve the
legal financing perimeter, any matched base and yield, administration and
compliance, incidence and distribution, interactions, and role review.

TRN-D-01 is complete with a discretionary FAA appropriation perimeter and null
fund/account assignments. TRN-D-02 is not required because no receipt-financed
instrument is selected. TRN-D-03 through TRN-D-06 are complete and TRN-D is
closed as a bounded bridge. TRN-E-01 discovery is active. The gate still
preserves null trust-fund/general-fund assignment, quantitative incidence,
receipt base, target cost, rate, solver result, and savings outputs.
