# Stage-C catch-up bundle schema

The bundle advances health, education/workforce, and Social Security/OASDI to
completed bounded C stages while transportation remains beyond C at TRN-E.

Every stage record must list every package through role review, assign a CORE-J
completion class, retain unresolved values as owned handoffs, and keep output
claims blocked. A C-stage candidate screen may close with no reform admitted;
screen completion must never be represented as reform, numeric, or output
completion.

CORE-K is the shared contract learned from the bundle. It requires explicit
calendar/fiscal/tax-year bridges, prohibits unsupported horizon extension,
keeps a published composite non-additive with its components, and requires
transfer lineage to remain explicit.
