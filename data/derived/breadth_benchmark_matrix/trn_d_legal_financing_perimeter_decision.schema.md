# TRN-D Legal Financing Perimeter Decision Schema

This record closes `TRN-D-01` when official bill and cost-estimate evidence can
identify the legal spending perimeter without inventing a financing source.

The H.R. 2247 evidence admits a conditional FAA discretionary-appropriation
perimeter under budget function 400. It does not assign the Airport and Airway
Trust Fund, the general fund, a fee, an assigned receipt, or a named
appropriation account. Those fields therefore remain null.

`TRN-D-02` is not required unless a fee, tax, or assigned-receipt instrument is
selected. `TRN-D-03` may start, but no rate, incidence result, TRN-D closure, or
TRN-E start follows from this decision.
