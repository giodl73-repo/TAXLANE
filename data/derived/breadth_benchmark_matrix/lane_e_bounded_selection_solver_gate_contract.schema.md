# Lane E bounded selection and solver gate contract

This contract generalizes the reviewed TRN-E precedent across every Taxlane
track. E begins only after D. It must classify integrated inputs, resolve
candidate and financing selection, run the solver only when a candidate and all
required inputs exist, reconcile any actual run, and complete role review.

When selection resolves to no admissible candidate, packages 3 and 4 are
`not_required`; that permits bounded structural E closure but never numeric
completion, output admission, or an F-stage start. PAY and REV remain
non-additive overlays and NET remains endogenous.
