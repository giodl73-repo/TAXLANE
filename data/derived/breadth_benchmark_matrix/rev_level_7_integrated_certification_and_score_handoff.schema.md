# REV Level 7 integrated certification and score-handoff schema

Level 7 separates internal evidence certification from official fiscal
certification. Internal checks may pass only when the all-15 rerun is complete,
dependencies and claim profiles are enforced, and the model is reproducible.
Official gates identify the exact legislative, CBO, JCT, compliance,
distribution, macro, debt, and balance evidence still required. External
outputs remain null until actually received.

The Level 7 closure must link the scorer-ready substantive policy specification
and the ten-year score-request workbook. Those artifacts may close the internal
specification gate but may not claim Legislative Counsel text, submission, or
an official response.

A nonofficial discussion draft may be linked and marked complete only when its
own record preserves the Legislative Counsel, introduced-bill, enactment,
submission, score, and certification boundaries.
