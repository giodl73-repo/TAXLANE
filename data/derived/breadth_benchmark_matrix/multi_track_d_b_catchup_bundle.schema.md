# Multi-track D/B catch-up bundle schema

This bundle closes transportation at bounded E; health, education/workforce,
and OASDI at bounded D; and income-security/family, veterans, and agriculture at
bounded B.

`complete` means the named structural package and its handoffs were reviewed.
`not_required` means a downstream numerical package could not run because no
candidate or financing instrument passed its prerequisite gate. Neither status
may be used to claim numerical completion or output readiness.

CORE-L governs subjects that appear in multiple lanes. Every overlap needs one
owner, at least two named participants, and one treatment: exclusive owner,
shared non-additive context, or a sourced allocation whose shares cover every
participant and sum to 10,000 basis points.
