# Adaptive needs and balance cycle schema

This schema describes `adaptive_needs_and_balance_cycle.v1.draft.json`.

The record separates three questions that must not be collapsed:

1. Is a lane structurally out of balance relative to obligations, outcomes,
   history, and definition-matched peers?
2. Where is measurable pain most severe and remediable now?
3. Should a response be permanent base funding, temporary repair funding, a
   pilot, or a contingent reserve?

Every cycle must measure all lanes, protect applicable floors, rank specific
interventions rather than departments, allocate with an explicit funding type,
monitor dated outcomes, sunset or continue, and rebalance. Peer position is a
diagnostic field only. It never creates an automatic cut or increase.

`same_perimeter_peer_bridge.profile_post_policy_percent` must remain null until
every profile dollar has a same-year general-government and COFOG owner,
federal/state/local and private cost shifts are reconciled, and the post-policy
denominator is recalculated. A federal FY2026 envelope must not be applied
directly to the 2022 general-government table.

PAY is non-additive, REV is a financing constraint, NET is endogenous, and OAS
requires separate solvency and adequacy treatment. The remaining eleven tracks
may compete for an adaptive reserve; none is entitled to an increase.
