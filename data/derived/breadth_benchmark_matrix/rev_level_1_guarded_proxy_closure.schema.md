# REV Level-1 guarded proxy closure schema

This record closes the bounded REV Level-1 planning objective by selecting one
reproducible rate-sensitivity case. It must preserve the assumed spending
correction, model vintage, proxy method, uncertainty, distribution boundary,
and the distinction between a planning schedule and an assigned statutory rate.

Closure does not constitute a JCT or CBO score, a solver result, a tax proposal,
a CORE-N rate card, or a balanced-budget claim. Formal REV-F remains blocked
until current-law nonlinear tax-unit scoring and quantitative distribution are
available.
