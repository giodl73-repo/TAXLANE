# JUS District Judgeship Capacity Current-Law Audit Schema

This record reconciles H.R. 1702's legislative and cost-estimate perimeter with
the current federal district-court capacity and caseflow baseline.

Proposal cost must remain separate from current-law spending. Authorized
judgeships, vacancies, nominations, weighted filings, filings, terminations,
pending cases, and median times are operating context rather than a causal
candidate effect. Judicial Conference recommendations are need assessments,
not enactment, staffed delivery, or proof of better justice outcomes.

No proposal cost, vacancy count, recommendation, workload level, or aggregate
caseflow change may be transformed into savings or a rate effect.
