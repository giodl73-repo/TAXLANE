# REV Level-1 post-2025 rate-rescore proxy schema

This record must expose the partial-year annualization, receipt-growth scaling,
and exact arithmetic used to create a post-2025 planning proxy. It may publish
numeric all-bracket and top-four schedules, but must keep formal-score,
distribution, assigned-rate, and public-rate-card outputs null.

The proxy is not a JCT or CBO score and may not assume linear revenue response
outside the displayed planning sensitivity.
