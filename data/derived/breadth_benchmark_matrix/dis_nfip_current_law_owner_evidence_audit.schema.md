# DIS NFIP Current-Law Owner-Evidence Audit Schema

This artifact reconciles the December 2018 CBO option with the current NFIP
authorization, Risk Rating 2.0, affordability glidepath, debt, coverage, and
mitigation evidence.

Required sections are `source_rows`, `legacy_cbo_option`,
`current_program_perimeter`, `affordability_and_solvency`,
`repetitive_loss_and_mitigation`, `component_dispositions`,
`successor_contract`, `portfolio_handoff`, and `claim_boundaries`.

Cumulative borrowing is not outstanding debt. A premium shortfall is not an
appropriation or saving. Historical claim concentration is not a prospective
avoidable-loss share. The stale CBO score cannot be rolled forward through Risk
Rating 2.0.

Only a component may be selected for further evidence work. No component,
saving, solver input, or rate change is admitted by this record.
