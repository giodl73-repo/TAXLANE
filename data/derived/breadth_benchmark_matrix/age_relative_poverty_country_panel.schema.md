# Age Relative Poverty Country Panel

The panel uses the OECD Income Distribution Database poverty rate: the share of
an age group living below 50 percent of its country's median equivalised
disposable household income.

Older-person observations use each country's latest actual year from 2020 to
2024 because there is no common year for all 11 OECD peers. Child observations
use 2021 for ten peers and 2020 for Australia. Every reference year and the
Netherlands provisional status remain explicit.

This is a relative within-country income measure, not an absolute material-
deprivation threshold. It excludes wealth and does not establish policy
causality, spending efficiency, or savings.
