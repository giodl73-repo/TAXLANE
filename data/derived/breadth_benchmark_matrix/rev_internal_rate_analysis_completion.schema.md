# REV internal-rate analysis completion schema

The completion record must hash both executable runners and all three generated
outputs. It must preserve the frozen target, spending, PAY, NET, timing,
behavior, administration, macro-stress, and debt assumptions.

Exactly three recommendation tiers are required: preferred central,
behavior-robust contingency, and severe internal stress ceiling. The preferred
schedule is the smallest one-decimal candidate covering the central case after
administration. The contingency is the smallest covering all behavior cases
without macro stress. The ceiling is the smallest covering all combined stress
cases and must not be mislabeled as the baseline recommendation.

All fifteen tracks must be reviewed once. REV and NET may carry changed
analysis; PAY remains non-additive and every spending track retains zero
admission unless its own evidence changes.
