needed for later accounting work. It must preserve OMB Function 400 outlays,
Highway and Airport and Airway trust funds, Treasury corroborating anchors, CBO
balance-only extension context, roadway-safety scope, and every source boundary.

FY2032-FY2035 Function 400 or named-fund income/outgo values have been invented.
Those values remain null, the OMB/CBO overlap remains non-stitchable, and solver,
rate, savings, and balanced-budget outputs remain blocked.
