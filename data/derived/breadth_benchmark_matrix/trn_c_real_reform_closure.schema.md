# TRN-C Real Reform Closure Schema

TRN-C closes only when all five work packages are complete, exactly one
conditional policy scenario is admitted, applicable policy and stress floors
pass, reusable findings are extracted to CORE-I, and role review approves the
boundary.

The closure may admit a cost-only reform. Such a closure must keep enactment,
financing-source, lower-target-cost, savings, rate, public-card, and
balanced-budget outputs blocked while allowing TRN-D to begin.
