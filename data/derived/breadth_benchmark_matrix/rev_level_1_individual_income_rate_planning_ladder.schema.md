# REV Level-1 individual-income rate planning ladder schema

This record may publish numeric planning sensitivities while keeping every
assigned-rate and public-rate-card field null. Each row must identify the
fiscal balance target, annual spending reduction, remaining revenue need, and
both the static TY2023-base and December 2024 CBO/JCT option calculations.

The record must disclose that neither calculation is a current-law score: one
mixes tax-year and fiscal-year periods, and the other predates Public Law
119-21. Percentage-point uplifts are not flat total tax rates.
