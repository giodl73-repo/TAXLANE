# TRN-D Financing and Fairness Closure Schema

This record closes all six TRN-D packages, treating TRN-D-02 as not required
because no receipt instrument is selected. Closure means the supported
appropriation, burden, qualitative-incidence, and interaction boundaries are
reconciled and role-reviewed. It does not populate fund allocation,
quantitative incidence, macro effects, a rate, solver result, or savings.
Completed TRN-D permits guarded `TRN-E-01` discovery.
