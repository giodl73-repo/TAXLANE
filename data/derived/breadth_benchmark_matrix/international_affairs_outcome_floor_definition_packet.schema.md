# International-affairs outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for
`international_affairs_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the international-affairs lane, target-cost
  contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, agriculture packet precedent,
  international-affairs depth card, international financial programs account
  bridge, and lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving separate diplomacy, ODA/development,
  humanitarian, security, exchange, and financial-instrument components, null
  missing values, false blocked gates, and no international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six international-affairs-specific floor
  definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

Component policy paths, commitment-to-outlay bridges, recipient/instrument/purpose
decomposition, matched peer scope, floor thresholds, pass/fail findings, target
costs, federal effects, savings, solver inputs, department-cut instructions,
technology-savings claims, and balanced-budget claims remain blocked.
