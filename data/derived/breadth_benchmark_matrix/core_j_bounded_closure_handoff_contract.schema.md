# CORE-J Bounded Closure, Evidence-State, and Lane-Handoff Contract Schema

CORE-J is derived from TRN-E-01, HLT-B-01, and EDU-B-01. It standardizes four
completion classes, six evidence states, fourteen cross-perimeter account roles,
signed/non-additive role rules, owned handoff obligations, and the distinction
between successor discovery and output admission.

The Rust interfaces must reject unreviewed bounded closure, unresolved numeric
completion, output admission from a merely bounded closure, unsigned credit or
offset roles, and additive treatment of state/local, private, or explicitly
non-additive context.
