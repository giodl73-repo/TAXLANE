# EDU Pell Add-On Award and Substitution Stress Schema

This record executes normalized award cases under the enacted 2026–27 rule and
a mechanical add-on-elimination design. It also allocates a grant reduction
across loans, institutional aid, household work or transfers, and unmet need.

Every award row must replay through `taxlane-edu-pell`. The reduction identity
is current award minus design award. Every substitution row must preserve the
same reduction across its four destinations.

The design minimum and SAI threshold are assumptions, not enacted policy. Case
rows have no population weights, intensity rows are linear exposure rather than
payment schedules, and substitution shares are stresses rather than forecasts.
No row supplies a current federal score or an admissible saving.
