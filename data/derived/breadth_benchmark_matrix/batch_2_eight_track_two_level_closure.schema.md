# Batch 2 eight-track two-level closure schema

The packet must contain VET, EDU, ISF, AGR, DIS, JUS, SEE, and INT exactly
once. Each Level A result records ready and blocked gates plus a source-grounded
finding. Each Level B result reruns admission and keeps a candidate out unless
the current-law annual path, implementation boundary, interactions, and every
material outcome and distribution floor are closed.

VET must keep direct and discretionary effects separate and must not treat a
late direct-spending reduction in an unenacted bill as FY2026 package savings.
All unavailable annual paths, integrated savings, lower rates, and formal
balance results remain null.
