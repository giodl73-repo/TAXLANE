# ENVOY Nepal Compact candidate intake disposition schema

## Product question

Does ENVOY's Nepal Compact delivery envelope establish an admissible INT
savings candidate or change Taxlane's rate result?

## Contract

`producer_input` preserves the exact commit, replay command, hash, fourteen
sections, program status, readiness, and denied authorities.
`official_financial_and_delivery_position` separates U.S. compact funding,
Nepal cofunding, expenditure, unexpended obligation, contracts, mobilization,
and outcomes.

## Disposition

The candidate is a new active-program delivery identity, not the existing
uniform function-150 reduction. It adds program-and-region specificity but
does not close that reduction's diplomacy, health, humanitarian, security,
geographic, or national-security gates.

## Invariants

- The exact pack replays with all fourteen sections.
- `$550M + $197M = $747M` public commitment.
- `$550M - $115.8M = $434.2M` unexpended U.S. obligation.
- Cofunding and unexpended obligations are not U.S. savings.
- Contracts and mobilization are not completed outputs or durable outcomes.
- INT does not reopen and no rate is recomputed.
