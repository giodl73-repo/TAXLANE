# Fifteen-lane stage-E portfolio closure schema

This is the controlling proof that all fifteen canonical Taxlane tracks are at
or beyond E. It requires exactly fifteen unique lane IDs, prefixes, E evidence
paths, reviewed selection decisions, and null-output guards. Every lane must
have a completed D predecessor and a five-package E reconciliation.

Bounded E may close with no solver run only when selection is complete and
selects no candidate. That outcome cannot open F or any numeric/public output.
PAY/REV non-additivity and NET endogeneity remain mandatory.
