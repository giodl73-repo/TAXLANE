# AGR insurer-compensation public-evidence ceiling schema

This record tests whether the narrower insurer-compensation component selected
by the AGR audit has a current-law administrative savings path. It must use
current statute, the current SRA, current owner operating evidence, an official
market-return analysis, and the vintage CBO option.

Seven unique official sources and eight gates are required. The record must
distinguish current-law and current aggregate evidence from historical market
comparators, observed baselines without policy response, owner market-data
needs, and scorekeeper work. Provider count cannot prove competition; a service
baseline cannot prove a compensation-change effect; aggregate compensation and
historical return gaps cannot become recoverable savings.

If current law closes the administrative savings path, the record may permit a
bounded legislative design. It may not infer enactment, admit a fiscal effect,
run a solver, initiate an official request, authorize a public release, or
change a rate.
