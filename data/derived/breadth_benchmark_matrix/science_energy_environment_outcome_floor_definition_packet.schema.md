# Science/energy/environment outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for
`science_energy_environment_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the science/energy/environment lane,
  target-cost contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, justice packet precedent,
  science/energy/environment depth card, and lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving separate science, energy, and
  environment components, no composite target, null missing values, false
  blocked gates, and no international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six science/energy/environment-specific
  floor definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

Separate component scenarios, state/local scope, tax-expenditure scope, floor
thresholds, pass/fail findings, composite targets, target costs, federal effects,
savings, solver inputs, department-cut instructions, technology-savings claims,
and balanced-budget claims remain blocked.
