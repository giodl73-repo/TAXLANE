# TRN-B Function 400 Mapping Schema

The mapping must reconcile Function 400 totals to related named-fund outgo and
explicit perimeter residuals for FY2025-FY2031. Highway Fund outgo is related
to ground transportation and Airport and Airway Fund outgo is related to air
transportation, but neither relationship is one-to-one.

The non-named-fund and perimeter residual must not be relabeled as a general-
fund amount, solver allocation, or savings without account-level evidence.
