# TRN-D Incidence, Distribution, and Fairness Boundary Schema

This record closes `TRN-D-04` with a qualitative map of FAA, pilot,
connectivity-constrained, employer, and federal-funder relationships. The
official sources support optional digital use, physical fallback, connectivity
conditioning, authentication, and a scored FAA cost.

They do not support payer, income, geographic, employer, or user-benefit dollar
incidence. Those fields remain null. Completion permits `TRN-D-05` only.
