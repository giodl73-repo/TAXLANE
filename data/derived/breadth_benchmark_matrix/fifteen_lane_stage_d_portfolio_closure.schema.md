# Fifteen-lane stage-D portfolio closure schema

This artifact is the controlling proof for the portfolio goal that all fifteen
canonical Taxlane tracks are at or beyond stage D. It requires exactly fifteen
unique lane IDs and prefixes, one existing evidence path per lane, a reviewed
lane closure, an at-or-beyond-D result for every row, and zero remaining rows
below D.

The rollup must preserve payment integrity and revenue solvency as non-additive
overlays and net interest as endogenous. Every guarded numeric or public output
must remain null. Stage-D completion is bounded structural completion unless a
separate lane artifact positively admits a reform and downstream outputs.

The portfolio closure may not claim full lane coverage, floor passage, solver
readiness, a rate, savings, a public card, or a balanced-budget result.
