# Justice/courts/public-safety outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for
`justice_courts_public_safety_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the justice/courts/public-safety lane,
  target-cost contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, disaster packet precedent,
  justice depth card, and lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving federal/state/local translation,
  caseload/population denominators, rights/safety review, null missing values,
  false blocked gates, and no international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six justice-specific floor definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

All-government justice costs, federal/state/local translations, component policy
paths, caseload models, rights findings, floor thresholds, pass/fail findings,
target costs, federal effects, savings, solver inputs, department-cut
instructions, technology-savings claims, and balanced-budget claims remain
blocked.
