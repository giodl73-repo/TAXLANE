# Fifteen-track terminal disposition schema

This record closes Taxlane's current internal analytical portfolio across the
fifteen canonical tracks. Every track must appear exactly once with a reviewed
terminal disposition, the typed output (if any), its admitted FY2026 primary
spending effect, and a precise condition that would reopen analysis.

Terminal means no further Taxlane work is justified on the evidence currently
in custody. It does not mean enacted, implemented, officially scored, or proven
optimal. A reviewed zero-admission result is a completed analytical result, not
missing work. PAY must remain non-additive, OAS must remain on its dedicated
solvency rail, NET must remain endogenous, and unsupported spending amounts
must not lower REV's frozen revenue target.
