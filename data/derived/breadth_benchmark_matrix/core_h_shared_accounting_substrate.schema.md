# CORE-H Shared Accounting Substrate Schema

CORE-H is complete only when reusable checked-integer interfaces exist for
named-fund flows, separate reserves, federal deficit/debt rollforward, and
endogenous interest. Transfers, credited offsets, other income, balance
adjustments, financing/timing, and rounding must remain explicit.

The engine performs arithmetic; artifact adapters retain source vintage,
perimeter, nulls, and non-stitchable overlap diagnostics. CORE-H completion
does not choose lane-specific bridges or numeric reserve parameters and does
not imply a lane solver, rate, savings estimate, or balanced-budget result.
