# Fifteen-lane two-level F advancement queue schema

The queue contains exactly fifteen unique prefixes. Every row has a substantive
level 1 candidate/input task and a level 2 E-rerun/F-start audit with explicit
exit criteria. Priority tiers express dependency order and evidence maturity,
not policy importance. Queue publication records, but does not itself create,
candidate, solver, F-start, rate, or savings claims.

A completed level may cite its selection or rerun evidence. TRN now completes
both levels and completes F only as its typed cost note. REV Level 1 is started
at the legal and economic base perimeter. Neither state implies a solver run,
rate, savings result, or public rate card.
