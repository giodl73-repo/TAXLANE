# Seven owner/outcome lane execution-frontier schema

This record reranks exactly the seven `D_owner_outcome_evidence` lanes from the
fifteen-lane frontier. Ranks must be unique and contiguous from 1 through 7,
and the track set must be exactly `AGR`, `DIS`, `EDU`, `JUS`, `SEE`, `TRN`, and
`VET`.

Each row distinguishes evidence work that can advance against a currently
operating program from candidate-effect observation that requires a policy
change. A higher rank means a shorter active public-evidence path; it does not
mean that a proposal is desirable, enacted, scored against current law, or
admitted to the fiscal model. Fiscal scale is a tiebreaker only after operating
status, owner evidence, service floors, distribution, and current-law fit.

Exactly one row may be selected for the next evidence audit. Selection cannot
admit savings, sum headline envelopes, run a solver, initiate an official
request, authorize a public release, or change a rate.
