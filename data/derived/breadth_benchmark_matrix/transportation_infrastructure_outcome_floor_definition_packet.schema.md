# Transportation/infrastructure outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for
`transportation_infrastructure_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the transportation/infrastructure lane,
  target-cost contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, veterans packet precedent,
  transportation depth card, transportation pilot floor-indicator contract, and
  lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving federal/state/local translation,
  thresholdless pilot status, null missing values, false blocked gates, and no
  international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six transportation-specific floor
  definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

Asset inventories, maintenance-gap estimates, federal/state/local translations,
simulator runs, target costs, federal effects, savings, solver inputs,
department-cut instructions, technology-savings claims, and balanced-budget
claims remain blocked.
