# JUS District Capacity Delivery and Caseflow Stress Schema

This record executes normalized district-court capacity cases through
`taxlane-jus-capacity`.

New effective capacity must pass authorization, appointment, supporting-staff,
facility/security/technology, and productive-capacity gates. Baseline and
candidate pending-case identities equal beginning pending plus filings less
terminations, floored at zero. Direct compensation, operating, and one-time
readiness costs remain distinct.

Termination throughput is a declared stress assumption, not an observed or
causal effect. Pending-case arithmetic is not a timeliness, quality, counsel,
liberty, victim, disparity, due-process, or durable-resolution result. Cases
have no national population weight and supply no admissible fiscal saving.
