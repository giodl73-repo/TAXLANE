# VET H.R. 2137 Current-Law Owner-Evidence Audit Schema

This record reconciles H.R. 2137's status, CBO score, present VA claims
operations, and successor service candidate.

Claims and appeals process costs, Toxic Exposures Fund costs, Court of Appeals
for Veterans Claims costs, VA pension reductions, and Medicaid increases must
remain componentized. Pension and Medicaid interaction is not claims-process
efficiency. Current workload, backlog, accuracy, and appeal timing are baseline
context rather than an effect of unenacted legislation.

No proposal cost, late pension offset, backlog change, accuracy level, or
appeal time may be transformed into current savings or a rate effect.
