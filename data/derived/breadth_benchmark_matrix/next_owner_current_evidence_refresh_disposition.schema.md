# Next-owner current-evidence refresh disposition schema

## Product question

Do current official LIFELINE, ENVOY, COVENANT, and TRIBUNAL observations close
any Taxlane fiscal-admission or rate gate?

## Contract

`owner_refreshes` preserves each producer commit, replay command, pack hash,
feature/test count, evidence label, exact official findings, gate result, and
denied admission. `cross_owner_results` distinguishes corpus progress from
observed effects. `portfolio_result` records the fiscal and rate consequence.

## Invariants

- A source update date is not an observation date for every series.
- Financial activity is not access, rights, delivery, or outcome evidence.
- A grievance mechanism is not current resolution quality, safeguard
  compliance, local incidence, or a do-no-harm pass.
- House reporting, committee action, and cosponsors are not enactment,
  appropriation, implementation, or delivery.
- No track reopens and no rate changes without an admissible net fiscal effect
  and all applicable service, distribution, delivery, and overlap gates.
