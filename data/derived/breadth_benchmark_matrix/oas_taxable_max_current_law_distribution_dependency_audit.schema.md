# OAS taxable-maximum current-law and distribution dependency audit schema

This record corrects the evidence inventory without broadening the claim. CBO's
published retired-worker cohort and household-quintile results may close a
bounded distribution gate, but they may not stand in for survivor, disability,
family, poverty, employer-incidence, self-employment, or administrative results.

Vintage deficit and solvency effects must remain distinct from spending cuts,
REV, a 2026 Trustees rescore, and sustainable solvency. All eight gates must
reconcile to the decision counts, with zero admission and no rate change.
