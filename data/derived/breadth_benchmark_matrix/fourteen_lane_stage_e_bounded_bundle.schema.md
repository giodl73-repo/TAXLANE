# Fourteen-lane bounded stage-E bundle schema

The bundle contains exactly one unique HLT, EDU, OAS, ISF, VET, AGR, DEF, DIS,
JUS, SEE, INT, PAY, REV, and NET row. Every row must cite an existing E closure
whose reviewed D dependency is complete, whose five packages reconcile, whose
selection is complete with no candidate selected, and whose prohibited outputs
remain null. PAY/REV non-additivity and NET endogeneity are mandatory.
