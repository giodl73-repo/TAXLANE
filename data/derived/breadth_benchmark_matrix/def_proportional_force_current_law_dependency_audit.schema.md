# DEF proportional-force current-law dependency audit schema

This record separates a vintage CBO budget envelope, an illustrative force
strategy, enacted authorization, enacted appropriations, and a current program
cost analysis. Authorization must not be represented as appropriation, and no
vintage score may become current or additive without a matching force package
and scorekeeper rescore.

The seven gate rows must reconcile to the decision counts. Completing the
dependency audit may park DEF on named policy and publication triggers, but it
must preserve zero admission and no rate change.
