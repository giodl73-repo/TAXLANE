# HLT Level-3 Medicare Advantage benchmark scale-proxy schema

This record may scale the official December 2024 CBO option across the overlapping
FY2027-FY2034 Medicare path only as a planning sensitivity. It must preserve the
original option values, both baseline vintages and perimeters, the proxy formula,
and the fact that the February 2024 denominator is not the exact June 2024
baseline underlying the option.

The proxy is not a CBO rescore, current-law policy score, admitted saving, or
solver input.
