# PAY Level-4 full-DMF accounting and floor-audit schema

This record distinguishes evidence that a control caused a reported benefit
from evidence that the benefit is an additive federal budget effect. Every
candidate must separately pass benefit-type, program ownership, fund
disposition, fiscal timing, legal durability, due-process, and distribution
gates.

Passing causal attribution and cost netting does not authorize package savings
when the remaining conjunctive gates are blocked.
