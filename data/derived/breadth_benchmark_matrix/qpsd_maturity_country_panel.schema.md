# QPSD Maturity Country Panel

This record preserves four separately reported joint World Bank–IMF Quarterly
Public Sector Debt components for general government at 2022 Q4. All values are
nominal debt stocks as percent of GDP.

Short-term debt is classified by original maturity. It does not mean all debt
due within the next year. A fuller near-term stock can be formed only where the
source also reports long-term-original debt with remaining maturity of one year
or less. Missing remaining-maturity components are never converted to zero.

QPSD reporting is voluntary and as available. The record is maturity-pressure
context, not projected redemptions, gross financing needs, average maturity, a
country ranking, or available savings.
