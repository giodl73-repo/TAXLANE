# PAY-NET-REV post-fifteen-track reconciliation schema

The packet must integrate all completed internal all-fifteen results without
turning PAY measurements into additive savings or treating NET as a direct
cut. The fiscal identity begins with the FY2026 primary deficit, subtracts only
admitted primary spending reductions, applies typed PAY and NET adjustments,
and produces the remaining revenue need.

An unchanged planning schedule is not a proper or official rate. Official
score, official rate, and formal balance fields remain false or null until the
external scoring and certification gates close.
