# TRN-B Source Bridge Decisions Schema

TRN-B-02 and TRN-B-03 require explicit decisions, not forced agreement.
FY2025 OMB/Treasury comparisons must preserve differences and classify
incomparable rows. FY2031 OMB/CBO comparisons must preserve both vintages and
must not extend balance-only CBO context into missing income/outgo rows.

A decision to retain separate perimeters completes the bridge work when it is
supported by source scope, arithmetic, and review. It does not make the full
FY2025-FY2035 named-fund ledger ready.
