# TRN Level-1 H.R. 2247 candidate dossier schema

This dossier applies CORE-M to the admitted H.R. 2247 evidence. Selection is
profile-specific: the candidate may be selected for cost-only explanation and
official incremental-cost outputs without being selected for a rate, lower-cost
target, or savings output.

Every gate must be ready, blocked with an owner, or reviewed not applicable
with a rationale. Level 1 may close with the E rerun blocked; public release may
not.
