# SOCX Old-Age And Family Country Panel

The record uses OECD SOCX aggregate public expenditure for calendar 2022 as a
share of GDP. `TP01` combines old-age and survivors benefits. `TP51` covers
family benefits. `_T`, `C`, and `K` preserve total, cash, and in-kind/services.

Public old-age spending is not identical to United States Social Security and
does not capture every mandatory occupational or private pension pillar. Family
tax breaks are outside this exact response and are not imported from a mixed-
year table. Blank values remain missing even when observed components appear to
equal a total.

Spending is descriptive context. Adequacy, poverty, participation, outcomes,
efficiency, and savings require separate evidence.
