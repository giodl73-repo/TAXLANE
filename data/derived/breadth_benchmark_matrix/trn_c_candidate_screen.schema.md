# TRN-C Candidate Screen Schema

This record closes TRN-C-01 by selecting at most one current, named policy
instrument with official legislative text and an official federal-cost path.

Required checks:

- exactly three candidates are screened and exactly one is selected;
- H.R. 2247 is identified as passed by the House but not enacted;
- the official GPO bill-text and House-report source captures exist and their
  hashes match their metadata;
- stale or unscored alternatives remain rejected; and
- selection publishes no savings, target-cost, rate, or enactment claim.
