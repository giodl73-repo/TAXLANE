# Batch 3 TRN and REV two-level closure schema

The packet must contain TRN and REV exactly once. TRN Level A refreshes current
bill, score, enactment, appropriation, implementation, fund, and safety status;
Level B may retain only a conditional cost note unless the candidate passes
package admission. REV Level A may complete as a documented external-channel
reblock after all internal handoff deliverables are ready. REV Level B may
retain a planning-only schedule but may not imply an official score, certified
rate, statutory schedule, or formal balance determination.

The internal all-fifteen wave can be complete while authorized external REV
certification remains pending. Every unavailable official or enacted output
must remain null.
