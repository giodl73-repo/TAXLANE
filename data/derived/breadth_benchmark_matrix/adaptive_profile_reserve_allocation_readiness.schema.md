# Adaptive profile reserve allocation readiness schema

This generated record audits all fifteen tracks against the scoring contract
and current intervention dossiers, then reconciles all four profile reserves.
Separate rails are never treated as
ordinary service-lane awards. Missing inputs remain named, priority scores stay
null, and the full reserve remains unallocated until at least one specific
intervention clears every gate.

When candidates become ready, the generator computes the disclosed primary
score, distributes the allocatable share proportionally, iteratively applies
the intervention cap and single-lane cap, and retains any remainder. A
provisional allocation remains an internal scenario, not an appropriation or
funding recommendation.
