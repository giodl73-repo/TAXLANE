# Next-owner semantic-program completion disposition schema

## Product question

Have the four next owners reached ANCHOR/BASTION bounded semantic-product depth
without converting synthetic demonstrations into Taxlane effects?

## Contract

`completion_standard` defines the feature, evidence, handoff, documentation,
review, and validation requirements. `producer_packs` preserves each exact
commit, command, hash, feature/test count, fourteen-section handoff, evidence
label, and denied admission. `cross_owner_results` records requirement coverage.

## Invariants

- Every owner has at least twelve executable features and all required product
  capabilities.
- Every integrated pack has fourteen sections and an existing candidate ID.
- Synthetic scenario, transition, incidence, and comparison values cannot
  supersede official candidate records or enter the fiscal model.
- Illustrative comparisons are not public peer findings or policy targets.
- Product maturity does not imply outcome, floor, delivery, savings, allocation,
  track-reopening, or rate authority.
