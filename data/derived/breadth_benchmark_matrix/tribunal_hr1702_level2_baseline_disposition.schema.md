# TRIBUNAL H.R. 1702 Level 2 baseline disposition schema

## Product question

Does TRIBUNAL's Texas Southern workload snapshot complete H.R. 1702 Level 2
or change Taxlane's fiscal result?

## Contract

`producer_input` preserves the exact commit, command, hash, fourteen-section
contract, readiness, and denied authority. `current_system_floor_snapshot`
preserves district workload, nested immigration case mix, and explicit rights
and causality nulls.

## Invariants

- Immigration felony defendants are contained within all felony defendants.
- Defendant filings are not unique people.
- Workload and vacancies do not prove candidate need or effect.
- Missing counsel, detention, victim, and disparity measures cannot pass.
- No savings, reopening, target change, or rate recomputation occurs.
