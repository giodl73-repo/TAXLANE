# DIS NFIP Repetitive-Loss Mitigation Stress Schema

This record closes the current public-evidence ceiling for targeted NFIP
repetitive-loss mitigation and executes a normalized federal accounting
identity through `taxlane-dis-flood`.

The cohort is eligible properties multiplied by participation and completion.
The modeled federal investment is completed properties multiplied by unit cost
and federal cost share. After the delivery lag, annual federal effects equal
avoided NFIP claims plus reduced other federal aid, less reduced premium
revenue and administration cost. The cumulative net subtracts the investment.

The normalized inputs are transparent stress assumptions, not estimates. FEMA
selection thresholds are not actual unit costs or program caps; the GAO claims
concentration is not an avoided-claims share; and FEMA's social benefit-cost
test is not the narrower federal budget identity. No row may be scaled to the
national inventory or treated as an admitted score.
