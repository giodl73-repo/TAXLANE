# HLT site-neutral imaging current-law dependency audit schema

This record must distinguish the selected December 2024 CBO option from current
law, an implemented adjacent policy, and a future proposed policy. Proposed
scope, protections, and estimates may be retained as design context only. They
may not be represented as final law, observed outcomes, a CBO rescore, admitted
savings, or support for a rate change.

All seven gates must have one disposition and the decision counts must reconcile
to the gate rows. A completed dependency audit may move HLT to
publication-triggered monitoring while leaving its admitted fiscal effect at
zero.
