# CORE-I Shared Reform Admission Contract Schema

CORE-I is complete when all eight shared interfaces are implemented in
`taxlane-core`, bounded score observations are preserved, failed applicable
floors block admission, and cost-only reforms do not unlock target costs.

CORE-I is reusable infrastructure. It never selects a lane policy, treats a
non-applicable floor as passed, assigns a financing source, computes endogenous
interest, or publishes solver, rate, or savings outputs.
