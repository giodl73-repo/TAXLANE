# SEE WAP delivery-control public-evidence schema

This artifact records six control findings, their already-defined corrective
actions, and the current result or closeout evidence still required. Every row
must carry zero recoverable savings.

Planned or responsive actions are not completed actions. Old audit metrics are
not current results. Control risk is not a questioned or disallowed cost, and
service recovery is not cashable savings.

The successor is verification of existing corrective actions, not a duplicate
control program. AGR-style fiscal modeling is ineligible until same-cohort or
matched results, service outputs, allowable-cost disposition, budget period,
and fund disposition are available.

The artifact may move SEE to named-trigger monitoring and advance EDU. It may
not make a request, admit savings, run the solver, release a claim, or change a
rate.
