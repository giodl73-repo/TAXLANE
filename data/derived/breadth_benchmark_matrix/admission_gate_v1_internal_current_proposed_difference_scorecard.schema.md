# Admission Gate v1 Internal Current-Proposed-Difference Scorecard Schema

This scorecard separates current-law statutory bracket rates, TAXLANE's
preferred internal analytical schedule, the behavior contingency, and the
incremental effect of Admission Gate v1.

Rate-point differences are not effective-rate or household-burden estimates.
The analytical schedules are not enacted, official, submitted, or balance
certified. Per-lane zero differences mean no effect was admitted; they do not
mean programs have no improvement opportunities or no future fiscal effect.
