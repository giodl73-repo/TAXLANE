# TRN Freight Connector Lifecycle-Price Stress Envelope Schema

This record replays mechanical traffic, public/private capital, straight-line
private-capital recovery, an explicit O&M stress, a public lease payment, and
weighted vehicle prices for one normalized utilization case.

Utilization, O&M, recovery horizon, and price weights are assumptions, not
observations. Straight-line recovery excludes financing return, inflation,
tax, reserves beyond the stated O&M stress, elasticity, collection leakage,
and residual value. The outputs are not actual or recommended tolls, benefit,
ROI, savings, or a replication decision.
