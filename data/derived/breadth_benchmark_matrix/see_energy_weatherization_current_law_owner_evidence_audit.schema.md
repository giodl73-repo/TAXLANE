# SEE energy/weatherization current-law owner-evidence schema

The audit must keep the CBO SCEP portfolio option, enacted FY2026 WAP funding,
and the unenacted H.R. 1355 authorization separate. It records current WAP
service context and OIG control findings without treating either as an observed
effect of a funding reduction.

Every component disposition carries a zero admitted amount. A portfolio option
may not be assigned to WAP without a published component allocation and matching
score. An authorization may not be treated as an appropriation. An OIG control
finding may identify a successor analysis, but it may not be monetized without
allowable-cost, corrective-action, service, and fiscal evidence.

The artifact may select a bounded public control audit. It may not make an
official request, admit savings, run the solver, release a public claim, or
change rates.
