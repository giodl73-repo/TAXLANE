# EDU Pell Add-On Current-Law Owner-Evidence Audit Schema

This artifact confirms whether the mandatory Pell add-on still exists, separates the
current award rules from the December 2024 CBO score, and classifies the evidence
needed before any reduction can enter TAXLANE's fiscal solver.

Required sections are `source_rows`, `current_law_perimeter`, `legacy_cbo_score`,
`existing_evidence_tests`, `award_reduction_claim_boundary`, `successor_contract`,
`portfolio_handoff`, and `claim_boundaries`.

The award components must reconcile to the published maximum. The legacy annual
mandatory series must reconcile to its ten-year total. A maximum-award reduction is
not a recipient-level distribution: award formula, enrollment, cost, eligibility,
and special-rule dimensions remain explicit dependencies.

The record cannot admit savings, run the solver, or authorize a rate change. Official
descriptive evidence cannot be relabeled causal, and narrow randomized short-training
offer evidence cannot be generalized to the national Pell population.
