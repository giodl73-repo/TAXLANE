# REV Level-1 individual-income legal/economic base perimeter schema

This record completes `REV-L1-02` only at a definition-and-custody boundary. It
must link the source-custodied current-law chain from 26 USC sections 1 and
61–63 to the Form 1040 operational surface while keeping taxable income, tax
liability, credits, payments, refunds, fiscal cash receipts, and economic
incidence as separate concepts.

No numeric legal base, economic base, matched fiscal-year base, yield, rate,
solver result, or public rate card is admitted.
