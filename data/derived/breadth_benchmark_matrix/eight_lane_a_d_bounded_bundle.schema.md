# Eight-lane A-through-D bounded bundle schema

This contract creates dependency-ordered bounded A, B, C, and D stage chains
for DEF, DIS, JUS, SEE, INT, PAY, REV, and NET from existing source-custodied
lane evidence. Each lane artifact must contain four ordered stage rows, five D
bridge components, a role-reviewed closure decision, and null blocked outputs.

The C stage may close with no reform admitted. Its D stage then treats the
candidate-dependent yield package as not required and closes only the
structural financing, administration, behavior, incidence, distribution,
interaction, and fairness interface. PAY and REV remain non-additive overlays;
NET remains endogenous and cannot be directly cut.

Bounded D completion is not full-coverage, reform, floor-passage, financing,
solver, rate, savings, or successor-E readiness.
