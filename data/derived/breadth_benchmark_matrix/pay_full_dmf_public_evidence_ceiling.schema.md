# PAY full-DMF public-evidence ceiling schema

This record separates a completed public-source search from a completed fiscal
candidate. Each source states what it closes and what it does not publish. Each
of the nine PAY gates must end as `evidence_pass`, `owner_data_required`, or
`owner_data_and_future_period_required`, with a named closure owner.

`public_search_exhausted` means the cited GAO, enacted-law, and current Treasury
surfaces do not publish the required field as of the stated date. It does not
prove that the underlying event is zero or that no agency record exists.

The artifact may prevent repetitive public searching and define reopen
triggers. It may not request restricted or person data, infer a fiscal split,
admit savings, or change a rate.
