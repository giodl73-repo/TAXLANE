# REV internal-analysis next-ten-steps schema

This record is the completed independent-analysis roadmap. It must contain exactly
ten ordered, complete steps. Step 1 resets scope; Steps 2-10 move from a frozen baseline
through sensitivity scoring, PAY-NET-REV reconciliation, targeted 15-track
review, candidate ranking, and publication of a Taxlane analytical result.

`official_request_planned`, `external_submission_in_scope`, and
`official_certification_required` must remain false. The final result may be
called a Taxlane analytical rate recommendation, but never an official score,
enacted schedule, or proof of balance.
