# Agriculture outcome floor definition packet schema

Draft schema for `agriculture_outcome_floor_definition_packet.v1.draft.json`.

Required structure:

- identity fields tying the packet to the agriculture lane, target-cost
  contract, Pulse 160 outcome-floor gap, science/energy/environment packet
  precedent, agriculture depth card, and lane-depth tracker;
- source-custody booleans with no new external request or agency/person contact;
- definition-policy guardrails preserving separate budget support, insurance,
  general services, market-price support, nutrition handoff, null missing values,
  false blocked gates, and no international/fraud shortcut;
- five mandatory floor classes and six agriculture-specific floor definitions;
- blocked inputs and blocked outputs, all null;
- summary counts/readiness booleans;
- public warning phrases;
- claim booleans with only `definition_packet_published` true.

Budget-support paths, insurance paths, general-services paths, market-price
support paths, FY2025 function-350 account crosswalks, farm/acre/production/risk
denominators, recoverable-savings lineage, floor thresholds, pass/fail findings,
target costs, federal effects, savings, solver inputs, department-cut
instructions, technology-savings claims, and balanced-budget claims remain
blocked.
