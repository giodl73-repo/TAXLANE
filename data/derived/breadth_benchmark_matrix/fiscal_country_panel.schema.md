# Fiscal Country Panel

The record joins two separately labeled official sources for calendar 2022:
OECD tax revenue and tax mix, and the pinned IMF October 2025 Fiscal Monitor vintage for
general-government revenue, balances, and debt.

Tax revenue is not total revenue. Gross debt is not net debt. Neither peer
averages nor individual countries are fiscal targets.

OECD annual government accounts transaction D.41 supplies direct interest
payable for the eleven core peers. The percent-GDP value uses the captured OECD
GDP denominator. Percent of revenue divides that result by the separately
labeled IMF general-government revenue ratio. A separate pinned OECD Government
at a Glance measure supplies net interest spending as percent GDP. Gross D.41
interest and net interest after receipts remain distinct. Singapore is missing
from both series. Interest is never inferred from primary minus overall balance;
those component series are not universally internally consistent.
