# TRN Houbolt Freight Connector Current-Owner Evidence Audit Schema

This record replaces an aviation credential procedure with an operating road
service owned by transportation actors. It separates documented project facts,
design traffic, public and private capital, lease receipts, current surrounding
I-80 work, and still-missing operating outcomes.

Design traffic is not observed traffic. Project cost is not a federal budget
effect. A public contribution is not a saving, and private toll receipts are
not federal revenue. No corridor benefit, competitive price, lifecycle return,
or scalable national reform may be claimed without current owner records.
