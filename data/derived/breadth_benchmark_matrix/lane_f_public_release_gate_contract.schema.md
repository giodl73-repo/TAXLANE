# Lane F public-release gate contract

F is a release stage, not another bounded structural closure. A lane may start F
only after E is output-ready with a selected candidate, any profile-required
solver run, reconciled applicable sensitivity/floor/distribution results, audited source
and fiscal lineage, beneficiary/taxpayer review, eight-role review,
public-language review, reproducible release, and any profile-applicable
publication gate.

All ten gates must receive a CORE-M disposition, and every applicable gate is
conjunctive. Reviewed non-applicability requires a rationale and cannot supply a
value or result. Bounded E is not sufficient and partial F starts are
prohibited. PAY/REV/NET retain their special accounting treatments.
