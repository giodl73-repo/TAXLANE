# Adaptive needs scoring and allocation contract schema

The contract defines ten admission gates, a visible primary weighting, three
sensitivity profiles, confidence discounts, a ten-percent contingency, a
single-lane cap, and sunset treatment. Weights are normative choices, not facts.

No lane may receive a score from a benchmark gap or spending level alone. Every
scored row needs a current pain measure, severity target, affected population,
specific intervention, marginal outcome, whole-system cost, distribution,
delivery, duration, overlap review, and policy/stress floor passage.

OAS, PAY, REV, and NET remain outside the general adaptive reserve because they
are respectively a dedicated solvency/adequacy rail, non-additive overlay,
financing constraint, and endogenous result.
