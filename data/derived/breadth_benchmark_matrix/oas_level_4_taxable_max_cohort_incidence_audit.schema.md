# OAS Level-4 taxable-maximum cohort and incidence audit schema

This record distinguishes an official aggregate score and current trust-fund
context from a complete OASDI solvency candidate. Admission requires cohort
benefits, economic incidence, adequacy floors, administration, tax-unit overlap,
and a current-law actuarial rescore.

Payroll-tax effects remain separate from spending reductions and from REV's
individual-income-tax instrument even when an official score contains an
aggregate income-tax offset.
