# HLT-A Health Lane Perimeter and Source Inventory Schema

This record closes `HLT-A-01` by freezing a bounded federal health/Medicare
perimeter and classifying each available source family by permitted use.

Federal budget functions 550 and 570 control the federal topline. CMS NHE,
state/local shares, private spending, premiums, receipts, and quality/access
indicators are adjacent context and are not additive without an explicit
reconciliation bridge.

Inventory completion does not mean source capture or baseline completion. It
only permits `HLT-A-02` to construct one source-vintaged FY2025-FY2035 federal
baseline with explicit bridge rows and nulls.
