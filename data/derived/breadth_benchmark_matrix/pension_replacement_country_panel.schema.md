# Pension Replacement Country Panel

This record preserves the OECD Pensions at a Glance 2025 baseline model for a
male worker entering employment at age 22 in 2024, earning 100 percent of the
national average-worker wage throughout a full uninterrupted career.

Gross replacement is pension entitlement relative to gross pre-retirement
earnings. Net replacement uses earnings and pension income after personal taxes
and social contributions. Mandatory public and private schemes are included;
voluntary schemes are excluded.

The values are theoretical future entitlements under legislated rules, not
observed current-retiree income, benefit adequacy, poverty, spending efficiency,
solvency, or savings. They are not divided into the 2022 SOCX spending panel.
