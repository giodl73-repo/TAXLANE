# PennDOT statewide rumble-strip inventory capture

Captured 2026-07-28 from PennDOT's official Safety Infrastructure Improvement
Programs page:

- `Statewide-Rumble-Strips-03-01-2024.xlsx` is the linked “Statewide Rumble
  Strips (2024)” workbook.
- `safety-infrastructure-improvement-programs.html` preserves the landing page,
  workbook link, program context, and the separate descriptive statement about
  more than 6,800 centerline-rumble-strip miles as of September 2022.

The workbook contains a treatment table and its own definition sheet. `Test
Date` may be video-log collection or district data-entry date and is not
assumed to be installation date. Derived mileage calculations normalize
reversed offsets, disclose intervals exceeding the reported segment length,
and preserve the distinction between strip line-miles and roadway footprint.
