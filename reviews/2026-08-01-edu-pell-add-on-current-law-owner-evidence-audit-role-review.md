# EDU Pell Add-On Current-Law Owner-Evidence Role Review

- Finance: accepts the 2024 annual score only as historical option context; requires a current-law rescore.
- Student access: requires a recipient award distribution under 2026–27 rules before judging exposure.
- Education outcomes: preserves causal findings from short-training experiments but rejects national generalization.
- Institutional delivery: requires incidence by sector, program length, price, geography, and Workforce Pell status.
- Household finance: requires bounded loan, work, transfer, and unmet-need substitution rather than dollar-for-dollar borrowing.
- Law and operations: confirms the add-on remains identifiable while recording the newer eligibility and award rules.
- Scope: records no official request, public release, candidate admission, solver run, or rate change.

Decision: advance EDU to its current-recipient distribution and substitution test. Keep both the $44.4 billion mandatory and $45.2 billion combined legacy figures outside admitted savings.
