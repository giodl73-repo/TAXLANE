# DIS NFIP Repetitive-Loss Mitigation Stress Role Review

- Fiscal accounting: accepts the federal identity; rejects the claims concentration as an avoidable share.
- Mitigation delivery: requires participation, completion, intervention type, and lag by cohort.
- Insurance: preserves avoided claims and reduced premium revenue as separate flows.
- Household and community: requires voluntary participation, relocation rights, affordability, retained coverage, and local capacity.
- Disaster assistance: keeps other federal aid separate and unestimated.
- Evidence: treats FY2024 selection thresholds as ranking rules, not actual costs or caps.
- Scope: records no request, release, national scaling, admission, solver run, or rate change.

Decision: the executable envelope is reusable, but the public evidence cannot populate a national score. Put DIS on named evidence triggers and advance JUS.
