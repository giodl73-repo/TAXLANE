# Minnesota CCAP Quality Learning Center CY2025 Existing-Records Request Specification Role Review

## Decision

Accept the machine specification, internal reader, and unsent request template
as an authorization-gated acquisition plan. Do not submit it. The bundle closes
no evidence gap and changes no external-claim or response state. All twelve
claim gates remain false.

## Source Custodian

Pass. The request route and rules use the official DCYF request page and public-
data guide plus official Minnesota Statutes sections 13.03 and 142E.02. Those
sources support request-process and program-administration context only. They do
not guarantee that requested records exist, are public, or will be released.

## Accountability Maintainer

Pass. The target is bounded to provider license 1087038 and CY2025 existing
records that could identify transaction, adjustment, cutoff, calculation, and
full-year aggregation lineage. The draft does not ask DCYF to calculate the
testimony's $1.9 million, run a new query, build a custom export, or create a
reconciliation.

The fifth atom remains `attributed_claim_supported` and `source-reviewed`, with
`not_recorded` request status, `none_established` legal or administrative
status, empty response-source, corroboration, counterevidence, and lineage
arrays, `not_summable`, and every gate false.

## Privacy and Due Process Reviewer

Pass for internal quarantine only. The draft excludes child, family, recipient,
caregiver, and staff identifiers; case, eligibility, benefit, taxpayer, and
Social Security identifiers; attendance details; contact and free-text data;
banking information; and credentials. It accepts provider-level aggregates,
lawful redaction, and redacted or deidentified transaction rows.

No request has been sent to the named provider or DCYF, and no response is
recorded. Future agency correspondence or produced records require separate
custody, classification, privacy, reconciliation, and due-process review.

## Taxpayer Reviewer

Pass. The target makes the exact gap inspectable: a source-stated CY2025 $1.9
million amount versus an official full-CY2025 $2,150,964, with the December
16/year-to-date cutoff and calculation basis unresolved. Drafting the request
does not make either amount correct or incorrect.

## Reform Skeptic

Pass with outbound action blocked. A no-records response, denial, redaction,
clarification request, classification decision, or cost estimate would be an
administrative event, not evidence of zero payments, fraud, debt, recovery, or
savings. A future production cannot change a claim field or gate until reviewed
and adopted separately.

## Authorization Gate

The owner must explicitly authorize submission, resolve requester and contact
placeholders, choose exactly one channel, approve a copy-fee cap, and approve
the frozen scope. Until then, `owner_authorization_obtained`,
`request_submitted`, `external_message_sent`, `fee_commitment_made`, and
`outbound_state_changed` remain false.
