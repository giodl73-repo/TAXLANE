# VET H.R. 2137 Current-Law Owner-Evidence Audit Role Review

- Fiscal: rejects the expired FY2026 enactment timing and separates direct from appropriated effects.
- Veterans benefits: identifies the pension reduction rather than claims processing as the late offset.
- Medicaid: preserves the federal Medicaid increase and forbids double counting the interaction.
- Claims operations: treats inventory, backlog, accuracy, and docket timing as baseline context only.
- Veteran access: requires missed-exam reason, notice, accommodation, travel, disability, rurality, survivor, and caregiver incidence.
- Technology and workforce: keeps IT, processor, attorney, exam-quality, training, and maintenance costs explicit.
- Scope: records no request, release, admission, solver run, savings, or rate change.

Decision: retire the old timing path and build a normalized missed-exam review and claimant-burden stress before placing VET on triggers.
