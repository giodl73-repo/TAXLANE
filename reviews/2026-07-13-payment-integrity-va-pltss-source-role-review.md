# VA PLTSS Payment-Integrity Source And Role Review

## Scope

This review covers the corrected Q4 2025 PaymentAccuracy scorecard probe for
VA Purchased Long Term Services and Supports (PLTSS), its reconciliation to the
FY2024 annual workbook, and the public wording in the payment-integrity depth
card.

## Source finding

The previously recorded scorecard URL does not resolve to a PDF, and the stored
$2.502B / 15.54% values do not appear in the official PLTSS scorecard. The
publisher's Q4 2025 scorecard reports **$218.30M** in projected FY2024
overpayments at **3.88%**, based on a sampling timeframe from October 2022
through September 2023. Those fields agree with the official FY2024 annual
workbook.

The source conflict is therefore closed as an extraction correction. It is not
evidence of a scorecard revision, program deterioration, fraud, or savings.

## Role decisions

| Role | Decision |
|---|---|
| Source Custodian | Source-grounded. The live PDF, observed date, raw capture, checksum, reporting period, sample period, and corrected fields are recorded. |
| Budget Accountant | Accounting-safe with boundary. Projected overpayments remain separate from technically improper payments, unknown payments, identified debts, recoveries, and net savings. |
| Program Beneficiary Reviewer | Beneficiary impact visible but incomplete. No control or savings proposal is approved until access, continuity, denial, appeal, and provider-burden floors are reviewed. |
| Reform Skeptic | Skepticism addressed. The correction removes false precision and does not convert the estimate into fraud, waste, recoverability, or savings language. |

## Decision

Pass for source correction and public explanation of the correction. The
official $218.30M FY2024 overpayment estimate may be used with its 3.88% rate,
sample period, projected-estimate label, and program scope.

Fail for fraud claims, collectible-recovery claims, prevented-loss claims, or
net-savings scoring. The scorecard's separately identified and recovered amounts
are operational recovery activity, not a direct subset of the projected
$218.30M estimate without a matched debt and period bridge.
