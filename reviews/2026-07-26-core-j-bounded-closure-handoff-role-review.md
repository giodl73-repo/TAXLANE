# CORE-J Bounded Closure and Lane-Handoff Role Review

Date: 2026-07-26

Decision: accept CORE-J and permit TRN-E-02, HLT-B-02, and EDU-B-02 discovery.

The fiscal, legal, evidence, accounting, beneficiary, taxpayer, implementation,
and public-language roles agree that CORE-J preserves rather than weakens
lane-local gates. The contract prevents bounded structural closure from being
described as numerical or output readiness, requires signed/non-additive
accounting boundaries, and makes unresolved handoffs explicit and owned.
