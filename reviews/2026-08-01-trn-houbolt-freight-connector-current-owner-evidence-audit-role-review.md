# TRN Houbolt Freight Connector Current-Owner Evidence Audit Role Review

- Transportation owner: the city, state, private delivery vehicle, and intermodal service are in the correct lane.
- Freight: design traffic is preserved as design traffic; actual truck use, queue time, reliability, and diversion remain open.
- Fiscal: public capital, private capital, private toll revenue, and city lease receipts remain separate.
- Price: no toll is called competitive without paid-price, utilization, cost, debt, reserve, and alternative-route evidence.
- Access: small carriers, households, community-road diversion, emergency access, noise, and emissions remain hard floors.
- Resilience: closure, recovery, maintenance condition, and alternate capacity require observation.
- Scope: no request, release, replication decision, admission, savings, solver run, or rate change is recorded.

Decision: retire H.R. 2247 from TRN and advance the Houbolt operating connector to a normalized price stress and owner-observation trigger.
