Accepted before implementation, 2026-10-05. This adds illustrative civic education arithmetic to the existing budget sandbox, not an admitted savings claim or a transportation service model.

Historical 2024 nominal costs, no inflation or marginal-cost adjustment.

Budget Accountant: accounting-safe with explicit illustrative allocation. Only the positive incremental transportation lane outlay times a user-selected 0–100% share becomes a hypothetical transit operating allocation. This is a suballocation, not added spending; federal budget totals remain exactly the existing scenario totals. No full transportation aggregate is treated as bus operations. Zero or negative increments give zero additional allocation, not estimated service losses. General revenue is fungible; deficit financing remains visible; this is not statutory earmarking or tracing taxes.

Reform Skeptic: skepticism addressed. Cost-equivalent hours = hypothetical allocation / historical average operating expense per vehicle revenue hour. This does not predict purchased or delivered service, ridership, access, a timetable, or feasible marginal expansion. Mode-specific domain outcomes remain owned by the jurisdiction-map providers. No official score or reform admission.
