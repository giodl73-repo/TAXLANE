# EXPL-A foundation — round 1 `.roles` review

Review basis: all eight AI-simulated Taxlane roles in `.roles/`. These are
review lenses, not people or external endorsements.

## Decision

Major revisions required before EXPL-B. Two P1 and six P2 findings are open.

## Findings

### T-1 Taxpayer Advocate — needs plain-language rewrite

- **P1:** The one-sentence result presents seven percentages before explaining
  that they are marginal model bracket rates, not one taxpayer's effective rate
  or liability. Define the concept in the same reading unit.
- **P2:** Add a compact example distinguishing a marginal bracket rate from the
  share of total income paid.

### T-2 Budget Accountant — fund accounting unclear

- **P1:** “Revenue target” needs a stronger boundary: it belongs to Taxlane's
  frozen FY2026 ordinary-income rate-model rail, not all receipts, trust-fund
  finance, total outlays, or an official federal funding requirement.
- **P2:** State explicitly that $813.727B is not a literal allocation of one
  taxpayer's dollars and that OAS payroll finance does not enter it.

### T-3 Source Custodian — needs version/date

- **P2:** The evidence inventory names records but does not surface their
  as-of date or pulse. Add a custody/vintage table and use exact paths in the
  number ledger.

### T-4 Public Goods Steward — funding-mode tradeoff missing

- **P2:** Add the civic purpose of the fifteen tracks and explain why Taxlane
  preserves both general-revenue flexibility and dedicated-financing
  visibility rather than assuming one funding mode fits every purpose.

### T-5 Program Beneficiary — beneficiary impact visible with revision

- **P2:** Explain that candidate rejection protects against counting savings
  before access, quality, reliance, and distribution effects are understood;
  it does not declare existing service levels optimal.

### T-6 Compliance Burden — complexity cost missing

- **P2:** The administration ceiling is visible, but the foundation does not say
  that taxpayer, employer, preparer, avoidance, enforcement, and transition
  burdens remain model limitations. Add them beside the rate result.

### T-7 Fiscal Sustainability — long-term commitment unclear

- **P2:** Distinguish the one-year FY2026 result from a ten-year or long-run
  solvency claim. Explain that NET is recomputed only from admitted upstream
  debt paths and that no formal balance result follows.

### T-8 Reform Skeptic — reform claim overstates evidence

- **P2:** Add an authority/control statement: Taxlane cannot change law,
  appropriations, tax bases, or program rules. Make the frozen assumptions and
  false-precision risk visible before downstream formats reuse the headline.
- **P3:** Prefer “result under this decision rule” to “answer” in later citizen
  materials.

## Required revision set

1. Rewrite the headline and add a marginal-rate example.
2. Define the exact revenue-target and fund boundary.
3. Add pulse/as-of custody and exact paths.
4. Add public-purpose, beneficiary, compliance, long-run, authority, and false-
   precision limitations.
5. Repeat the strengthened caveat in downstream outlines.

Round 2 may begin only after all P1 and P2 items are applied.
