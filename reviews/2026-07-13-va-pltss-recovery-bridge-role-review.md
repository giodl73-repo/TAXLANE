# VA PLTSS Recovery Bridge Role Review

## Scope

This review covers the attempted bridge from the $218.30M projected FY2024
PLTSS overpayment estimate to scorecard recovery activity and the FY2024
VA-wide annual recovery table.

## Findings

| Role | Decision |
|---|---|
| Source Custodian | Source-grounded. Each value retains its publisher, program or agency scope, reporting period, and measurement basis. |
| Budget Accountant | Accounting-safe only as separate rows. A statistical projection is not an established receivable, and collection activity is not a same-period reduction of the estimate. |
| Program Beneficiary Reviewer | Reliance risk remains visible. No control proposal or savings score is approved without access, continuity, denial, appeal, reversal, timeliness, and provider-burden evidence. |
| Reform Skeptic | Pass for the failed-match conclusion. Mixed-period rows, VA-wide amounts, and projected estimates are not combined into a recovery percentage or savings claim. |

## Decision

Pass the structured bridge as **blocked**. Public explanation may say that
PLTSS recovery activity exists and report the separate official rows with their
periods. It may also explain why no recovery rate against $218.30M is available.

Fail any calculation that divides, subtracts, or nets the recovery rows against
the projected estimate. Fail any fraud, collectible-debt, prevented-loss, or
net-savings claim.

## Closure evidence

Closure requires cohort-level lineage from sampled payment through projected
error, established receivable, review or appeal disposition, collection or
offset, write-off or non-collectability, and recovery/control cost.
