# EXPL-C paper series — round 1 `.roles` and PF-gate review

Review basis: four paper drafts, all eight `.roles` lenses, and the standing PF
paper gates. The lenses are AI-simulated and imply no external endorsement.

## Decision

Major revisions. One P1 and six P2 findings are open. PF-2 comparability and
PF-6 skepticism are provisional passes; PF-7 sourcing fails until inline
figure-to-ledger routing is added.

## `.roles` findings

| Lens | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| T-1 Taxpayer Advocate | P2 | Paper abstracts introduce schedules without defining “marginal” in the first reading unit. Add a one-sentence definition to the synthesis and rate abstracts. |
| T-2 Budget Accountant | P2 | Every paper needs a standard fiscal-object note separating FY2026 model target, total receipts/outlays, trust funds, borrowing, and formal balance. |

| T-6 Compliance Burden | P2 | The rate paper should distinguish the $0.077B administration ceiling from a complete taxpayer/employer/preparer compliance model. |
| T-7 Fiscal Sustainability | P2 | Add a standard note that borrowing and total debt remain outside the one-year target and NET feedback is not a long-run debt path. |
| T-8 Reform Skeptic | P3 | Replace any unqualified “adaptive” language with “analytically recomputable subject to legal authority.” |

## PF gate findings

- **PF-2 Comparative Fiscal Scholar — provisional pass:** no cross-country or
  mismatched-government comparison is load-bearing. Preserve fiscal/tax-year
  labels.
- **PF-6 Political Economy / Reform Skeptic — provisional pass:** normative
  selection and lack of legal authority are disclosed. Apply the T-8 wording.
- **PF-7 Budget Process Analyst — fail (P1):** figures are traceable only at the
  paper level, not at point of use. Inline NUM/CLM routing is required before
  acceptance.
- **PF-5 Distribution / Equity — P2:** the rate paper mentions model summaries
  but must explicitly state what the bundled CPS tax-unit analysis cannot
  establish about statutory incidence, family transitions, geography, or
  employer shifting.

## Required revisions

Add a shared evidence-key convention, inline IDs, fiscal-object note,
marginal-rate definition, public-purpose taxonomy, service-continuity rule,
compliance distinction, debt/horizon boundary, distribution limitations, and
legal-authority wording. Round two must re-evaluate PF-2, PF-6, and PF-7 before
PDF rendering.
