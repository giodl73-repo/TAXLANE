# REV Level-1 post-2025 rate-rescore proxy role review

Date: 2026-07-27

Decision: approve the proxy for guarded numerical planning and reject it as a
formal score or public rate card. The all-bracket and top-four alternatives make
the statutory-distribution tradeoff visible, but economic incidence remains
unquantified. Promotion requires a current-law tax-unit model, nonlinear
behavior, distribution, administration, and timing review.
