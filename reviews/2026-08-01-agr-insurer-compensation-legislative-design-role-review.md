# AGR insurer-compensation legislative-design role review

| Review lens | Disposition | Fixed-point finding |
|---|---|---|
| Public-finance economist | pass | The design specifies a scoreable mechanism while keeping gross effects, implementation cost, offsets, net outlays, and admission separate. |
| Agricultural economist | pass with risk | Producer premium support is unchanged, but indemnity, disaster-assistance, taxable-income, and participation interactions remain required score inputs. |
| Insurance economist | pass with risk | A 12-percent target is reproducible official-option precedent, not a current market optimum; capital, retained risk, entry, exit, and market-share response remain open. |
| Producer-service reviewer | pass with risk | Five fail-closed floors and targeted stabilization protect delivery conceptually; the preimplementation data needed to operate them remains unavailable. |
| Distribution reviewer | pass with risk | State, commodity, specialty, high-loss, farm-size, and resource-group slices are required; no distribution result is inferred. |
| Legislative counsel | pass | The necessary exception to budget neutrality and payment recycling is stated expressly, and the design does not mislabel administrative authority. |
| Scope guardian | pass | No enactment, official request, current score, admission, solver run, public release, or rate effect is claimed. |

Fixed point: the bounded design is complete for a repo-only sensitivity and
service-stress model. Candidate admission remains blocked by current market
data, operational floor evidence, and a matching current-law score.
