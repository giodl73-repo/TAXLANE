## Round-two result

**Accepted by all eight lenses with zero open P1/P2 findings.**

The revised value proposition is clear and supportable:

> **Taxlane is an evidence-control system for fiscal claims. It refuses to count
> savings until sources, accounting, affected people, timing, implementation,
> and overlap reconcile—then shows the rate implication of what remains.**

The site now states the difficult present result plainly: the current Taxlane
recommendation is not a lower-rate proposal. It also shows the honest route to
lower rates—specific, source-backed spending or financing changes that preserve
applicable outcomes and survive a new accounting and microsimulation cycle.

## Round-one findings closed

1. **International source custody — closed.** The site uses the repository's
   captured OECD panel and a new calculation record. U.S. GF05 is missing, not
   zero, and the partial normalization is visible.
2. **Hero marginality and scope — closed.** Marginal meaning and the full scoped
   target boundary sit beside the schedule.
3. **Concrete taxpayer and beneficiary examples — closed.** Six narrow taxpayer
   profiles and two candidate-gate cases are visible.
4. **Compliance and sustainability adjacency — closed.** Both appear directly
   on the rates page rather than only in end caveats.
5. **Effort-count framing — closed.** User-visible outcomes lead; counts are a
   subordinate, reproducible corpus inventory.
6. **Peer-aligned lower-rate expectation — bounded.** Defense-share and
   health-cost convergence are open downward research cases; social protection
   is shown as a likely expansion/restructuring case; all carry null savings and
   null rates until scored.

## Residual P3 research frontier

- Replace bracket-only teaching profiles with a full, reviewed household
  microsimulation surface if complete filing, credits, payroll, capital gains,
  incidence, and benefit data are approved for that purpose.
- Build same-year, all-government U.S.–peer component crosswalks before scoring
  defense, health, or social-protection convergence.
- For any selected peer scenario, publish the concrete policy instrument,
  transition path, outcome floors, distribution, annual net savings, debt
  feedback, and rerun rate schedule together.

These are future research improvements, not blockers to the revised local
explanation surface.
