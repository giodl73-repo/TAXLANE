# REV Level-1 individual-income legal/economic base perimeter role review

Date: 2026-07-27

Decision: approve REV-L1-02 as a definition-only completion. Candidate,
taxpayer, budget, legal, implementation, evidence, distribution, and
public-language review agree that the source-custodied legal chain is adequate
to distinguish taxable income, liability, credits, payment flows, cash
receipts, and economic incidence.

The review rejects using all returns as the payer universe, tax after credits
as cash receipts, or SOI taxable income as a quantified economic burden base.
Matched-base, behavior, incidence, solver, rate, and publication gates remain
closed.
