# Rust 2.0 site communication role review

> **AI-simulated review notice:** These are repository role lenses, not real
> people, professional advice, government review, or external endorsement.

## Scope

Reviewed the seven-page repository-local TAXLANE explanation site after its
Admission Gate v1 and Rust 2.0 communication refresh. The panel tested claim
precision, taxpayer legibility, accounting, source custody, public purpose,
beneficiary/service continuity, compliance burden, sustainability, and reform
skepticism. No public release or external submission was considered.

| ID | Role | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
