# TRN-C Real Reform Closure Role Review

Date: 2026-07-26

Decision: approve bounded TRN-C closure and permit TRN-D to start.

| Role | Finding |
|---|---|
| Fiscal accountant | The CBO FY2026 asterisk stays bounded; exact FY2027-FY2031 rows and the published $18 million total are not residually altered. |
| Budget scorer | The score is conditional on enactment and appropriation and is classified as function 400 spending subject to appropriation. |
| Transportation operator | Physical credentials remain usable when digital presentation, devices, cloud access, or connectivity fail. |
| Safety reviewer | FAA issuance plus authentication and verification are explicit; the scenario claims no change in qualification or safety outcomes. |
| Equity reviewer | Digital presentation is optional, the physical channel remains, and no user take-up or burden estimate is invented. |
| Technology reviewer | Software and rulemaking costs are positive outlays; productivity, staffing, time savings, and cost-overrun values remain unsupported nulls. |
| Legal reviewer | H.R. 2247 passed the House and was referred to the Senate but is not enacted; the record makes no current-law claim. |
| Public-language reviewer | The closure says cost-only, names the $18 million score and conditional boundary, and blocks savings, rates, endorsement, and enactment language. |

The review does not approve H.R. 2247 as policy. It approves the integrity of
the scenario boundary and the dependency handoff to TRN-D.
