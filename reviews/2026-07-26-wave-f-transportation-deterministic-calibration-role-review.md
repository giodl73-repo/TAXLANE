# Role Review: Wave F Transportation Deterministic Calibration

## Decision

Approved for deterministic simulator calibration only. Transportation is
selected only for this bounded mechanical fixture; this is not approval of a
reform instrument, target cost, revenue source, rate, or public policy claim.

| Role | Result | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| T-1 Taxpayer Advocate | Pass with guardrail | No assigned base, rate, burden, or taxpayer allocation is produced. |
| T-2 Budget Accountant | Pass | The fixture reproduces primary-outlay, net-cash, transfer, fund-balance, debt, interest, and rounding identities. |
| T-3 Source Custodian | Pass with guardrail | Synthetic units are unmistakably separated from official source-custodied values. |
| T-4 Public Goods Steward | Pass with guardrail | Transportation service scope remains visible and a mechanical run is not treated as service adequacy. |
| T-5 Program Beneficiary | Pass | Missing access, quality, equity, resilience, and delivery floors correctly block lower-cost recognition. |
| T-6 Compliance Burden | Pass with guardrail | No transition estimate, incidence estimate, or administrative savings is claimed. |
| T-7 Fiscal Sustainability | Pass | A higher primary deficit increases subsequent synthetic debt and net interest; interest is not cut directly. |
| T-8 Reform Skeptic | Pass | The adverse path and no-op reserve are calibration tests, not forecasts or parameter recommendations. |

## Preserved blockers

Real reforms, official fiscal effects, complete floors, transition estimates,
receipt bases, distribution and incidence, substantive reserve parameters,
full fund paths, target costs, rates, savings, and balanced-budget publication
remain blocked after Wave F.
