# REV Level-1 individual-income rate-candidate start role review

Date: 2026-07-27

Decision: approve the federal individual income tax as the first real REV
instrument context for Level 1. Candidate, taxpayer, beneficiary, budget,
legal, implementation, evidence, and public-language review agree that the OMB
receipt category and IRS return context establish a useful probe.

The review rejects dividing the unmatched FY2025 receipt and TY2023 base values.
It accepts REV-L1-02 as definition-only and REV-L1-03 as a bounded reblock.
Behavior, administration, incidence, distribution, selection, solver, and
publication gates remain blocked, with REV-L1-04 now active.
