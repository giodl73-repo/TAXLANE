# TRN Freight Connector Lifecycle-Price Stress Envelope Role Review

- Numeracy: capital shares, traffic volumes, annual requirement, and weighted-price identities replay in the feature crate.
- Finance: straight-line recovery is explicitly not debt service, return, NPV, or a concession model.
- Operations: assumed utilization cannot stand in for classed traffic, reliability, incident, queue, or downtime observations.
- Fair access: price, toll-free alternative, small-carrier, household, diversion, and community burdens remain gates.
- Asset and resilience: maintenance reserve, condition, closure, recovery, and alternate capacity remain unobserved.
- Fiscal: private receipts, city lease receipts, state capital, and any federal effect remain separate.
- Scope: the stress is not a forecast, actual toll, recommendation, replication decision, admission, savings, solver run, or rate change.

Decision: accept the normalized engine and place TRN on named owner-observation and reopen triggers at zero admission.
