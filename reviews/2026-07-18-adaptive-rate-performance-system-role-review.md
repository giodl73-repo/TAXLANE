# Role Review: Adaptive Rate and Performance System

## Scope

This review applies the existing `.roles` panel lenses to:

- `context/waves/2026-07-18-adaptive-rate-performance-system/WAVE.md`

The reviewed plan proposes the next phase after Pulse 81: adaptive effective
rates, overspending-risk detection, and technology-transition updates.

## Decision

Approved to proceed as a planning and contract-building phase.

Not approved for public statutory rates, effective rates, tax proposals,
department cuts, waste findings, fraud findings, savings estimates, or
balanced-budget claims.

## Cleanup applied after review

1. Replaced public-facing "prove to the world" posture with a testable thesis:
   TAXLANE can build a transparent operating model that calculates rates only
   when gates pass and names blockers otherwise.
2. Added explicit disallowed shortcuts for fraud, savings, recoverability, and
   technology-cut claims.
3. Made technology modernization a transition path with upfront costs, stress
   cases, and service-floor checks rather than an automatic reduction.
4. Added a public-card target shape that includes burden, distribution, floors,
   evidence grade, blockers, and public-claim status.
5. Deferred pilot-lane selection to a role-reviewed gate to avoid premature
   normative or source choices.
6. Preserved Pulse 80 and Pulse 81 blockers: no public rates, no public rate
   cards, and no balanced-budget claim.

## P1 blockers before any public claim

- Assigned-base data must include matched year, legal/economic perimeter,
  baseline amount, elasticity, avoidance/compliance, burden, distribution,
  interactions, current-law yield, and reform yield.
- Overspending-risk signals must not be described as waste, fraud, recoverable
  savings, or budget savings without separate positive evidence.
- Technology-transition scenarios must include implementation cost, training,
  cybersecurity, privacy, fallback/resilience, phase-in, measured productivity,
  and floors.
- Lower-cost scenarios must pass all required outcome floors.
- Net interest must be endogenous and reserves/emergency rules must be
  parameterized before any balanced-budget claim.
- Public language must pass role review before publication.

## Non-goals

- This review does not approve a tax rate.
- This review does not choose a pilot lane.
- This review does not identify a department as overspending.
- This review does not claim technology savings.
- This review does not approve a public balanced-budget claim.
