# Final-result explanation program role review

## Decision

Approved as a repository-only documentation and explanation program.

## Findings

- The audience ladder serves citizens, explainers, policy readers, and technical
  auditors without creating separate factual authorities.
- EXPL-A through EXPL-F have explicit dependencies and exit gates.
- Guides, papers, presentations, and HTML reuse canonical claims and numbers.
- Accessibility, adversarial reading, numerical consistency, and traceability
  are first-class review gates.
- Repository readiness is distinct from external release authority.
- Deployment, distribution, outreach, government contact, official requests,
  legislative submission, and endorsement claims are explicitly prohibited.

EXPL-A may start. No external action is approved by this review.
