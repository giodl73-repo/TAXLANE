# External Accountability Claim Intake Role Review

## Decision

Accept as an internal quarantine inventory only. The four URL-observed records
are not captured, exact-text verified, corroborated, or approved for attributed
public reporting. They create no finding, score, amount lineage, methodology
closure, or public route. All attributed and substantive gates remain false.

## Source Custodian

Pass with required follow-up. Every URL has a distinct source-ledger ID and the
records honestly state `url_observed_not_captured`. Do not advance source status
until content, retrieval date, custody path, and SHA-256 are recorded. Media
coverage and official hosting must retain their limited evidence relations.
The added DOJ, HHS-OIG, CDPH, and California DOJ URLs are likewise context only:
their sector or jurisdiction may be relevant, but exact-atom linkage and
overlap are not established.

## Accountability Maintainer

Pass. One row contains one amount atom; lower-bound claims remain bounds, all
amounts are not summable, and unknown universes, periods, derivations, and
overlap are visible. Attributed reporting is structurally separate from
underlying facts, findings, fraud, debt, recovery, performance, and savings.
Official context does not populate corroboration or counterevidence arrays or
change `none_established` legal status without exact program/entity/transaction,
period, universe, and amount mapping.

## Privacy and Due Process Reviewer

Pass for quarantine only. Neutral paraphrases replace quotations, subject IDs
are empty, and no named provider or recipient is introduced. Before any named
subject is added or attributed wording is published, record response status,
counterevidence, corrections, and role-approved language.

## Taxpayer Reviewer

Pass. The inventory makes potentially important claims inspectable without
presenting sensational aggregate numbers as established public loss. The
reader explains why none of the four amounts may be summed or treated as money
that was stolen, owed, recovered, prevented, or saved.

## Reform Skeptic

Pass with all gates blocked. A viral source, press account, congressional PDF
host, or official appearance does not establish the underlying allegation.
The claimed national savings amount lacks baseline, counterfactual, causal
link, timing, scope, offsets, costs, overlap treatment, and net reconciliation.

## Required use rule

Internal quarantine use only: record that an attributed claim was published,
preserve source and amount semantics, and request corroborating or
counterevidence; do not present the underlying allegation as fact or infer
fraud, waste, debt, collectibility, recovery, prevention, performance, or
savings.
