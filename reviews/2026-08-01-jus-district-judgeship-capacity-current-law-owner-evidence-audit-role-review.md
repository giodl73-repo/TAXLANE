# JUS District Judgeship Capacity Current-Law Audit Role Review

- Fiscal: retains CBO's $394 million as an expired-timing proposal cost, not current law or savings.
- Courts: treats Judicial Conference recommendations and weighted filings as capacity-need inputs, not outcome proof.
- Delivery: requires authorization, nomination, confirmation, chambers staff, facilities, security, and technology milestones.
- Caseflow: separates filings, terminations, pending matters, case mix, multidistrict litigation, and time-to-disposition.
- Rights: keeps counsel, liberty, victim notice and voice, disparity, due process, and durable resolution open.
- Federalism and incidence: requires district and litigant distribution rather than a national average.
- Scope: records no request, release, admission, solver run, savings, or rate change.

Decision: retire the old timing path, preserve the capacity need, and build only a normalized delivery-to-caseflow stress before placing JUS on triggers.
