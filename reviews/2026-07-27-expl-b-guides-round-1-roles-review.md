# EXPL-B citizen and teaching guides — round 1 `.roles` review

Review basis: six guide sources reviewed through all eight AI-simulated Taxlane
roles. No external endorsement is implied.

## Decision

Minor-to-major revision. One P1 and seven P2 findings must close before EXPL-C.

## Findings

### T-1 Taxpayer Advocate — needs plain-language rewrite

- **P2:** The one-page brief is accurate but dense. Add a 30-second “three
  things to remember” entry and define “taxable income response” without jargon.

### T-2 Budget Accountant — fund accounting unclear

- **P1:** The fifteen-track table shows `$0.000B` in one primary-spending column
  for OAS, PAY, REV, and NET. That can imply comparable spending-cut decisions.
  Use “not applicable; zero package credit” for separate, non-additive,
  financing, and endogenous rails.

### T-3 Source Custodian — needs primary-source route

- **P2:** The guides route to derived readers but do not consistently name the
  exact machine record or the Tax-Calculator version/data basis. Add a compact
  evidence footer shared by every guide.

### T-4 Public Goods Steward — public-purpose clear with revision

- **P2:** Add one short distinction among public goods, transfers,
  administration/enforcement, and debt service so “fifteen tracks” is not read
  as fifteen morally equivalent spending claims.

### T-5 Program Beneficiary — reliance risk unclear

- **P2:** The track guide should state that a reopening candidate still needs a
  shortfall/service-continuity rule where people or legal commitments rely on
  the program.

### T-6 Compliance Burden — simpler receipt path preferred

- **P2:** Add a direct answer distinguishing a clearer public receipt from a
  more complicated tax return; the explanation program must not imply that
  legibility requires seven new forms or employer reporting streams.

### T-7 Fiscal Sustainability — borrowing hidden

- **P2:** The one-page result mentions neither borrowing nor the fact that the
  one-year model target does not settle total deficits or debt. Add this next to
  the fiscal boundary.

### T-8 Reform Skeptic — reform claim overstates evidence

- **P2:** Name the rate-model basis (Tax-Calculator 6.5.1, bundled CPS tax-unit
  file, tax year 2026) and state that a preferred schedule depends on a chosen
  objective and assumptions. Avoid letting “preferred” sound technocratic and
  value-free.
- **P3:** Add a prompt asking who could legally change each rate, base, or
  appropriation rule.

## Required revisions

All guides must adopt the corrected non-comparable rail display and shared
evidence footer. The one-page and citizen guide must add the model basis,
borrowing boundary, and normative-choice explanation. The FAQ must distinguish
receipt clarity from return complexity. The educator guide must add the legal-
authority prompt. Round two follows only after these changes are visible.
