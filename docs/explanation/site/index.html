What a failed savings gate looks like

Two concrete candidates—and why the rate did not fall

Health imaging payment

A December 2024 CBO option carried a $0.4B FY2026 headline reduction—not admitted. Six gates remained: beneficiary cost sharing and distribution, provider access and location response, rural and safety-net protection, diagnostic quality and timeliness, implementation behavior, and a CY2026 overlap rescore.

Result: retained for new evidence; $0 entered the package.

Proportional active-force reduction

A CBO defense option carried a $15.0B FY2026 headline reduction—not admitted. Six gates remained: current force/program allocation, readiness stress, personnel/family/community transition, procurement and industrial-base timing, allied posture, and current-law legal/accounting rescore.

Result: strategy retained for review; $0 entered the package.

Authority and limits

Taxlane is repository-ready and externally blocked. It is not enacted law, personal tax or legal advice, an official score, formal proof of annual or long-run balance, a complete household-incidence analysis, or authorization to publish, deploy, submit, announce, contact government, or claim endorsement.

Repository-local explanation surface. No remote assets, analytics, tracking, forms, deployment configuration, or public release.
