The Taxlane result

Taxlane asks what federal services cost, what evidence supports changing those costs, and what ordinary-income model schedule follows when unsupported savings are excluded.

The conclusion

Across 15 tracks, Taxlane admitted $0.000B in FY2026 primary spending reduction and preserved a scoped $813.727B remaining ordinary-income model target. The preferred internal analytical marginal schedule is 21/23/33/35/43/46/48. [NUM-01, NUM-03, NUM-04, NUM-08]

Why zero matters

Zero admission means the tested candidates did not clear their evidence gates. It does not mean all programs are efficient, no reform could work, or public services have no value. Ten tracks reached a reviewed-zero conclusion; the other five have deliberately different accounting types.
