What should rates be when spending claims must prove themselves first?

Taxlane connects public services, spending evidence, guarded accounting, and an internal income-tax model. It preserves useful reforms without turning unproved benefits, efficiencies, or peer-country gaps into fictional savings.

Why zero is a result

Zero admission does not say every program is efficient or every reform is wrong. It says the current evidence supports a named trigger, an active monitor, or a downstream calculation instead of a fiscal number. That firewall prevents lower rates on paper from being financed by savings that have not yet been observed, attributed, netted, and reviewed.

What the full program taught us

Scale is not waste

A large budget, backlog, utilization rate, or portfolio share identifies a question—not an admissible reduction.

Service has independent value

A faster claim, safer corridor, or stronger supply chain can matter even when no cash saving is proved.

Full cost travels with the claim

Implementation, transition, maintenance, ownership, timing, employer and filing burden, administration, distribution, overlap, and downside stay visible.

Peers are diagnostics

Country comparisons require normalized definitions and institutions; they are not automatic targets.

Order prevents double counting

Primary effects come first, PAY remains owner-attributed, NET follows debt, and REV finances the remainder.

Budgets reopen

Observed outcomes, prices, implementation, shocks, and changing needs can revise a lane without rewriting history.

Owner evidence→Held pack→Taxlane review→Primary → PAY → NET → REV→Observe and reopen

Authority, fungibility, and limits

General-revenue dollars are fungible: this model does not trace a taxpayer's dollars to a program or earmark receipts. Congress and enacted law control tax rates, appropriations, benefits, and program authority. The $813.727B target is a scoped tax-year-2026 analytical financing target—not the whole deficit, a long-term debt path, or a rule requiring service cuts if modeled receipts fall short. Shortfalls, beneficiary reliance, borrowing, debt service, compliance burden, and program changes require separate review. This repository is not enacted law, personal advice, an official score, or authorization to publish, deploy, submit, or seek endorsement.

Local review surface only. No analytics, remote assets, forms, tracking, or deployment configuration.
