One accounting spine

  1. Define the candidate and correct fiscal object.
  2. Check current sources, net costs, timing, and overlap.
  3. Test beneficiary, service-continuity, distribution, implementation, and compliance boundaries.
  4. Admit an effect only when the gates close; otherwise record a typed zero or separate rail.
  5. Let program owners book supported PAY effects; do not add PAY again.
  6. Recompute NET only from an admitted annual debt path.
  7. Send the remaining scoped target to REV and test behavior and stress alternatives.

PAY–NET–REV

PAY measures

Gross errors are not automatically cash. Owners need net effects after appeals, burden, implementation, distribution, and overlap.

NET follows debt

No interest effect is claimed until an admitted annual primary path changes debt.

REV finances the remainder

The internal model tests a scoped target and alternatives; it does not enact law.

Revision, not automatic adjustment

New enacted text, scores, owner evidence, annual debt paths, inputs, or a changed policy objective can reopen the relevant gate. Reopening triggers another evidence and review cycle; it does not automatically alter a legal rate.
