Canonical frozen frontier

Fifteen lanes, fifteen explicit dispositions

Every lane has one candidate or downstream function, one terminal class, one owner route, and one reopening event. A trigger is not an admission; a monitor is not an effect; NET and REV cannot originate primary savings.

Versioning rule

Calendar passage alone does not reopen a lane. Qualifying owner evidence creates a successor version for that lane and requires portfolio revalidation.

Every displayed admitted FY2026 primary reduction is $0.000B.
