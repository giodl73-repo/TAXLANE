Glossary and frequently asked questions

Terms

Admitted reduction
An effect allowed into the package after its gates close.
Named trigger
A specific owner publication or observation that can reopen one lane; it is not an admitted effect.
Active monitor
An implemented or time-bounded candidate awaiting a complete qualifying outcome cohort; calendar passage alone is insufficient.
Held pack
A deterministic domain-evidence handoff that preserves sources, costs, floors, readiness, overlap, and authority while denying fiscal admission.
Reviewed zero
A completed conclusion that current evidence supports no admitted amount.
Primary
Excluding net interest.
Marginal rate
The rate applied only to income within a bracket, not to all income.
Cash proxy
A first-year model output, not an official score or formal balance.
Endogenous
Produced by upstream changes; NET follows an admitted debt path.

FAQ

Does zero mean no reform is possible?

No. It means the tested candidates did not clear the current evidence gates. New evidence can reopen review.

Are these the rates a person would pay on all income?

No. They are model marginal bracket percentages. Effective rates depend on the full tax calculation.

Is $813.727B the whole federal deficit?

No. It is a scoped remaining ordinary-income model target, not total receipts, outlays, or formal balance.

Do payment errors equal savings?

No. PAY is non-additive; owners need net evidence including appeals, burden, implementation, distribution, and overlap.

Does repository readiness authorize publication?

No. Deployment, distribution, outreach, official requests, and endorsement claims remain prohibited without new explicit owner authorization.
