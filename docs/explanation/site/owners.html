Domain evidence before fiscal admission

The Rust 2.0 evidence family

A public account becomes meaningful when money can be traced to service, capacity, full cost, rights and safety floors, distribution, delivery, and observed adaptation. Domain owners build that evidence. Taxlane keeps exclusive authority over fiscal admission and rate recomputation.

Existing owner families

Transportation

ROUTE, GAUGE, TARMAC, and HARBOR preserve mode-specific service, ownership, lifecycle, access, reliability, and price evidence.

Environment, hazards, and production

The energy, water, ecology, weather, hazard, and production owners supply system pressure, capacity, resilience, and service-floor evidence.

Fiscal controls

Taxlane owns PAY attribution, NET endogeneity, REV recomputation, overlap control, and the admission firewall.

What compatibility means

Four owners already emit the same fourteen-section taxlane.lane-evidence-pack-candidate.v1 vocabulary. ANCHOR and BASTION retain older held shapes until a reopening-capable producer-consumer path justifies an adapter. Shared fields demonstrate interoperability; they do not admit savings or authorize another framework.

Every owner must preserve service continuity, reliance, safety, rights, distribution, delivery capacity, administrative and employer burden, and an explicit null path. A funding shortfall never becomes an automatic benefit or service cut.

How to read VERDICT

VERDICT scores demonstrated repository capability—not policy merit, program performance, funding need, savings, or fiscal admission. Applicable service, safety, rights, equity, distribution, and authority floors override every total.
