What “marginal” means

Only income within a bracket is taxed at that bracket's model percentage. The highest listed rate does not apply to all income and is not an average or effective rate.

What was tested

Tax-Calculator 6.5.1, bundled CPS tax-unit input, tax year 2026; 14 schedules across three taxable-income response cases, or 42 behavior cases. The central schedule's first-year cash proxy is $819.220B; its administration ceiling is $0.077B and reported model gap is $5.416B. These are model quantities, not an official score.
