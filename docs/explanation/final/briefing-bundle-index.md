## Canonical result

Taxlane examined 15 tracks, admitted **$0.000B** in FY2026 primary spending
reduction, preserved a scoped **$813.727B** remaining ordinary-income model
target, and selected **21/23/33/35/43/46/48** as its preferred internal
analytical marginal bracket schedule. Zero admission means the tested candidates
did not clear current evidence gates; it is not a claim that reform is
impossible or public purposes have no value.

This is not enacted law, personal tax advice, an official score, formal proof
of balance, or authority to publish, deploy, submit, announce, or seek
endorsement.
