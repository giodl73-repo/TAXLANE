# EXPL-A terminology and visual grammar

## Terms

| Term | Required meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| admitted | passed applicable evidence, accounting, implementation, interaction, distribution, and outcome gates for the stated use | “good,” “approved,” or “enacted” |
| zero admission | no amount from the tested candidate may enter the fiscal package under current evidence | “no opportunity exists” |
| track | one of Taxlane's fifteen analytical fiscal lanes | “department” when the track spans agencies or financing rails |
| revenue target | amount left on Taxlane's frozen FY2026 ordinary-income rate-model rail after admitted package effects | total receipts/outlays, trust-fund requirement, tax liability, or official revenue requirement |
| bracket rate | marginal statutory-style model rate applied to income within a bracket | effective rate or share of all income paid |
| central case | Taxlane's selected baseline behavioral case | forecast or certainty |
| contingency | tested schedule robust to all three behavior cases without added macro stress | recommendation equal in rank to the central case |
| stress ceiling | deliberately adverse internal sensitivity boundary | forecast, official score, or default policy |
| primary spending | non-interest outlay effect on the defined fiscal rail | total government cost without scope label |
| PAY | non-additive payment-integrity measurement overlay | a stand-alone savings account |
| NET | interest result derived from admitted upstream debt paths | a direct spending cut |
| OAS | dedicated Social Security solvency rail | ordinary-income-tax package savings |

## Visual semantics

- **Navy:** sourced or reconciled context.
- **Teal:** admitted model input or completed internal result.
- **Amber:** uncertainty, contingency, unresolved gate, or conditional output.
- **Red outline, never solid fill:** prohibited or blocked claim.
- **Gray:** context that is not additive or not selected.

Color is always redundant with text, icon, pattern, or position. No chart may
require color vision to distinguish status.

## Standard marks

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ Admitted | passed for the stated typed use |
| ○ Reviewed zero | completed review, no amount admitted |
| ◇ Conditional | typed output survives with conditions |
| ∥ Separate rail | must not be added to the ordinary package |
| ↻ Endogenous | recompute only after upstream paths change |
| ! Blocked | claim or transition is not supported |

## Required visual patterns

1. **Result card:** target, zero admitted spending, preferred central schedule,
   and caveat block in one reading sequence.
2. **Fifteen-track matrix:** one row per track with disposition, fiscal effect,
   and reopening trigger; never rank tracks by political desirability.
3. **Rate ladder:** central, contingency, and stress tiers vertically ordered;
   never merge them into a single range.
4. **Evidence gate:** show headline context entering the gate and admitted amount
   leaving it, including every unresolved gate count.
5. **PAY–NET–REV flow:** PAY as non-additive annotation, NET as feedback, and REV
   as remaining financing—not three additive boxes.

## Accessibility and copy rules

- Use semantic headings, real tables, text alternatives, visible focus, and
  keyboard-operable disclosure controls.
- Pair every abbreviation with its full name on first use.
- Define marginal bracket rate before displaying a schedule.
- Do not use “your rate,” “your bill,” or “what you owe.”
- Keep the canonical caveat visible without requiring hover, animation, or a
  download.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences; motion may explain sequence but never carry
  unique evidence.

## Required marginal-rate teaching example

Every citizen-facing schedule explains that a marginal rate applies only within
its bracket. It also states that filing status, thresholds, deductions, credits,
payroll taxes, withholding, and refunds are needed before anyone's liability or
effective rate could be calculated.
