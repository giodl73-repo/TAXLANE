# Canonical Taxlane result statement

Status: EXPL-A working source. This text is the factual and narrative source for
all later guides, papers, presentations, and local HTML.

## One sentence

Taxlane completed an internal analysis across fifteen federal fiscal tracks,
admitted no FY2026 primary spending reduction from the candidates it tested,
and therefore retained an $813.727 billion FY2026 model revenue target and seven
preferred marginal model bracket rates: 21/23/33/35/43/46/48 percent. A marginal
bracket rate applies only to income within that bracket; this schedule is not
one person's effective rate or tax bill.

## One paragraph

Taxlane asked what spending changes could responsibly reduce the revenue needed
to meet its frozen FY2026 target. Every candidate had to pass source, current-
law, implementation, accounting, distribution, interaction, and outcome-floor
gates before its savings could enter the package. Ten tracks closed at reviewed
zero admission; TRN retained a conditional cost note, OAS stayed on its
dedicated-solvency rail, PAY stayed non-additive, REV retained an internal
planning rate card, and NET stayed endogenous. With zero admitted primary
spending reduction, the revenue target remained $813.727 billion. Taxlane's
fourteen-candidate, forty-two-behavior-case rate analysis selected
21/23/33/35/43/46/48 as its preferred central schedule. This is a model result
and recommendation under frozen assumptions—not current law, an official
score, a personal tax calculation, or proof that a budget is balanced.

The $813.727 billion amount is the remaining financing target on Taxlane's
frozen FY2026 ordinary-income rate-model rail. It is not total federal receipts,
total outlays, a trust-fund requirement, an official federal funding target, or
a literal allocation of one taxpayer's dollars. Social Security's dedicated
payroll-financing analysis remains separate.

## What the result means

- All fifteen tracks reached a reviewed internal disposition.
- A zero-admission disposition is a completed decision: available evidence did
  not justify counting the tested headline amount as savings.
- Excluding unsupported savings kept the revenue target from falling.
- The preferred schedule is the smallest tested one-decimal uniform uplift that
  cleared Taxlane's central case after its administration ceiling.
- Every track has a condition that can reopen analysis if evidence or policy
  assumptions materially change.
- The tracks make distinct public purposes and financing relationships visible
  without assuming every service should use the same funding mode. General
  revenue preserves flexibility; dedicated finance can make particular
  commitments visible, and Taxlane does not collapse one into the other.

## What the result does not mean

- It does not say that spending reform is impossible.
- It does not say every existing program is efficient or correctly designed.
- It does not trace an individual's tax dollars literally to named programs.
- It does not replace statutory tax brackets or calculate anyone's liability.
- It does not constitute an official CBO, JCT, Treasury, OMB, or agency score.
- It does not prove formal budget balance, enact a policy, or authorize a public
  release.
- It does not prove long-run solvency or supply a ten-year score from a one-year
  FY2026 result.
- It does not establish that current service levels are optimal; outcome gates
  prevent savings from being counted before access, quality, reliance, and
  distribution effects are understood.
- It does not fully model taxpayer, employer, preparer, avoidance, enforcement,
  or transition burdens.
- Taxlane cannot change tax law, appropriations, tax bases, program eligibility,
  or agency implementation rules.

## Canonical caveat block

> Taxlane's schedule is an internal analytical recommendation under frozen
> FY2026 assumptions. Bracket rates are not one person's effective rate, and
> the analysis does not calculate individual liability. No FY2026 primary
> spending reduction was admitted because the tested candidates did not clear
> all applicable evidence gates. The result is not enacted law, an official
> score, personal tax advice, a ten-year or long-run solvency result, formal
> balance certification, or authorization for external release. Taxpayer,
> employer, preparer, enforcement, avoidance, and transition burdens remain
> limitations, and Taxlane has no authority to change law or appropriations.

## Marginal-rate example

If a hypothetical bracket began above a threshold, its rate would apply only to
taxable income above that threshold—not retroactively to every dollar earned.
Taxlane's schedule does not provide the thresholds, deductions, credits,
filing status, payroll taxes, withholding, refunds, or other information needed
to calculate an individual's liability or effective rate.
