# Taxlane explanation glossary

| Term | Plain-language definition |
|---|---|
| admitted | passed the evidence and accounting gates for the stated use; not the same as enacted or endorsed |
| appropriation | legal authority for federal agencies to incur obligations and make payments within stated limits; not identical to outlays |
| behavior case | a tested assumption about how taxable income responds to a rate change; not a forecast |
| borrowing | financing obtained when current receipts do not cover outlays and other cash needs; not a tax receipt |
| bracket | a range of taxable income to which a marginal rate applies |
| budget authority | authority provided by law to incur obligations; not necessarily cash paid in the same year |
| central case | Taxlane's selected baseline behavioral case; not certainty |
| compliance burden | time, money, complexity, reporting, enforcement, and transition costs borne by taxpayers, employers, preparers, and administrators |
| contingency | an alternative tested schedule designed to survive a wider behavior range without added macro stress |
| dedicated finance | receipts legally associated with a stated account or purpose, such as payroll-tax trust-fund finance; still subject to its governing law |
| deficit | outlays exceeding receipts over a period under a defined budget measure |
| effective tax rate | total tax divided by a defined income measure; different from a marginal bracket rate |
| endogenous | calculated from other model results rather than set directly; net interest is endogenous here |
| evidence gate | a requirement that must pass before a claim or amount is admitted |
| fiscal year | the federal accounting year; Taxlane labels it `FY` to distinguish it from calendar and tax years |
| fungibility | the practical ability of money and budget choices to substitute across uses; a receipt display does not literally tag each dollar |
| general revenue | receipts available to finance general federal activity subject to law and appropriations, rather than one dedicated program account |
| headline amount | a reported estimate retained as context until its scope and gates support the intended use |
| marginal rate | the rate applied to the next dollar within a taxable-income bracket, not the rate on all income |
| model result | an output conditional on stated data, rules, and assumptions |
| net interest | federal interest outlays net of defined interest receipts; Taxlane derives changes from upstream debt paths |
| non-additive | must not be summed with package amounts because it overlaps, measures another object, or lacks owner-attributed fiscal effect |
| official score | a result issued through an authorized official scoring process; Taxlane's result is not one |
| outlay | a federal payment or disbursement recorded under budget rules; different from budget authority and obligations |
| outcome floor | minimum service, access, quality, safety, adequacy, or other condition required before a lower-cost scenario is admitted |
| PAY | Taxlane's payment-integrity track, treated as a non-additive measurement overlay |
| primary spending | non-interest spending within the defined fiscal scope |
| recommendation | Taxlane's normative selection among modeled alternatives, not a sourced fact or law |
| revenue target | amount left on Taxlane's frozen FY2026 ordinary-income model rail after admitted effects; not total federal receipts or official need |
| reviewed zero admission | completed review whose supported admitted amount is zero under current evidence |
| stress ceiling | deliberately adverse sensitivity boundary; not a forecast or baseline recommendation |
| tax liability | tax legally owed after applying the full law to a taxpayer's facts; not calculated by Taxlane's schedule alone |
| track | a Taxlane analytical fiscal lane; not necessarily one department or account |
| trust fund | a federal accounting structure with specified receipts and outlays under law; its balances are not private escrow accounts |
| withholding | tax paid during the year through amounts withheld from pay or other payments; not necessarily final liability |

This glossary is educational and does not provide tax, legal, or accounting
advice.
