## How to read the table

- The final numeric column is an admitted package effect, not a statement of the
  track's total budget or social value.
- OAS, PAY, and NET are deliberately prevented from becoming interchangeable
  additive “savings.”
- A conditional cost note can be a valid typed output without becoming savings.
- Reopening does not guarantee admission; it restarts the relevant gates.
- A reopened candidate still needs a shortfall and service-continuity rule where
  beneficiaries or legal commitments rely on the program.
- The track labels do not map one-to-one to departments or literal taxpayer-
  dollar destinations.
