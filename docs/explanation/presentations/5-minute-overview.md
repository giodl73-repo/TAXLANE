# What question did Taxlane ask?

What federal services cost, what evidence supports changing those costs, and
what ordinary-income rate schedule follows when unsupported savings are
excluded. [CLM-01]

Speaker note: This is an analytical framework, not an official budget or a
proposal authorized for public release.

---

# The result

- 15 tracks examined. [NUM-01]
- 10 reviewed zero-admission tracks. [NUM-02]
- **$0.000B** admitted FY2026 primary spending reduction. [NUM-03]
- **$813.727B** frozen remaining ordinary-income model target. [NUM-04]

Speaker note: Zero means the tested candidates did not clear their gates. It
does not say no reform could ever work or that every program is efficient.

# One accounting spine

**PAY measures; owners book. NET follows debt. REV finances the remainder.**

No PAY amount may be double-counted as owner-program savings. No NET effect is
claimed without an admitted upstream annual debt path. [CLM-05]

Speaker note: The $813.727B target is a scoped model target, not total federal
receipts, total outlays, or a trust-fund requirement.

# What would change the result?

New enacted policy, current scores, owner-attributed net effects, closed
beneficiary and service-continuity gates, changed model inputs, or a changed
policy objective can reopen a track or rate rail. [CLM-08]

Speaker note: Reopening restarts review; it does not guarantee admission.
