# Civic Denominator Values

## Decision Supported

TAXLANE can now support broad civic burden views on a sourced calendar-year
basis: per resident and per household. These denominators are useful for public
goods and financing-cost lanes such as defense and net interest.

## CY2025 Census Denominators

| Denominator | CY2025 value | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| U.S. resident population, July 1 estimate | 341,784,857 | `SRC-CENSUS-POP-EST-2025`, NST-EST2025-POP |
| U.S. households | 134.790M | `SRC-CENSUS-HH1-2025`, HH-1 |

## Display Rule

Use resident population for broad civic cost only. It does not mean every
resident paid the same tax, and it does not turn a public-good lane into a
personal benefit claim.

Use households only when the visible claim says "per household." Do not use
households as a substitute for taxpayers, workers, beneficiaries, or enrollees.

## Basis Warning

These are calendar-year Census denominators. They can be used for illustrative
per-resident or per-household civic views, but they do not convert FY2025 OMB
outlays into an individual liability calculation.
