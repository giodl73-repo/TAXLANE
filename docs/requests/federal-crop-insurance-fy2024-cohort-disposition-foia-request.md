## Request

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, I request access to existing records
maintained by the USDA Risk Management Agency or Federal Crop Insurance
Corporation concerning the 326 policies selected from Reinsurance Year 2022 for
the FY2024 payment-integrity review announced in COM-23-001.

Please search records dated April 1, 2023 through the date the search is
performed in locations
reasonably likely to contain responsive records, including the Compliance
Activities and Results System case-policy document libraries, Regional
Compliance Office review and disposition records, Deputy Administrator for
Compliance records, related OCFO submission or reconciliation records, and any
linked FCIC settlement or accounts-receivable records reasonably likely to
contain responsive dispositions.

I request existing cohort-level exports, ledgers, reports, query outputs, data
dictionaries, and reconciliations that show, to the extent recorded:

- a stable deidentified case key, only if one is already present and safely
  releasable without creating a pseudonym or identifying a producer or AIP;
- review disposition and date;
- Initial and Final Finding status, date, and amount by payment class;
- final administrative determination request, outcome, and date;
- post-review corrected amounts;
- debt establishment status, date, and amount;
- waiver, reduction, or delay status and amount;
- setoff and cash-receipt status, dates, and amounts;
- compromise, write-off, or uncollectible status, dates, and amounts;
- any existing reconciliation to the reported 2.43-percent or $579.36-million
  FY2024 estimate, without calculating a new recovery rate.

This request seeks existing records only and does not ask the agency to conduct
research, answer questions, perform new analysis, or create a new record.

## Privacy, segregability, and format

Please exclude producer or policyholder names, addresses, contact information,
taxpayer identifiers, policy and claim numbers, farm or geospatial identifiers,
AIP identities, crops, exact locations, and identifying free text. I will accept an existing deidentified row-level
record or an existing statistical or aggregate record that does not permit
identification of the supplier, consistent with 7 U.S.C. 1502(c).

If any portion is exempt, please release all reasonably segregable nonexempt
portions and identify the applicable exemption. Please provide data in CSV,
XLSX, JSON, or another readily reproducible native electronic format, with
supporting documents as searchable PDF or in native format. Rolling production
is welcome.
