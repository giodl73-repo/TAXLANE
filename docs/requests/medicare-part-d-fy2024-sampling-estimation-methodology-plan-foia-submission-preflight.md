# Medicare Part D FY2024 S&EMP FOIA Submission Preflight

Status: **blocked; owner completion and explicit submission authorization required**

This is an internal checklist. No request, fee commitment, appeal, or other
external contact is authorized. Owner authorization is necessary but
insufficient; every applicable item must be complete before the exact outbound
action can be separately authorized.

## Owner, requester, and channel

- [ ] Requester name is complete.
- [ ] Full mailing address is complete.
- [ ] Phone and email are complete.
- [ ] Owner has reviewed the exact final scope and date boundary.
- [ ] Owner explicitly authorizes the exact external submission.
- [ ] Exactly one CMS channel is selected and its destination is verified.
- [ ] No second channel or duplicate submission will be used.

## Fees and expedition

- [ ] Truthful requester fee category is confirmed from requester facts.
- [ ] Exact fee ceiling is approved.
- [ ] Fee-waiver choice is recorded; any request is supported by owner-approved
  facts rather than assumed eligibility.
- [ ] Expedited-processing choice is recorded; any request includes a certified,
  owner-approved factual basis.
- [ ] No fee is paid or committed without separate authorization.

## Scope, privacy, and format

- [ ] Scope remains existing final CY2022/FY2024 Part D S&EMP, OMB checklist,
  incorporated attachments, operative version history, and approval or
  transmittal records.
- [ ] The request asks no questions, calculations, research, reconstruction,
  new analysis, or new record creation.
- [ ] Beneficiary, PDE, provider, prescriber, pharmacy, employee, and contractor
  identifiers and person-level claims remain excluded.
- [ ] Credentials, security controls, source extracts, raw inputs, executable
  code, and production access remain excluded.
- [ ] Commercial information may be lawfully redacted without predicting an
  exemption or release outcome.
- [ ] The same-cohort debt, appeal, collection, recovery, and write-off track
  remains separate.
- [ ] Native electronic format, searchable PDF fallback, rolling release, and
  reasonably segregable nonexempt portions remain accepted.
- [ ] Public-library duplicate check is complete; stable URLs may replace exact
  duplicate production.

## Freeze and custody

- [ ] Every placeholder is replaced and the exact request text is frozen.
- [ ] The outbound body and every attachment are saved locally.
- [ ] SHA-256 is computed and recorded for the exact outbound body and each
  attachment.
- [ ] Approved channel, destination, request version, and checksum agree.
- [ ] Submission timestamp and confirmation-capture fields are ready.
- [ ] A blank intake record exists at `draft_not_submitted` with no events.
- [ ] If separately authorized and actually transmitted, append one supported
  `submission_transmitted` event; do not rewrite the blank history.

## Stop conditions

Stop if any applicable item is incomplete, facts conflict, a second channel is
proposed, scope changes after hashing, destination cannot be verified, or the
owner has not separately authorized the exact outbound action. Completing this
checklist does not itself authorize or perform submission.
