# DEF proportional-force current-law dependency audit

The selected proportional active-force reduction is not current law. The
FY2026 NDAA and appropriations were enacted after CBO's December 2024 option,
and the NDAA increased total authorized active-component end strength by 26,100
from FY2025 rather than beginning the candidate's long-run reduction.

The new CBO analysis sharpens the dependency: the FY2026 request totaled $961
billion, 2025 reconciliation supplied $156 billion available through 2029, and
no 2026 FYDP was published. That makes an old proportional strategy less—not
more—executable without a unit, program, readiness, transition, allied, and
industrial-base allocation tied to the enacted accounts.

DEF therefore closes the current-law dependency audit at two evidence-ready
gates and five design, owner-analysis, or rescore gates. The $15 billion FY2026
and $959 billion vintage outlay figures remain unadmitted. DEF moves to named
policy and publication triggers; OAS becomes the next dependency track.
