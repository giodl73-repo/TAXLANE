# REV Level-1 individual-income legal/economic base perimeter

REV-L1-02 is complete at a definition-only boundary. Official U.S. Code
custody establishes that the individual tax is imposed on taxable income, with
gross income, adjusted gross income, and taxable income defined as distinct
steps. The 2025 Form 1040 instructions then keep taxable income and tax
calculation separate from credits, withholding, estimated payments, refunds,
and amounts owed.

That separation matters. IRS SOI taxable income is useful legal-base context,
but it is neither a complete fiscal-year receipt base nor an economic-incidence
base. Tax after credits is not the same quantity as Treasury cash receipts.
The economic base is therefore definition-only until behavior and incidence
work is sourced and quantified.

No rate is calculated. REV-L1-03 owns the timing bridge and must either produce
a source-supported cohort reconciliation or explicitly preserve the mismatch.
