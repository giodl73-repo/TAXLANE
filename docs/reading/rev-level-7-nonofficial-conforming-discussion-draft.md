# REV Level 7 nonofficial conforming discussion draft

> **Status: nonofficial discussion draft.** This text was not prepared or
> approved by Legislative Counsel. It is not an introduced bill, an official
> proposal, enacted law, an official score, or a certified rate schedule.

## A BILL

To modify the ordinary individual income-tax rates while preserving
current-law filing-status bracket thresholds and inflation adjustments.

### SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Ordinary Individual Rate Schedule Act”.

### SEC. 2. MODIFICATION OF ORDINARY INDIVIDUAL INCOME-TAX RATES.

**(a) In general.—**Section 1(j) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is
amended by adding at the end the following new paragraph:

> **“(7) MODIFIED ORDINARY INDIVIDUAL RATES FOR TAXABLE YEARS BEGINNING AFTER
> 2025.—**
>
> **“(A) IN GENERAL.—**In prescribing tables under paragraph (3)(B) with
> respect to the tables described in subparagraphs (A), (B), (C), and (D) of
> paragraph (2), the Secretary shall make the following substitutions:
>
> 1. ‘21 percent’ for ‘10 percent’;
> 2. ‘23 percent’ for ‘12 percent’;
> 3. ‘33 percent’ for ‘22 percent’;
> 4. ‘35 percent’ for ‘24 percent’;
> 5. ‘43 percent’ for ‘32 percent’;
> 6. ‘46 percent’ for ‘35 percent’; and
> 7. ‘48 percent’ for ‘37 percent’.
>
> **“(B) PRESERVATION OF BRACKET THRESHOLDS.—**The minimum and maximum dollar
> amounts of every rate bracket, and every adjustment to those amounts under
> paragraph (3), shall be determined without regard to this paragraph. The
> amount of tax shown for each bracket shall be recomputed by applying the
> rates specified in subparagraph (A) through the existing marginal bracket
> structure.
>
> **“(C) ESTATES AND TRUSTS.—**This paragraph shall not apply to the table
> described in paragraph (2)(E).
>
> **“(D) NO OTHER INFERENCE.—**Except for the rate substitutions expressly
> provided by subparagraph (A), nothing in this paragraph shall be construed
> to modify any rate, threshold, deduction, credit, exclusion, tax base,
> filing-status rule, or inflation adjustment under this title.”.

**(b) Section 15.—**Section 15 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 shall not
apply to a change in a rate of tax made by this section.

**(c) Effective date.—**The amendments made by this section shall apply to
taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.

### SEC. 3. ADMINISTRATION AND TRANSITION.

**(a) Withholding guidance.—**The Secretary of the Treasury (or the
Secretary’s delegate) shall revise wage and pension withholding methods as
soon as practicable. Employers and payors may use previously published 2026
methods until the mandatory effective date stated in that guidance.

**(b) Estimated-tax and reasonable-cause relief.—**The Secretary shall provide
appropriate relief from additions to tax or penalties when an underpayment is
attributable solely to reasonable reliance, before implementing guidance, on
the individual rate schedule previously published for taxable year 2026.

**(c) Returns and systems.—**The Secretary shall update forms, instructions,
electronic-filing specifications, and other implementation materials needed
to administer the amendments.

**(d) No appropriation.—**Nothing in this Act shall be construed to provide or
deem an appropriation.

## Legislative Counsel review points

Legislative Counsel should determine whether replacing all four tables is
preferable to adding paragraph (7), whether any cross-reference beyond
§1(j)(2)(F) needs a conforming amendment, whether the §15 clause is necessary,
and the preferred statutory placement of transition relief and any required
budget-act or administrative language.

The draft follows the current official structure in which §1(j)(2)(A)–(D)
contain individual filing-status tables, §1(j)(2)(E) separately governs
estates and trusts, and §1(j)(3)(B) prescribes indexed later-year tables.
