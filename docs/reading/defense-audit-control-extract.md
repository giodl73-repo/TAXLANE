## Boundary

These rows are control probes only. They are not waste findings, fraud findings,
readiness findings, or savings estimates. The next extract must attach
component ownership, corrective-action status, repeat-finding age, closure
targets, and mission/readiness floor evidence before any public performance or
cost-down claim.
