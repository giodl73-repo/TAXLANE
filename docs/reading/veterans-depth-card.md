Veterans Benefits and Services totaled **$377.163 billion**, or **5.38% of all

The previously displayed 4.98% figure was a valid but incomplete subtotal of
income security plus medical care. The complete function adds education and
rehabilitation, housing, and other veterans services.

## Service Evidence Available Now

The VA detailed claims page supplied two observed June 30, 2026 probes:

- 68,207 compensation-and-pension rating-bundle claims in backlog; and
- 84.91% claim-based quality.

These are dynamic-page probes, not locked time series and not findings of poor
performance. Backlog and quality must be paired with inventory, decision time,
accuracy method, appeals, remands/reversals, staffing, and access.

## What Is Not Yet Defensible

- There is no matched international "expected veterans spending" percentage.
- A country comparison cannot ignore eligibility, conflict history, disability
  design, integrated health care, and government scope.
- VA program-specific improper-payment estimates are not a whole-lane rate.
- Neither backlog nor improper payments equal fraud or recoverable savings.
