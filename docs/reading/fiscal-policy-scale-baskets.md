The central finding is blunt: **the fiscal gap is package-sized**. It cannot be
credibly closed with fraud recovery, administrative efficiency, or a handful
of small program eliminations.

## What “package-sized” means

TAXLANE's gradual paths require cumulative primary adjustments of about
**$2.74T**, **$5.23T**, or **$7.71T** from 2026 through 2036. For scale, CBO's
latest option compendium scores these individual choices over 2025–2034:

| CBO option | Ten-year deficit reduction |
|---|---:|
| 5% value-added tax | $2.18T–$3.38T |
| AGI surtax | $1.05T–$1.44T |
| Reduce employment-health tax subsidies | $521B–$965B |
| Greenhouse-gas emissions tax | $645B–$919B |
| Reduce the annual defense budget | $959B |
| Reduce Medicare Advantage benchmarks | $489B |

An arithmetic basket near the primary-balance scale, for example, combines
the high VAT variant, the defense option, Medicare Advantage benchmark reform,
full-rate taxation of foreign corporate income, and a two-point capital-gains
rate increase. Their published scores sum to about **$5.27T**, close in scale
to TAXLANE's **$5.23T** primary-balance path.

That is not a recommendation and not a valid combined score. It demonstrates
the magnitude of the choice: primary balance needs several of the largest
available levers, not a collection of painless savings.

## Why the basket is not a score

- CBO's option window is 2025–2034; TAXLANE's path is 2026–2036.
- The options were scored against an earlier baseline.
- Revenue bases, behavior, and spending provisions can overlap.
- Costs may shift to states, households, employers, or providers.
- Distribution, access, outcomes, phase-in, and implementation must be tested.

The next honest step is to choose candidate policy families and request or
construct an updated joint score. Until then, the baskets answer only one
question: **are we discussing changes remotely large enough to meet the debt
path?**

Source: CBO, *Options for Reducing the Deficit: 2025 to 2034* (updated October
2025). CBO presents options for analysis and makes no recommendations.
