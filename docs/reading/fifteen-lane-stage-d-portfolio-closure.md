# Fifteen-lane stage-D portfolio closure

All fifteen Taxlane tracks are now at or beyond bounded stage D:

`TRN, HLT, EDU, OAS, ISF, VET, AGR, DEF, DIS, JUS, SEE, INT, PAY, REV, NET`.

Each track has a named prefix, dependency-ordered evidence, a reviewed closure,
and explicit blocked outputs. PAY and REV remain non-additive overlays. NET
remains endogenous. Transportation is beyond D; the other fourteen are at D.

This is structural stage completion. It means each lane has an explicit
baseline/source boundary, accounting boundary, candidate-screen decision, and
financing/behavior/incidence/fairness interface. It does not mean every source
gap is closed or that every lane has a reform, floor result, complete financing
path, solver input, rate, savings estimate, public card, or full coverage.
