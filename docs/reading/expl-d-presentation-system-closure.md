and three fully local HTML review previews. Round one found one P1 and six P2
issues. The applied revisions teach marginal rates, bound the fiscal target and
horizon, preserve public-purpose and beneficiary rules, prevent PAY gross-error

material outside the repository. EXPL-E is ready.
