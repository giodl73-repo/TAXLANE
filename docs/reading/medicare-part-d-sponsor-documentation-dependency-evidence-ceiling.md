## Reconciliation

The captured Q4 2025 scorecard corrects a stale transcription. It reports the
FY2024 Part D overpayment estimate as $3,053 million at 3.16%, not 1.02%. The
scorecard classifies the full rounded amount as outside agency control because
of failure to access data or information needed. Its narrative identifies drug
discrepancies, drug-pricing discrepancies, and insufficient documentation—not
state data—as the underlying issues.

The exact annual workbook amount is $3,052.65 million. CMS's FY2024 findings
split the 3.16% overpayment rate into 2.70% for insufficient, invalid, or missing
documentation and 0.46% for drug and pricing discrepancies. The sources now
reconcile at their published precision.

## Decision

The inherited `state-data dependency treatment` label is not supported for Part
D. The evidence supports reframing it as `sponsor-data and documentation
dependency treatment`. The full field stays open pending same-period method text
showing how unavailable or corrected sponsor documentation flows through PDE
review, estimation, reconsideration, and final reporting. Part D therefore
remains two fields closed and six open.

## Claim firewall

“Outside agency control” is a publisher taxonomy, not a legal determination of
nonrecoverability. Insufficient documentation is not automatically fraud, waste,
identified debt, or collectible loss. Scorecard recovery language for specific
audit closeouts does not establish recovery of the full statistical estimate.
No score, public claim, or savings estimate is allowed.
