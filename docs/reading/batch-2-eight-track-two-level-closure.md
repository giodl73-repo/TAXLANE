# Batch 2 eight-track two-level closure

VET, EDU, ISF, AGR, DIS, JUS, SEE, and INT have completed both new advancement
levels. The evidence refresh changed several boundaries: H.R. 2137 remains
unenacted and increases FY2026 direct and discretionary outlays; 2025
reconciliation legislation changed the Pell, student-loan, agriculture, and
crop-insurance context; later flood research does not replace the 2018 NFIP
score; and a 2026 weatherization estimate uses a newer appropriation context.

None of those updates supplies a current, executable, floor-complete annual
savings path. The DOJ, weatherization, and international-affairs options also
remain broad allocation envelopes whose program, geographic, and outcome
effects are unresolved. All eight Level B reruns therefore produce zero
admission. Together with Batch 1, thirteen of fifteen tracks have now completed
the active two-level wave; TRN and REV are next.
