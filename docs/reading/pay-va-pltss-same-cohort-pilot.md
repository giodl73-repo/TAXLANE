# PAY VA PLTSS same-cohort pilot

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/pay_va_pltss_same_cohort_pilot.v1.draft.json`.

## Selection

VA Purchased Long Term Services and Supports (PLTSS) is selected as PAY's first
same-cohort lineage pilot. The selection is based on six already custodied
sources and a sharply defined evidence ceiling—not on a savings estimate.

The FY2024 statistical row reports $218.30 million of projected overpayments
for the October 2022–September 2023 sample. Later scorecard recovery rows use
different periods and bases. They cannot be subtracted, divided, or netted
against the estimate.

## Required lineage

One privacy-safe cohort key must follow a sampled payment through:

1. sampled payment and projection weight;
2. final payment classification;
3. bill or receivable establishment;
4. dispute, review, and appeal;
5. correction, reversal, waiver, compromise, write-off, or noncollectibility;
6. collection, refund, or offset transaction; and
7. certified recovered, credited, or realized cash.

Projected error, identified debt, collectible debt, collection, and certified
cash remain separate quantities. No person data are required in TAXLANE.

## Service and cost floors

The same cohort must also report eligible-payment timeliness, care and provider
continuity, notice and appeal access, false positives, correction time,
documentation burden, provider burden, subgroup distribution, implementation
cost, monitoring cost, appeal cost, and remediation cost.

Any future net realized benefit must use certified same-cohort cash, subtract
all implementation and remediation costs, and remain non-additive in PAY. The
owning VET program books any admitted primary effect once.
