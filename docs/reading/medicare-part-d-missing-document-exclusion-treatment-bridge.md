# Medicare Part D Missing-Document Exclusion Treatment Bridge

## Same-period component closure

The CY2022 submission guide establishes that documentation submitted by the
final deadline remains in the Part D IPM process even if incomplete or otherwise
problematic. This appears at printed page 5/PDF file page 8 and again at printed
page 33/PDF file page 36. The guide also requires a Missing Documentation Form
when a hardcopy or medication order cannot be obtained, while limiting that
form’s substitution role. Missing or invalid evidence can be replaced with
corrected evidence before the April 19, 2024 final deadline.

The CY2022 FAQ at printed and file page 1 states that a Missing Documentation
Form is not valid supporting evidence, so the PDE remains failed while the
defect is unresolved. Evidence located before the final deadline may still be
submitted. The FY2024 findings at printed and file page 2 then report a 2.70%
overpayment error category for insufficient documentation, defined there as
invalid or missing documentation.

Together, these sources close one narrow internal component: timely incomplete
or problematic documentation remains in review, unresolved missing evidence
retains failed status, and predeadline correction is permitted. They do not
close the full exclusion-rules field.

## Historical comparison boundary

The FY2020 findings report, based on CY2018 payments, states at printed page 2/
PDF index 1 that CMS excluded 27 of 4,526 sampled PDEs because requested
supporting documentation could not be obtained. That treatment is historical
comparison only and cannot be transferred to CY2022.

The FY2020 report separately notes COVID-era suspension of provider evidence
requests and a two-week deadline extension. It does not say COVID-19 caused the
27 exclusions, and this bridge makes no such inference.

## Remaining full-field gaps and firewall

The missing evidence is the complete CY2022 exclusion taxonomy and authority;
counts and reasons for excluded, ineligible, duplicate, replaced, late, or
nonresponsive units; distinctions among submission states; post-deadline,
adjustment, dispute, and appeal treatment; replacement and weighting mechanics;
effects on projection, variance, and confidence limits; and the reason for any
change from FY2020.

“Reviewed” does not mean every incomplete record becomes an error or enters the
estimator unchanged. “Remain fail” applies to the unresolved Missing
Documentation Form case and is curable before the deadline. The 2.70% aggregate
does not establish identical treatment for every defect.

One component closes internally, but no full field closes. Medicare Part D
remains three fields closed and five open. All scoring, public-claim, fraud,
waste, debt, recovery, and savings gates remain blocked.
