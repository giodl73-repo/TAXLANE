This is a forward-looking OECD model for a male worker entering employment at
age 22 in 2024, earning the national average wage throughout a full career to
the country-specific normal retirement age. It includes mandatory public and
private schemes and excludes voluntary schemes.

The values do not describe today's retirees, actual interrupted careers,
poverty, solvency, or current benefit spending. They are not combined with the
2022 SOCX spending panel into an efficiency score.
