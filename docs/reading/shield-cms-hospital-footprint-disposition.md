# SHIELD establishes the HLT hospital denominator, not healthcare adequacy

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's first current public-data result. The May 13, 2026
CMS Hospital General Information release contains 5,432 unique
Medicare-registered hospitals across 56 state and territory groups. It includes
4,498 facilities flagged for emergency services, 1,378 Critical Access
Hospitals, and 41 Rural Emergency Hospitals. Hospital type,
emergency-service, and geography partitions each reconcile to the same source
denominator.

That is a useful HLT source spine, but it is not a spending candidate or an
adequacy result. Facility presence does not establish travel reach, staffing,
service availability, wait time, affordability, quality, safety, outcomes,
equity, need, resilience, costs, or savings. The source categories also cannot
be treated as interchangeable units of capacity.

Taxlane therefore admits the official denominator as context and admits zero
candidate effect or fiscal savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining target
stays **$813.727B**, and both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
