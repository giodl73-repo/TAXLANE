## Why This Is Not OMB Plus State Plus Local

OMB reports $105.373B of FY2024 federal outlays for elementary, secondary, and
vocational education and $68.364B for FY2025. That is a federal public-purpose
classification. Census reports revenue received by in-scope school systems and
already separates its federal, state, and local sources.

The two FY2024 federal figures differ by $9.712B, but that difference is not a
funding gap or unexplained loss. Outlays and recipient revenue use different
recognition timing, program boundaries, pass-through treatment, and coverage.
Adding OMB outlays to Census revenue would double count federal support.

## What This Closes—and What It Does Not

This bridge closes the federal-versus-state/local scale problem for public K–12
school-system finance. It does not cover private education, all vocational
education, all public nonschool entities, or postsecondary education. The
[K–12 Outcome Baseline](k12-outcome-baseline.md) now attaches national public-
school learning and completion distributions, but its periods and populations
are not cohort-matched to this finance frame. This bridge does not supply an expected spending level.
Access, attendance, postschool outcomes, and a definition-
matched peer remain necessary before benchmarking cost or estimating any
addressable opportunity.
