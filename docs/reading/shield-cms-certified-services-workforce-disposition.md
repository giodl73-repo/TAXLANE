# SHIELD adds certified-service and workforce context—not current capacity proof

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's Q2 2026 CMS QIES Provider of Services baseline. The
source contains 13,566 hospital rows with unique CCNs. Exact identity matches
5,422 of the 5,432 hospitals in SHIELD's current footprint (99.82%). Fourteen
service fields are complete for 5,286 current hospitals (97.31%); the 136-row
residual is 112 Veterans Administration and 24 Department of Defense hospitals.

The service codes distinguish delivery by staff, under arrangement, both, or
not provided. Seven employed-workforce fields are present for every matched
CCN. SHIELD preserves source-recorded zeros and conspicuous maxima—including a
64,782.00 recorded-RN-FTE maximum—rather than silently cleaning them.

These facts materially deepen the HLT evidence spine, but certification does
not prove a service is open now, and recorded employed FTE is not a count of
unique workers or current shift coverage. Schedules, hours, throughput,
vacancies, contract labor, patient access, local need, quality, and adequacy
remain unproved.

Taxlane therefore admits the official identity, service-mode, and workforce
context but no candidate effect or savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining
target stays **$813.727B**, and both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
