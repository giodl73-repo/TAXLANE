# PAY Level-3 full-DMF causal-control spine

PAY now has a specific control candidate: Treasury Do Not Pay access to SSA's
full Death Master File. Treasury reported $113.5 million of calendar-2024
payments prevented, identified, or recovered and $4.6 million of cost, producing
a $108.9 million net benefit.

The causal boundary is stronger than a government-wide error estimate. Treasury
counted payments for which the full DMF was the sole death-data source and used
agency determinations that the payments were improper. The result is only
0.0585 percent of the FY2025 $186 billion improper-payment measurement universe
and 0.0218 percent of the assumed $500 billion annual package target.

The result still combines three different accounting events. Prevention may be
an avoided outlay, identification alone is not a budget effect, and recovery
depends on where the cash is deposited. The public evidence does not split those
events, allocate them to owning programs, or establish a recurring FY2026
effect. The separate $337 million three-year projection is not admitted.
