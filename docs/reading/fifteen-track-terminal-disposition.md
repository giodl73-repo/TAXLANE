# Fifteen-track terminal disposition

Taxlane's current internal fifteen-track program is finished.

“Finished” now has a precise meaning: every canonical track has a reviewed
terminal disposition under the evidence in custody, and no active work item is
left merely to make the track look more advanced. A zero-admission decision is
a completed result when the evidence does not support a fiscal claim.

The portfolio closes as follows:

- TRN retains one conditional cost note; REV retains the internal planning rate
  card.
- HLT, EDU, ISF, VET, AGR, DEF, DIS, JUS, SEE, and INT close at reviewed zero
  admission. INT's broad allocation envelope is not executable or
  floor-complete.
- OAS stays on its dedicated-solvency rail, PAY stays a non-additive measurement
  overlay, and NET stays endogenous to admitted upstream paths.

No FY2026 primary spending reduction is admitted. The remaining revenue target
therefore stays $813.727 billion, and the preferred Taxlane schedule remains
21/23/33/35/43/46/48. Each track has a specific evidence-triggered reopening
rule; absent one of those triggers, the active fifteen-track queue is empty.

This is internal analytical completion, not enactment, implementation, an
official score, formal balance certification, or a claim that all policy
uncertainty has disappeared.
