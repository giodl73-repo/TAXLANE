# NET Level-3 zero-input endogenous recomputation

The February 2026 CBO baseline places FY2026 debt held by the public at
$32,095.165 billion and net interest at $1,038.976 billion. Every upstream
primary-policy path remains unadmitted, so the only eligible endogenous NET
recomputation is the identity result: zero debt change and zero interest change.

Applying Treasury's 3.409 percent June 2026 average interest rate to the
illustrative $500 billion full-year debt reduction produces $17.045 billion.
That number is a mechanical carry sensitivity, not savings: it has no admitted
debt path, maturity schedule, fiscal-year timing, or iterative feedback model.
NET is never a direct cut.
