## Boundary

The primary-deficit proxy subtracts OMB Table 3.2 gross Treasury-interest
subfunction outlays from the FY2025 deficit gap. It is not a CBO primary-deficit
score, not a policy scenario, not a savings estimate, and not a recommendation
to cut any program. The next extract needs a primary-deficit series, debt held
by the public as percent of GDP, interest-rate assumptions, and policy-specific
revenue/spending assumptions.
