# Multi-Track D/B Catch-Up Bundle

This bundle advances the established tracks and adds three new bounded tracks:

- TRN-E closes its candidate-selection and solver gate without selecting a
  financing source or running the solver. TRN-F remains blocked.
- HLT-D, EDU-D, and OAS-D close legal/financing, behavior, incidence/fairness,
  interaction, and role-review packages. Their C stages admitted no reforms, so
  E stages and all numeric results remain blocked.
- ISF-A/B, VET-A/B, and AGR-A/B close bounded baseline/source and accounting
  stages. Their C stages may begin discovery, but no reform follows from this
  bundle.

The new lanes expose three cross-lane overlaps: nutrition between ISF and AGR,
veterans healthcare between VET and HLT, and crop-insurance payment integrity
between AGR and the payment-integrity overlay. CORE-L assigns one owner and
keeps each overlap non-additive until a complete sourced allocation exists.

These are structural closures, not solver runs, rates, fraud findings, savings,
department cuts, public cards, or balanced-budget claims.
