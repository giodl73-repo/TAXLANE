# Balanced-rate readiness gate

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/balanced_rate_readiness_gate.v1.draft.json`

This is a balanced-rate readiness gate, not a rate card, tax proposal, savings estimate, or balanced-budget claim.

Pulse 80 cannot calculate rates on this base because the target paths, assigned
bases, behavioral assumptions, distribution, macro feedback, endogenous net
interest, reserves, emergency rules, and zero unrounded deficit gap are not yet
available together.

The gate preserves two denominator definitions:

- all-receipt funding share = gross program cost / total funded federal cost;
- residual general-fund requirement share = residual general-fund need / total residual general-fund need.

A value calculated after subtracting dedicated receipts is not share of every tax dollar.

The FY2025 ledger remains a 17-row budget ledger totaling $7,011.105B, including commerce/housing credit and undistributed offsetting receipts as negative offset rows. Fifteen analytical lanes are not the same as the 17 budget rows.

No statutory rate, effective rate, target cost, gross savings, net savings, public rate card, or balanced-budget result is published by this gate.
