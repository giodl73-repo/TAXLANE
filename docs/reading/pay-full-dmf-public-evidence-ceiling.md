# PAY full-DMF public-evidence ceiling

The public-source pass is complete for the current PAY candidate. GAO reviewed
Treasury's calendar-2024 ROI study and reports that its benefit equals improper
payments prevented, identified, and recovered, less acquisition and
implementation costs. GAO does not publish the component amounts, counted
programs, fund dispositions, false-positive outcomes, appeals, or beneficiary
distribution.

Public Law 119-77 closes permanent authority and statutory safeguard design.
Treasury's current Do Not Pay data page confirms that the full DMF remains an
available data source and names correction routes, but it does not publish the
calendar-2024 cohort or its fiscal and beneficiary outcomes.

The evidence ceiling is therefore three passes and six owner-data gates. This
is a useful completion result: another generic web search should not be treated
as progress. Reopen on a published Treasury ROI study, successor annual
assessment, agency cohort table, scorekeeper budget effect, or audit of cohort
corrections and distribution.

No restricted or person data are needed for TAXLANE. Aggregate event, program,
fund, period, correction, appeal, and distribution rows would be sufficient.
Until then, the reported $108.9 million remains a control result rather than an
admitted annual spending reduction.
