# Deterministic Annual Update Simulator Contract

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/deterministic_annual_update_simulator_contract.v1.draft.json`.

This is a deterministic annual update simulator contract, not a simulator run,
optimization result, selected pilot lane, public rate card, statutory-rate
proposal, effective-rate publication, savings estimate, waste finding, fraud
finding, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, or
balanced-budget claim.

No pilot lane is selected and all simulator outputs remain null.

The simulator requires baseline, modernization, and stress paths. It is narrow
and deterministic; optimization is not allowed.

Net interest must be recomputed after every primary-balance change and cannot
be cut directly. OASDI, Medicare HI, transportation trust, general fund, and
reserves remain separate, with explicit interfund transfers.

Lower-rate recognition remains blocked unless access, quality, equity,
adequacy/resilience, delivery-feasibility, lane-specific floors, measured
productivity, transition cost, stress case, and score provenance pass.

All target-cost, assigned-base-rate, funding-share, fund-balance, net-interest,
debt, reserve, deficit-gap, rounding-line, and public-rate-card outputs remain
null.

Every claim boolean remains false.
