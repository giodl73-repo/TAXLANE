## Interpretation Boundary

The account bridge is complete, but it is not an efficiency score. Positive
and negative account entries reflect trust-fund sales flows, customer deposits,
collections, credit re-estimates, and program outlays. The −$14.936B total must
not be described as negative foreign aid, fraud recovery, spending cuts, or
automatically recoverable savings.
