# OAS Level-3 taxable-maximum and 2026 Trustees bridge

The selected OAS candidate would raise the maximum taxable earnings level until
90 percent of covered earnings are subject to Social Security payroll taxes.
CBO and JCT estimated $727.6 billion of deficit reduction over FY2025-FY2034,
including $72.3 billion in FY2026.

This is a dedicated-solvency instrument, not a spending cut. The official score
raises off-budget payroll-tax receipts, increases scheduled benefits for
affected workers, and includes an offset for reduced individual-income-tax
receipts. It therefore cannot be added mechanically to REV.

The 2026 Trustees report now projects combined OASDI depletion in 2034, with 83
percent of scheduled benefits payable then. OASI alone is projected to deplete
in 2032 with 78 percent payable, while DI remains able to pay full benefits
through 2100. The combined 75-year actuarial deficit is 4.42 percent of taxable
payroll.

The official option is useful scale evidence, but it has not been rescored on
the 2026 Trustees assumptions and lacks cohort, incidence, and administrative
effects needed for admission.
