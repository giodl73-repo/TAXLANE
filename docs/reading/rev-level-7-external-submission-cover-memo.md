# REV Level 7 external scoring request cover memo

To: Authorized congressional requester and official scoring staff  
Subject: Technical assistance request for a permanent TY2026 ordinary
individual-income-tax rate schedule

This packet requests technical assistance and, if accepted through the proper
congressional channel, official scoring of a fully specified rate-only policy.
It does not represent enacted legislation, Legislative Counsel text, a score
already issued by JCT or CBO, or a certified balanced-budget result.

## Requested policy

For tax years beginning after December 31, 2025, replace the seven ordinary
individual rates of 10, 12, 22, 24, 32, 35, and 37 percent with 21, 23, 33, 35,
43, 46, and 48 percent. Retain the current-law filing-status thresholds,
indexing, capital-gain treatment, deductions, credits, and all other tax
provisions. Treat the change as permanent for scoring. The nonofficial draft
in this packet is a drafting aid and must be conformed by Legislative Counsel.

## Requested outputs

- Legislative Counsel conforming text and transition review.
- JCT conventional annual revenue effects for FY2026 through FY2035.
- TY2026-to-FY2026 cash-timing and withholding transition effects.
- IRS administration and private compliance costs.
- Distributional and incidence tables described in the workbook.
- Proposal-specific behavioral and macroeconomic analysis, with assumptions.
- CBO spending interactions and debt-service effects, as applicable.
- A final annual and budget-window balance test using official scored values.

## Package boundary

No spending reduction is admitted. PAY is a non-additive measurement overlay,
NET is endogenous, and the H.R. 2247 transportation item is excluded as a
conditional cost note. The internal FY2026 revenue need is $813.727 billion.
The Taxlane FY2026 cash estimate of $819.220 billion is a model proxy, not an
official score.

Please identify any ambiguity, missing legislative language, alternative
baseline treatment, or output that the scoring offices cannot provide. All
official results should cite the legislative text and baseline vintage used.

Submission is prohibited until the authorization record names the requester,
authority, signer, selected channel, and approved payload digest.
