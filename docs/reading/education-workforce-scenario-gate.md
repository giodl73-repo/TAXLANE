# Education/workforce scenario gate

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/education_workforce_scenario_gate.v1.draft.json`

This is a readiness gate, not an education or workforce policy plan.

The current-law federal context is the FY2025 OMB function 500 lane already in
the rate model: $72.042B for Education, Work, and Social Services. That number
is federal budget context only. Federal spending is not the full education/workforce system because state, local, household, and employer financing must be translated.

The gate attaches existing education and workforce evidence: the education
depth card, higher-education account bridge, training/employment account bridge,
Census CPS education access/transition baseline, BLS CPS worker baseline, and
WIOA national outcome baseline. Those records help describe the perimeter and
the evidence surface. They do not select a policy package or prove a target
cost.

Current status:

- current law is available as federal FY2025 context;
- federal/state/local translation is missing;
- learner and worker population modeling is missing;
- attainment, completion, access, employment, and equity floor thresholds are
  missing;
- behavior, transition-cost, incidence, and distribution models are missing;
- policy-specific score provenance is missing.

Because those blockers remain open, central and stress education/workforce scenarios remain unscored and solver-ineligible. Stress must later mean the same selected policy under adverse realization, not an unrelated aggressive spending cut.

No target cost, gross savings, net savings, federal budget effect, or balanced rate is published by this gate.
