Agriculture covers farm income stabilization, crop insurance, agricultural research, inspection, market support, and general agricultural services. FY2025 Function 350 agriculture was $47.447B: $21.185B for farm income stabilization and $26.262B for agricultural research and services. Food and nutrition assistance is function 600 income security, not function 350 agriculture. Budget support, insurance, general services, and market-price support must be distinguished before any policy scenario or benchmark use.

Crop-insurance improper-payment facts do not prove fraud. They also do not create recoverable savings without causal prevention or same-cohort collection lineage. Subsidy levels, insurance losses, indemnities, and payment-integrity estimates are review signals only, not waste findings, fraud findings, savings estimates, or department-cut instructions.

International affairs covers diplomacy, development, humanitarian aid, security assistance, foreign information and exchange, and international financial programs. FY2025 Function 150 international affairs was $45.171B, or 0.64% of federal outlays. The five components are development and humanitarian assistance, international security assistance, conduct of foreign affairs/diplomacy, foreign information and exchange, and international financial programs. Diplomacy, ODA, humanitarian, security assistance, information/exchange, and financial instruments must stay separate. International spending differences are not savings.

The negative international-financial-program line is an accounting result driven mainly by FMS customer deposits exceeding same-year trust-fund outlays. It is not negative diplomacy, aid, fraud recovery, or automatic savings.
