# HLT Level 5 site-neutral imaging candidate audit

CBO estimates that applying site-neutral rates to imaging at all off-campus
hospital outpatient departments would reduce outlays by $7.6 billion over
FY2025–FY2034, including $0.4 billion in FY2026. This is much narrower than the
rejected uniform Medicare Advantage benchmark option.

The narrower scope does not itself satisfy the outcome floors. CBO's option
does not publish beneficiary cost-sharing, provider-access, rural and safety-
net, diagnostic-timeliness, distribution, or separately exposed behavioral
results, and it has not been rescored against the February 2026 baseline.
Taxlane therefore selects it for further audit but admits zero package savings.
