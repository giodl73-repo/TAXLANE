# REV Level-5 macro assignment methodology audit

JCT's January 2025 methodology defines macroeconomic revenue feedback as the
proposed-law macro revenue result less the present-law result and less the
conventional revenue estimate. JCT uses calibrated models and weights their
results for a point estimate.

TAXLANE has none of those proposal-specific macro runs. The independent
Tax-Calculator result already includes a partial-equilibrium taxable-income
response, but it is not a conventional JCT score or a general-equilibrium
model. Historical dynamic-score ratios therefore cannot be transferred.

REV Level 5 is methodologically complete and quantitatively reblocked. The
strongest bounded planning sensitivity remains +10.923 points centrally and
approximately +10.160 to +11.854, before unscored private compliance and macro
effects. No proper assigned rate is published.
