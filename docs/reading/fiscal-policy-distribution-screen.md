# Fiscal Policy Distribution Screen

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/fiscal_policy_distribution_screen.v1.draft.json`.

A package large enough to change the debt path cannot be distributionally
neutral. The important distinction is between **who remits a tax or loses a
federal payment first** and **who ultimately bears the economic burden**.

| Lever | First-order exposure | Central protection question |
|---|---|---|
| 5% VAT | Consumers, workers, and owners through prices and factor incomes | Is there a visible refundable credit or cash offset for basic consumption? |
| AGI surtax | Filers above the chosen threshold | Are thresholds indexed and tested across household sizes? |
| Limit employer-health exclusion | Workers with higher-premium plans | Does the cap distinguish generosity from geography, age, and health risk? |
| Emissions tax | Energy users and carbon-intensive workers and regions | Are household rebates and transition support explicit? |
| Defense reduction | Personnel, contractors, communities, and capabilities | Are choices capability-led with readiness and workforce floors? |
| Medicare Advantage benchmarks | Plans and enrollees through benefits, networks, premiums, or exits | Are access, quality, county, and enrollee-transition protections enforceable? |
| Foreign-income corporate tax | Shareholders, workers, consumers, and investment through uncertain channels | Are incidence and avoidance modeled rather than assigned to the legal payer? |
| Financial-transactions tax | Investors, savers, intermediaries, issuers, and trading activity | Are pass-through, retirement exposure, liquidity, and avoidance tested? |

This is a qualitative screen, not a distributional score. It deliberately does
not assign dollar burdens or income-decile results without a household, firm,
health-plan, energy, and regional model.

## Package gate

Before a basket becomes a proposal, TAXLANE should require one joint analysis
showing gross burdens, rebates or offsets, net household burdens, service and
access effects, employment and regional transitions, and uncertainty. Isolated
option tables are not enough because the same household can be affected by
several components simultaneously.

The persuasive position is therefore two-sided: the fiscal adjustment must be
large enough to matter, and its protections must be explicit enough to prevent
the debt target from laundering hidden hardship.

Source: CBO's individual option descriptions in *Options for Reducing the
Deficit: 2025 to 2034*. The incidence channels and protection gates are
TAXLANE analytical requirements, not CBO recommendations.
