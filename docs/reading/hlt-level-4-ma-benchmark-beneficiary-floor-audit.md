# HLT Level-4 Medicare Advantage beneficiary-floor audit

The unmitigated 10 percent benchmark reduction is not admitted. CBO expects
average premiums to fall, but also expects Medicare Advantage enrollees to pay
more out of pocket and receive fewer supplemental benefits. Those are two
adverse no-regression results, not merely missing evidence.

Plan availability, access, quality, vulnerable-population distribution, provider
networks, and administration remain unquantified under the policy. MedPAC's
2026 baseline shows a robust program and substantial excess-payment evidence,
but it does not prove that a uniform cut passes those floors. The next HLT
candidate should target excess-payment drivers and include beneficiary mitigation.
