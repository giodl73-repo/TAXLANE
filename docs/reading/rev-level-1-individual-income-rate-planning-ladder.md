# REV Level-1 individual-income rate planning ladder

Taxlane now shows numerical rate planning sensitivities. The official 2026
ordinary-income schedule is 10, 12, 22, 24, 32, 35, and 37 percent. The ladder
then shows the uniform percentage-point uplift associated with primary- and
total-balance targets under several annual spending-reduction assumptions.

For the more tractable primary-balance target, no spending reduction produces
a 7.000-to-7.655 point sensitivity. A $500 billion annual spending reduction
reduces that to 2.699-to-2.951 points; $750 billion reduces it to
0.548-to-0.600 points. These are not assigned rates.

The range compares transparent static TY2023 taxable-income arithmetic with a
CBO/JCT December 2024 scored option. The former has a period mismatch; the
latter predates the 2025 law that made the current schedule permanent. The next
rate-critical task is a current-law rescore plus distribution and behavior.
