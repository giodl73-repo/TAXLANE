# Fiscal-package OAS contribution bridge

OAS requires three separate accounting rails:

- primary spending correction: OAS contributes zero because the candidate is a
  payroll-tax instrument that also increases scheduled benefits;
- OASDI solvency: the old official score shows $72.3 billion of FY2026 deficit
  effect, but the 2026-rescored admitted value remains null;
- REV income tax: the guarded uplift remains 2.760 points because the OAS score
  already contains an aggregate individual-income-tax offset and the joint
  tax-unit allocation is unfinished.

The official FY2026 OAS scale equals 14.46 percent of the assumed $500 billion
target, but that comparison does not convert it into spending savings. NET is
next and may respond only to admitted primary policy paths.
