# Taxpayer profiles: current law, Taxlane, and research scenarios

## What is ready

Taxlane can now show a narrow, reproducible comparison of 2026 current-law
ordinary-income bracket tax with its +11-point analytical schedule. The
calculation starts with **taxable ordinary income after deductions**, not gross
salary, and preserves the current-law filing-status breakpoints.

Because every ordinary rate rises by the same 11 percentage points, the narrow
Taxlane difference is 11% of the stated taxable ordinary income. This identity
does not make the example a complete tax return. Credits, deductions, payroll
taxes, capital gains, AMT, withholding, refunds, household incidence,
compliance cost, and behavior are outside the profile calculation.

## What peer comparison says

The captured 2022 OECD general-government panel points in different directions
by purpose:

- the U.S. defense spending share is above the five displayed peers;
- the U.S. government health spending share is above the five displayed peers;
- the U.S. social-protection share is below the five displayed peers.

That supports research into defense-share convergence and health-cost
convergence. It does **not** support booking savings or a lower rate. Defense
readiness and alliance commitments, health access and quality, transition,
distribution, federal/state boundaries, timing, and a matched FY2026 dollar
crosswalk remain unresolved. Social-protection convergence would more likely
expand or restructure protection than produce a simple cut.
