# ANCHOR and BASTION SEM-012 intake disposition

Taxlane has completed its first direct test of the two lane-owner evidence-pack
candidates. The result is useful but intentionally conservative:

> The ANCHOR and BASTION packs share enough structure to support a future
> adapter, but neither contains evidence that can change Taxlane's OAS or DEF
> fiscal disposition today.

Both producer artifacts replay successfully from their published `main`
commits. Both preserve a source digest, scenario version, bounded alternatives,
transition range, distribution, uncertainty, overlap, dissent, unresolved
holds, an external admission owner, and explicit no-authority fields. That is a
real interoperability result: Taxlane does not need the two domains to flatten
their distinct outcome evidence into one generic score.

## ANCHOR to OAS

ANCHOR contributes a coherent retirement/disability research shape:

- retirement and disability realization remain separate;
- adequacy, service delivery, rights, and distribution remain visible;
- annual-balance and transition-cost uncertainty are bounded;
- overlap and dissent are explicit; and
- the pack cannot admit, allocate, change rates, change policy, or release
  itself.

It does not yet provide the official, actuarially certified, price-year and
horizon-specific annual current-law path required to reopen Taxlane's OAS
disposition. Candidate-specific cohort adequacy, employer incidence,
administration, and overlap evidence also remain incomplete.

OAS therefore remains a dedicated-solvency overlay with **$0.000 billion** of
admitted FY2026 primary spending reduction.

## BASTION to DEF

BASTION contributes a coherent defense research shape:

- funded capacity remains distinct from usable readiness;
- lifecycle and transition ranges remain visible;
- readiness, safety, and supplier floors remain separate;
- distribution, overlap, dissent, and unrealized savings are explicit; and
- the pack cannot admit, allocate, change rates, procure, plan operations, or
  release itself.

It does not yet provide a current FYDP-mapped proposal, candidate-specific
annual current-law cash path, simultaneous-demand readiness evidence,
personnel/family/community transition evidence, acquisition and industrial
schedules, allied effects, or a legal current-law rescore.

DEF therefore remains at reviewed zero admission with **$0.000 billion** of
admitted FY2026 primary spending reduction.

## Shared-contract finding

The two packs already share a useful candidate envelope. Their domain evidence
should remain typed:

- ANCHOR owns annual-balance, delivery-confidence, and rights evidence.
- BASTION owns lifecycle-cost, readiness, safety, supplier, and
  unrealized-savings evidence.
- Taxlane owns track mapping, fiscal-object classification, current-law cash
  effects, admission review, and the resulting rate dependency.

A shared adapter should not be built yet. Neither input triggers an existing
Taxlane reopening condition, so adapter code would currently be
project-management infrastructure without a product-changing consumer. The
adapter becomes justified when at least one lane supplies evidence capable of
entering an admission rerun.
