# REV Level-2 formal-rate gate audit

Two gates pass: the official 2026 statutory schedule and the package arithmetic.
Six gates block a proper rate:

- formal current-law tax-unit scoring;
- nonlinear behavior and income shifting;
- tax-year liability to fiscal-year cash timing;
- distribution and economic incidence;
- administration, compliance, and transition costs;
- cross-instrument and macroeconomic feedback.

The audit converts those blockers into a formal scoring specification for REV
Level 3. It requires fiscal receipts, tax liability, effective and marginal
rates, distribution, behavior, administration, macro feedback, and uncertainty.
