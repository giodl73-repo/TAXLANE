# Income security and family scenario gate

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/income_security_family_scenario_gate.v1.draft.json`.

This is a readiness gate, not an income-support or family-policy plan.

The current FY2025 context records $701.609B of Income Security function cost,
$69.451B of dedicated payroll/excise receipts, and a $632.158B residual general-fund need. That residual need is not a savings target and does not by itself choose a benefit package, eligibility rule, or tax rate.

Before a central or stress scenario can be solver-eligible, Taxlane still needs
a benefit package model, eligible-population model, take-up model, federal/state/local translation, child-poverty floor, material-hardship floor, formal-childcare-access floor, work-transition floor, distribution by income/family/work status, administration and transition cost, and policy-specific score provenance.

Family spending comparisons are context, not an income-security target.
Child-poverty and childcare-access measures are outcome floors, not spending cut
rules. Work-transition evidence must distinguish reduced need from reduced
access.

Until those inputs exist, central and stress income-security/family scenarios remain unscored and solver-ineligible.
