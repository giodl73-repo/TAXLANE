# REV Level 7 official-score request workbook

The official-score handoff is now operational rather than narrative. It has one
row for every fiscal year from 2026 through 2035 and fields for conventional
revenue, spending interactions, administration, macro feedback, primary
deficit, net interest, and total deficit. All official fields remain null.

The workbook separately shows Taxlane's $819.220 billion FY2026 model cash
proxy, requests three distribution tables, enumerates behavioral and macro
outputs, defines the deficit and balance identities, and asks nine concrete
scorer questions. It is ready for an authorized scoring channel but has not
been submitted and is not an official score.
