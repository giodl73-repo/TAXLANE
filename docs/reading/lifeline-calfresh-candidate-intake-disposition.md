budget stress test. The two-year proposal combines **$86.8 million** in gross
administration work with a **-$78.6 million** caseload-related administration
offset, leaving **$8.2 million** in net administration cost.

## Why ISF still does not reopen

The envelope implements enacted federal law; it is not a proposed reduction
against current law. California's state funding was still proposed in the
source record. Postimplementation timeliness, churn, erroneous denial,
correction, notice, appeal workload, food hardship, adequacy, county capacity,
and overlap evidence are absent. It also does not resolve the distinct gates
on Taxlane's existing school-meal candidate.

Taxlane therefore custodies the identity but admits no FY2026 reduction. The
$813.727B target and 21/23/33/35/43/46/48 planning schedule do not change.
