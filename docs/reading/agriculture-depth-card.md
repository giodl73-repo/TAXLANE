# Agriculture Depth Card

Machine record: `data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/agriculture_depth_card.fy2025.v1.draft.json`.

The complete FY2025 federal Agriculture function was **$47.447B**, or **0.68%
of federal outlays**:

- agricultural research and services: $26.262B / 0.37%;
- farm income stabilization: $21.185B / 0.30%.

Food and nutrition assistance is not inside function 350; it belongs to Income
Security. Combining it here would double count another public-purpose lane.

A serious benchmark needs crop-insurance and commodity-account flows, farm/acre/
production/risk denominators, research adoption and productivity, conservation
crossovers, and matched subsidy/rural-economy definitions. Aggregate spending
alone supports no fraud, waste, or recoverable-savings claim.
