# SHIELD establishes the shortage registry—not a hospital shortage verdict

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's July 31, 2026 HRSA primary-care HPSA census. The
daily download contains 79,150 designation-component rows. Counting unique
HPSA IDs within status yields 7,682 currently designated, 1,014 proposed for
withdrawal, and 8,999 withdrawn designations. Of the current designations, 762
span multiple components and 282 span multiple rural-status values.

HRSA's separate June 30 quarterly report counts 9,003 primary-care HPSA
designations, a designated population of 108,566,500, 47.43% of need met, and
18,541 practitioners needed. Taxlane preserves those official summary values
but does not force them to match the newer daily file. Their dates and status
surfaces differ, and designation populations can overlap.

This is the first formal shortage-registry spine for HLT. It is not a
hospital-level or whole-county shortage assignment, a count of unique affected
people, an access or staffed-capacity measure, or a bounded intervention.
Taxlane therefore admits the registry evidence but no candidate effect or
savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining target stays **$813.727B**, and
both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
