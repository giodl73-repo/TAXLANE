asset-maintenance and safety pilot. It does not capture source bytes or close source custody.

The selected pilot remains transportation asset maintenance and safety under
the `transportation-infrastructure` lane.

This is a transportation pilot source plan, not captured source evidence,

Planned source families:

- OMB federal transportation outlay baseline.
- Treasury trust-fund receipts, balances, transfers, offsets, and financing
  reconciliation.
- DOT budget, performance, grant, project-delivery, asset, and safety context.
- FHWA condition, performance, bridge, pavement, exposure, and investment-need
  context.
- NHTSA and BTS safety, exposure, reliability, mobility, and access indicators.
- Census state and local finance context.
- GAO and DOT Inspector General project-delivery and control context.
- International Transport Forum and OECD context after matched scope review.

Every future source capture must include retrieval date, source URL, publisher,
vintage, raw bytes, byte count, SHA-256, local raw path, metadata, matched
period, matched unit, matched perimeter, and missingness disclosure.

Trust funds remain separate. Explicit general-fund transfers and credited
offsetting collections must be recorded. State, local, private, and
user-financed activity remains contextual until translated. International transportation differences are not savings.

Access, quality/safety, equity, adequacy/resilience, delivery feasibility, and
asset-condition lane-specific floor families are planned but not thresholded.
Their values remain null and their pass flags remain false.

The baseline path, floor indicator contract, modernization path, and stress path
are not created by this pulse. The simulator has not run.

Only the source plan is published. Source custody, target costs, rates, public
cards, savings, waste, fraud, department cuts, technology savings, and
balanced-budget claims remain blocked.
