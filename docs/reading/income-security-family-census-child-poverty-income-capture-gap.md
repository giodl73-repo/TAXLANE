# Income-security/family Census child poverty and income capture gap

This packet explains
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/income_security_family_census_child_poverty_income_capture_gap.v1.draft.json`.

Pulse 200 records the unresolved Census domestic poverty and income source gate
for the income-security/family lane. Candidate official surfaces include
Census Current Population Reports poverty releases, Census Supplemental Poverty
Measure material, and CPS ASEC poverty/income documentation.

The gap remains open because there is no local raw Census poverty report, Census
poverty table, or Census SPM table in custody for this lane gate yet. Existing
source-ledger context for `SRC-CENSUS-P60-280` and the OECD child
relative-poverty bridge do not close this domestic Census gate.

Before values can be populated, a reviewer needs to capture official raw bytes,
source URLs, retrieval dates, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes, metadata paths, and
the poverty measure, income definition, household or family unit, age perimeter,
and source year.

This capture gap is not Census raw source custody, not official child poverty
context, not SPM child poverty context, not deep poverty or near-poverty
context, not an income definition, not an income-unit perimeter, not
child-poverty floor values, not pass/fail findings, not target-cost selection,
not gross savings, not net savings, not solver input, not rate calculation, not
a public rate card, not a department-cut instruction, not a technology-savings
claim, and not a balanced-budget claim.

Short validator phrases: Census child poverty and income source-capture gap
only; not Census raw source custody; not solver input; not a balanced-budget
claim.

Exact validator phrases: Census Current Population Reports poverty releases; Census Supplemental Poverty Measure material; CPS ASEC poverty/income documentation; no local raw Census poverty report, Census poverty table, or Census SPM table; Census child poverty and income source-capture gap only.
