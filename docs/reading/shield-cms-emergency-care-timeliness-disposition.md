# SHIELD adds emergency-process observations—not a live access verdict

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's May 2026 CMS Timely and Effective Care emergency
baseline. The standard reporting surface matches 4,660 of 5,432 current
hospital IDs by exact CCN (85.79%). The 772-hospital residual is 635 psychiatric,
132 Veterans Administration, and five long-term hospitals.

For overall ED time, 4,050 hospitals report a numeric value and 610 report
`Not Available`. The facility median is 154 minutes versus CMS's 167-minute
national value. Transfer time is available for 2,340 hospitals and unavailable
for 2,320. Left-without-being-seen percentages are available for 3,832 and
unavailable for 828. All 41 Rural Emergency Hospitals use a distinct CMS
164-row reporting surface rather than the suppressed standard rows.

This is meaningful process evidence, but the values cover 2024 or July 2024
through June 2025. Facility medians are neither live waits nor a patient-weighted
national average. CMS's national value is a descriptive reference, not an
adequacy threshold. Current staffing, schedules, travel/catchments, local need,
causes, quality outcomes, and delivery remedies remain unproved.

Taxlane therefore admits the official process context but no candidate effect
or savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining target stays **$813.727B**, and
both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
