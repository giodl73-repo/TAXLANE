# CBO Revenue Detail FY2026-FY2035 Context

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/cbo_revenue_detail_fy2026_2035_context.v1.draft.json`.

This packet extracts official CBO February 2026 revenue-detail context for
FY2026-FY2035 from the already-custodied CBO open-data revenue-detail CSV. It
adds annual context for individual income, payroll, corporate income, excise,
customs, estate and gift, Federal Reserve remittances, miscellaneous fees, and
total receipts.

## Boundary

This is official CBO February 2026 revenue-detail context only. It is not a legal/economic base, not a payer-universe definition, not an incidence or distribution model, not a rate bridge, not solver input, not a tax proposal, not a savings estimate, and not a balanced-budget claim.

The values reduce revenue-solvency source-context gaps, but matched receipt
bases, legal and economic perimeters, current-law and reform yields,
administration burden, behavior, incidence, distribution, solver mapping, public
rates, and balanced-budget claims remain blocked.
