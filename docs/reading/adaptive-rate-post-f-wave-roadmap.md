This Pulse 233 predecessor mapped the next six waves as one dependency chain:

| Wave | Closure |
|---|---|
| G | Official FY2025-FY2035 current-law federal topline spine |
| H | 17-row, fund, transfer, offset, reserve, rounding, debt, and interest substrate |
| I | First real transportation reform scenario and complete applicable floor decision |
| J | Matched financing base, yields, behavior, incidence, distribution, and administration |
| K | Integrated internal candidate solver and effective-rate calculation |
| L | Role-reviewed first public transportation pilot rate card |

Discovery may overlap, but no wave may admit an output that depends on an open
prior gate. One successful pilot does not establish all-lane readiness; other
lanes repeat the reform-through-publication sequence after Wave L.

Pulse 234 superseded these generic names with the extensible CORE/TRN corpus
plan. The mapping is `G -> CORE-G`, `H -> CORE-H`, and `I` through `L` became
the transportation stages `TRN-C` through `TRN-F`; `TRN-A` and `TRN-B` make
the lane-specific baseline and accounting work explicit. Read
`adaptive-rate-corpus-track-plan.md` for the controlling plan.

The predecessor status is now reconciled: G/CORE-G, H/CORE-H, and I/TRN-C are
complete; J/TRN-D has started; K/TRN-E and L/TRN-F remain blocked. TRN-C's
H.R. 2247 scenario is conditional and cost-only.

This historical roadmap is not a solver run, target cost, rate publication,
savings claim, or balanced-budget claim.
