# VET Missed-Exam Review and Accommodation Stress Envelope

The new feature engine makes the service path after a missed examination
explicit. In its 1,000-claim normalized fixture, notice reaches 900 claimants,
720 receive an accommodation offer, 540 accept rescheduling, and 432 complete
an examination. The remaining 568 claims receive an existing-evidence review;
142 have sufficient evidence in the hypothetical case and 426 remain
unresolved. Thus 574 claims become procedurally reviewable, not necessarily
granted or corrected.

The fixture requires 1,212 work hours, or 0.808 normalized FTE, plus $100,000
of assumed IT and support. At $180,000 assumed compensation per FTE, modeled
cost is $245,440, or $427.60 per procedurally reviewable claim. Completed exams
also impose 1,728 claimant hours under the assumed four-hour burden.

The funnel matters: a policy can reduce summary denials while increasing
processor work, examination travel, evidence review, and follow-up. Public
evidence does not yet provide the missed-exam cohort, reasons, accommodation
take-up, sufficiency, corrected decisions, appeal effects, or claimant burden
needed to weight the model.

VET moves to named triggers for enactment, observed delivery, matched outcomes,
rights passage, implementation cost, and a current score. Zero savings are
admitted; TRN becomes the next lane.
