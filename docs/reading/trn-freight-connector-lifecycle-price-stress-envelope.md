# TRN freight-connector lifecycle-price stress envelope

The executable TRN engine converts the documented $190 million capital split
and 11,000-vehicle design basis into a transparent normalized stress. At 70
percent assumed utilization, the fixture carries 2.811 million annual trips,
including 1.686 million truck trips.

With an assumed 30-year straight-line recovery of $169 million private capital,
a 2 percent annual O&M stress on total capital, and the documented $100,000 city
lease payment, the mechanical annual requirement is $9.533 million. A chosen
3:1 truck-to-other price weight produces equivalent prices of $4.63 per truck
trip and $1.54 per other trip.

These are diagnostic prices, not actual or recommended tolls. The calculation
omits financing return, inflation, taxes, contract reserves, elasticity,
collection leakage, residual value, and observed performance. Its value is to
make the owner-data question precise: compare actual classed trips, paid tolls,
service, full lifecycle cost, public receipts, diversion, access, and resilience
against the same identities. TRN is now on named observation triggers with zero
admitted savings and no rate effect.
