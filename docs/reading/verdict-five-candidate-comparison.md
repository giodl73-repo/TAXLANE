## What the comparison validates

1. **Owner alignment is mandatory.** A lane label cannot substitute for the
   repository that owns the candidate's service semantics.
2. **Delivery maturity is visible.** Active contracts, implementation, and
   observation cadence raise Deliverability and Iteration without pretending
   that they are outcomes.
3. **Access evidence is independently visible.** CalFresh's denial, timeliness,
   error, hearing, and affected-population baselines raise Coverage evidence
   while the postimplementation gate remains blocked.
4. **Capacity is not outcome.** Judges, staff, contracts, software, design,
   training, and appropriations remain inputs until matched service results
   exist.
5. **Fiscal components retain their meaning.** Partner cofunding, caseload
   offsets, pension/Medicaid interactions, and unexpended obligations cannot be
   relabeled as savings.

## Evidence priority

The next useful work is not more scoring. Each candidate needs its specific
result chain:

- **CalFresh:** one complete candidate-coded recertification cycle.
- **Nepal Compact:** one completed and independently evaluated road or
  electricity cohort.
- **H.R. 2137:** enactment and implementation, then matched claimant and rights
  outcomes.
- **H.R. 1702:** enactment and staffed seats, then matched caseflow and rights
  outcomes.
- **H.R. 2247:** assign the aviation service owner, then establish baseline,
  adoption, failure, burden, access, and learning evidence.

Until one chain closes all applicable floors and fiscal requirements, admitted
spending reduction remains zero and REV has no candidate-driven rate reduction
to recognize.
