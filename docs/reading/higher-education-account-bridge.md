## What Drives The Sign

Federal Direct Student Loan credit accounting dominates. OMB records
**$64.659B** in the Direct Loan program account, **−$132.927B** in negative-
subsidy receipts, and **−$10.439B** in downward subsidy re-estimates. Together
those three rows equal **−$78.707B**.

The OMB Education Appendix reports the same negative-subsidy and downward-
reestimate receipt amounts. The Department of Education's FY2025 Agency
Financial Report independently supports the accounting cause class: credit
subsidy costs are re-estimated as assumptions and loan performance change, and
loan modifications alter recorded subsidy cost. Its financial-statement
categories do not substitute for the OMB account reconciliation.

FFEL credit accounts add −$6.752B. Positive student-aid, administration,
institutional-support, tax-credit, and other entries offset much of those
negative credit-account entries, leaving the exact −$35.005B total.

The [Pell short-training randomized evaluation](pell-short-training-impact-evidence.md)
tests offers of experimental eligibility to selected applicants from 2012–17.
Its $1,752 average disbursement among grant users is not a full incremental cost
and cannot be assigned to a FY2025 account row, subsidy estimate, reestimate,
receipt, or outlay in this bridge.

The [FY2024 FSA Title IV baseline](fsa-title-iv-student-access-baseline.md)
reports $120,816.1 million of aid disbursed and qualifier-bounded program reach.
Those administrative delivery amounts do not reconcile to this bridge's FY2025
net outlays: period, accounting basis, account coverage, and credit-subsidy
treatment differ.

The [B&B Pell bachelor-recipient baseline](pell-bachelor-recipient-outcome-baseline.md)
adds historical, completion-conditioned survey outcomes. Those worker,
borrower, and repayment measures are not FY2025 account outlays, Pell program
costs, credit-subsidy entries, or a reconciliation crosswalk.

The [BPS first-time-student longitudinal bridge](bps-first-time-student-longitudinal-bridge.md)
adds an early descriptive 2019–20 entrant cohort followed through June 2022.
The separate [Pell current-entrant persistence baseline](pell-current-entrant-persistence-baseline.md)
attaches the official DataLab entry-year receipt-group cross-tab. Its five
categories are not the First Look six-category distribution, receipt is not
eligibility, and the pandemic-era table is neither causal nor mature. It
supplies no program cost or FY2025 fiscal crosswalk.

The linked [significance screen](pell-current-entrant-persistence-significance-screen.md)
is an independent-estimates descriptive screen, not a covariance-aware test,
causal estimate, program-cost allocation, or FY2025 account crosswalk.

## Interpretation Boundary

The account bridge is complete, but cash-collection equivalence is not
established. Negative-subsidy and downward-reestimate receipts are federal
credit-accounting entries; the total does not mean students received negative
education, that $35.005B of cash was collected in FY2025, or that an equal
amount of fraud, waste, or recoverable savings was found.
