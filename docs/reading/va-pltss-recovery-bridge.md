# VA PLTSS Recovery Bridge

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/va_pltss_recovery_bridge.fy2024-q4-2025.v1.draft.json`.

## Result

The available sources do **not** support a recovery rate against the $218.30M
FY2024 projected-overpayment estimate.

The estimate is a statistical projection from payments sampled between October
2022 and September 2023. The Q4 2025 scorecard separately reports operational
recovery activity:

| Recovery method | Activity period | Identified | Recovered to date |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Routine recovery activity | FY2025 Q4 | $6.91M | $4.46M |
| Recovery audit | FY2025 Q4 | $1.18M | $0.76M |
| PIIA samples and post-payment deep dives | FY2023–FY2025 | $3.97M | $3.71M |

These rows are useful operational evidence, but their periods and bases do not
match the projected estimate. The scorecard does not provide debt- or
claim-cohort lineage showing which projected errors became established debts,
which debts survived review or appeal, or which collections came from those
same debts. Mixed-period rows must not be summed.

## Why the annual recovery table does not close the gap

The FY2024 annual workbook reports VA-wide recovery activity, including
$1.517B identified and $792.55M recovered through recovery activities. That row
covers the whole agency, not PLTSS. Its recovery-audit collections also exceed
the same-year identified amount, showing that collections can relate to prior
periods. It cannot serve as the denominator or numerator for a PLTSS recovery
rate.

## Safe public wording

TAXLANE may display the projected estimate and each recovery row separately
with its period and basis. It may not divide one by the other, describe the
$218.30M as collectible debt, or turn either source into fraud, prevented-loss,
or net-savings claims.

The missing bridge is transaction lineage: sampled payment, projected error,
established receivable, appeal or reversal, collection or offset, write-off,
and control cost must be connected for the same cohort.

The later [VA OIG verification review](va-pltss-oig-verification.md) independently
supports the sampling and estimate process but publishes no such cohort lineage.
