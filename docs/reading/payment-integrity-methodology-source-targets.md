This packet turns the methodology plans into a source-discovery queue. Each
selected program has three open source targets: the PaymentAccuracy methodology
appendix, the agency/program methodology source, and the program-specific
control or reporting materials.

## Priority

Priority 1 is the PaymentAccuracy methodology appendix because it should anchor
the scorecard probe to the reporting-period method. Priority 2 cross-checks the
program-specific methodology. Priority 3 resolves the program-specific
interpretation risk: state data for Part D, targeted medical review for VA
PLTSS, quality-control/compliance review for Federal Crop Insurance, and PERM
state-cycle/corrective-action materials for Medicaid.
