## Interpretation Boundary

Budget authority is not outlays. Training and Employment Services has
$3,898.587M of FY2025 enacted budget authority in the CBJ but $1.800B of
FY2025 actual PBD outlays. Multi-year availability, obligations, carry-in, and
the timing of cash disbursements prevent a same-year comparison from being a
program cost calculation.

The WIOA outcome record follows program-year exit cohorts and later follow-up
quarters, whereas this bridge records fiscal-year account outlays. Actual
outlays cannot be allocated to those cohorts from these sources. Cost per
participant or outcome is therefore blocked, and no causal effect, fraud,
waste, or recoverable savings is inferred.
