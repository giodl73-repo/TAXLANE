# REV Level-6 policy-rate decision

REV Level 6 selects the smallest one-decimal uniform uplift not below the
+10.923-point bounded central sensitivity: **+11.0 points**.

Applied to the verified 2026 ordinary-income rates, the planning schedule is
21, 23, 33, 35, 43, 46, and 48 percent, with bracket thresholds unchanged. The
exact Tax-Calculator run produces a $1,058.117 billion full-tax-year behavioral
liability change and an $819.220 billion first-year cash proxy, $5.493 billion
above the $813.727 billion target.

This is a reviewed model-planning assignment, not enacted law or an official
score. The public uncertainty band is +10.2 to +11.9 points. The model assigns
52.53 percent of the liability change to the top decile and estimates aggregate
after-tax income down 8.326 percent; those effects must accompany the rate.
