# Rate-down bundle dependency rerun

The first rate-down pass did not lower the rate. It replaced two overbroad
spending envelopes with better candidates, then applied the existing admission
rules:

- HLT's narrow site-neutral imaging option remains blocked by beneficiary,
  provider, rural, behavior, distribution, and current-law score evidence.
- DEF's proportional-force concept remains blocked by a current executable
  FYDP allocation, readiness testing, transition costs, and a matched score.
- PAY remains a non-additive measurement overlay.
- NET remains endogenous and is zero without an admitted primary path.

Consequently the FY2026 cash target remains $813.727 billion and the public
planning schedule remains 21, 23, 33, 35, 43, 46, and 48 percent. This closes
the bundle honestly and hands work to the eight-track catch-up rather than
manufacturing a lower rate from rejected envelopes.
