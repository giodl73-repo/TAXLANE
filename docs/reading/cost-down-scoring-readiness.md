# Cost-Down Scoring Readiness

Machine rows:
`data/derived/efficiency_pressure/cost_down_scoring_readiness.v1.draft.jsonl`

This packet ranks the ten cost-down first-pass rollup rows by near-term scoring
readiness. The rank is not a savings estimate. It says where the next source
extract is most likely to turn a pressure question into a scoreable review
artifact without skipping access, service, quality, methodology, or legal
floors.

## Current Priority Shape

The most ready rows are payment-integrity eligibility accuracy, defense
procurement control, and defense audit-control closure. They already have
program or control signals attached, but they still need methodology,
corrective-action, access-floor, readiness-floor, and ownership evidence before
any public finding or savings claim.

Disaster mitigation is next because FEMA project rows expose useful project
fields. It still needs a project-to-declaration and account/outlay bridge before
avoided-loss or savings language would be safe.

Debt rows carry the highest scale pressure, but they require scenario alignment.
Primary balance needs CBO/OMB baseline and policy assumptions. Maturity risk
needs Treasury maturity and refinancing exposure records plus reviewed
rate-scenario rules.

Health and Medicare remain high-pressure but source-extract blocked. Benchmark
and trustee context are not enough to score waste or savings. Price discipline
needs service-level price, utilization, quality, access, and case-mix evidence.
Administrative simplification needs claims, denial, appeal, authorization,
rework, administrative-cost, and service-floor records.

## Boundary

Use these rows to decide what to extract next. Do not publish them as fraud,
waste, abuse, poor performance, avoided-loss, or savings findings.
