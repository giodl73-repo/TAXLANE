# REV Level-1 individual-income tax/fiscal timing bridge

REV-L1-03 is complete as a bounded reblock. The available IRS figures describe
TY2023 return aggregates, while the OMB figure describes FY2025 federal cash
receipts. Form 1040 separately identifies withholding, estimated payments,
credits, refunds, and balances due, and Treasury reports federal activity on a
fiscal modified-cash surface.

A defensible bridge therefore needs cohort assignments for withholding,
estimated payments, return settlements, refunds and refundable credits,
enforcement collections, timing lags, and legal-perimeter differences. Those
assignments are not in custody. The three available values remain context only
and must not be divided.

REV-L1-04 may now gather behavior, compliance, administration, incidence, and
distribution evidence. REV-L1-05, a rate, and a public rate card remain blocked.
