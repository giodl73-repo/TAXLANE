# TRN-F H.R. 2247 cost note

TRN-F is complete as Taxlane's first typed cost note. H.R. 2247 is not enacted.
If enacted and funded, it would require FAA modernization of airman-certificate
access, authentication, and delivery. CBO estimates $18 million of additional
federal outlays over FY2026-FY2031, subject to appropriation.

This note publishes no tax or fee rate. It claims no savings, lower target
cost, named-fund assignment, quantitative distribution result, policy
endorsement, or balanced-budget effect. CORE-N prevents this cost note from
being presented as a public rate card.
