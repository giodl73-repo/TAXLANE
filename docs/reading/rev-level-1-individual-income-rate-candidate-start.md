# REV Level-1 individual-income rate-candidate start

REV Level 1 has started with a real rate-bearing probe: the current-law federal
individual income tax. OMB reports $2,656,044 million of FY2025 individual
income tax receipts. IRS SOI reports $11,625,278.987 million of TY2023 taxable
income and $2,108,587.001 million of TY2023 income tax after credits.

Those values must not be divided into a rate. They use different year bases and
perimeters. REV-L1-02 now source-custodies and separates the legal and economic
definitions, while REV-L1-03 names the complete timing bridge and closes as a
bounded reblock because cohort assignments are unavailable.

The next work is REV-L1-04 behavior, compliance, administration, incidence,
and distribution evidence. Candidate selection and REV Level 2 remain blocked
until that evidence and a numeric matched-base bridge pass.
