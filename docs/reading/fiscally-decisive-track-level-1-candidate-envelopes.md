# Fiscally decisive track Level-1 candidate envelopes

Five tracks now have concrete, typed envelopes:

- HLT: CBO's 10 percent Medicare Advantage benchmark reduction option.
- OAS: CBO/JCT's 90-percent taxable-earnings alternative.
- PAY: GAO's FY2025 improper-payment measurement universe, explicitly not savings.
- NET: a 3.409 percent mechanical carry sensitivity, explicitly not a direct cut.
- DEF: CBO's annual DoD budget-reduction option.

HLT, OAS, and DEF scores retain their December 2024 vintage and therefore move
to a current-law dependency audit before use. PAY still needs a causal control,
same-cohort collection, appeal, burden, and returned-fund chain. NET still needs
timing, maturity, rate-path, and debt-stock integration.
