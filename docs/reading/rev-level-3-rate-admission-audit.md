# REV Level-3 rate-admission audit

Three model gates now pass: current-law tax-unit simulation, explicit behavioral
sensitivity, and income-group distribution. Three assignment gates remain
blocked:

- tax-year liability to fiscal-year cash receipts;
- administration, compliance, withholding, and transition costs;
- cross-instrument and macroeconomic feedback.

The central +8.353-point uplift is therefore a provisional modeled rate, not a
proper fiscal rate or assigned public schedule. REV Level 4 owns the remaining
assignment closure.
