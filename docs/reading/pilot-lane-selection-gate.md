This is a pilot-lane selection gate, not a final pilot choice, public rate card,
statutory-rate proposal, tax proposal, savings estimate, waste finding, fraud
finding, department-cut instruction, technology-savings claim, solver result, or
balanced-budget claim.

conflicts.

disaster administration and mitigation reserve operations, and claims-processing

any lane requiring immediate normative tax-distribution or benefit-adequacy
choices are excluded as first pilots.

official-source feasibility, floor observability, technology-transition fit,
solver containment, and public-language safety.

All selected-pilot, pilot-rate-card, pilot-target-cost, pilot-effective-rate,
pilot-savings-estimate, and pilot-solver-result outputs remain null.
