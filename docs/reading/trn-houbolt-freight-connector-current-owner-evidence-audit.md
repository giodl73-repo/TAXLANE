# TRN Houbolt freight-connector current-owner evidence audit

TRN now studies an operating transportation service rather than an aviation
credential procedure. The Houbolt Road Extension connects I-80 to the Joliet
intermodal district through a tolled bridge and road package delivered under a
public-private model.

FHWA documents a $190 million project: $21 million of state work and an implied
$169 million private share. The facility was designed for 11,000 daily vehicles,
including 6,600 trucks. Joliet's 2023 financial statements record completion
and transfer to city ownership on April 27, 2023 and a 99-year ground-and-bridge
lease paying the city $100,000 annually.

Those facts prove an operating, owner-aligned financing and delivery structure.
They do **not** prove 11,000 actual daily trips, congestion relief, safety,
competitive tolls, lifecycle sufficiency, equitable access, federal savings,
or a model ready for replication. Current I-80 reconstruction is recorded as
context and is not mixed into the Houbolt project cost.

The next layer is a transparent normalized utilization, lifecycle-cost, and
weighted-price stress. Admission remains zero until owner records close actual
traffic, price, cost, performance, diversion, access, and fiscal disposition.
