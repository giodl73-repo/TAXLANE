# Fiscal-package DEF contribution bridge

DEF is fiscally material but not yet admissible. The official option contributes
$15 billion of mechanical scale in FY2026 and averages $106.556 billion from
FY2026 through FY2034. That is 21.311 percent of the assumed $500 billion annual
spending correction.

For scale only, combining the DEF option with HLT's rejected planning
sensitivity averages $161.783 billion per year, or 32.357 percent of the target.
The average mechanical residual is still $338.217 billion. Neither component is
admitted: HLT failed its beneficiary screen, and DEF lacks a reviewed force and
allocation case.

Therefore admitted spending savings remain zero, the guarded FY2026 REV proxy
remains a 2.760-point uplift, and there is still no solver result or balance
claim. PAY is next, under a non-additive rule that permits only attributable,
returned-fund recoveries to enter any later package.
