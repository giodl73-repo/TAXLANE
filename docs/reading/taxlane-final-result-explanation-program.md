# Taxlane final-result explanation program

Taxlane will turn its completed fifteen-track internal analysis into a layered
civic-education corpus. The program is designed for a future national audience,
but its present authority is repository-only. No deployment, announcement,
distribution, outreach, or official request is authorized.

The six waves are:

1. **EXPL-A:** canonical narrative, claim and number ledgers, terminology, and
   visual grammar;
2. **EXPL-B:** citizen brief, guides, FAQ, glossary, and teaching materials;
3. **EXPL-C:** four source-of-truth research papers;
4. **EXPL-D:** five-, twenty-, and forty-five-minute presentation systems;
5. **EXPL-E:** an accessible local static-HTML explanation and track explorer;
6. **EXPL-F:** cross-format consistency, accessibility, adversarial review, and
   repository-readiness closure.

The program plans 21 canonical deliverables. Every simplified form routes back
to validated evidence, shares one result statement and caveat block, and treats
Markdown or machine data—not PDF, slides, or HTML—as its factual source.

EXPL-A through EXPL-F are complete after two eight-role review rounds and
applied feedback in each wave; EXPL-C also passed the relevant publication-panel
gates and rendered four repository-only PDFs. The 21 canonical deliverables are
repository-ready, while external release remains blocked. The full sequencing, content portfolio, review gates,
and explicit release boundary are in the
[program plan](../programs/2026-07-27-final-result-explanation-program.md).
