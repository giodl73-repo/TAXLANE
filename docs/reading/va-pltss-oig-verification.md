# VA PLTSS Independent Verification

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/va_pltss_oig_verification.fy2024.v1.draft.json`.

The VA Office of Inspector General's FY2024 PIIA review independently supports
the reporting methodology around PLTSS, but it does not supply the transaction
lineage needed for a recovery or savings calculation.

## What the OIG verified

The review reports PLTSS improper and unknown payments of $760.10M at 13.52% in
FY2024, down from $1.418B at 38.72% in FY2023. The $760.10M total includes the
separate overpayment, underpayment, technically improper, and unknown categories
already extracted from the annual workbook; it is not a collectible amount.

OIG statisticians reviewed the statistical validity of VA sampling methods,
verified sample estimates and margins of error, and developed independent
estimates from VA test results. The report also explains that OMB permits FY2024
reporting to use prior-year activity. That supports the October 2022 through
September 2023 sample period shown in the official PLTSS scorecard and annual
workbook.

## What the report does not provide

The compliance review does not publish a PLTSS claim cohort, established
receivables tied to sampled errors, appeal or reversal dispositions, or
collection transactions tied to those receivables. Oversight.gov reports zero
questioned costs and zero funds for better use for this review.

The report therefore strengthens confidence in the published estimate while
leaving the [recovery bridge](va-pltss-recovery-bridge.md) blocked. Statistical
verification is not debt establishment, and a reduction target is not a savings
estimate.
