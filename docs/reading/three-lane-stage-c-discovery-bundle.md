# Three-lane stage-C discovery bundle

ISF-C, VET-C, and AGR-C are complete as bounded candidate screens. Each lane
examined a current-policy reference and three reform families against the
shared CORE-I/J/K/L admission, evidence, timing, and overlap rules.

No screened reform has the selected authority, complete signed annual federal
effects, implementation or behavior evidence, and applicable-floor results
needed for admission. Accordingly, ISF-D, VET-D, and AGR-D may not start.

This bundle reports discovery completion only. It does not report a benefit or
service package, crop-insurance schedule, fraud finding, reform score, floor
result, target cost, solver input, assigned rate, or savings estimate.
