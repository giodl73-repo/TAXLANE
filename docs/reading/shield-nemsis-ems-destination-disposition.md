# SHIELD adds national EMS destinations—not a travel-time map

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's bounded NEMSIS 2024 national EMS destination spine.
The report records 60,298,684 activations submitted by 14,756 agencies across
54 states and territories, including 46,733,668 activations on its 911 surface.

The separately grained destination table contains 30,123,274 coded events.
Hospital emergency departments received 27,706,728 and freestanding emergency
departments 156,346. Together they are 27,863,074, or 92.50% of coded
destinations. A separate incident-urbanicity table contains 2,652,293 rural and
452,100 frontier events.

That is substantive emergency-routing context, but not county origin and
destination, travel duration, or road travel time. Submissions are voluntary,
activations are not unique patients, table denominators differ, public
state/county/ZIP identifiers are restricted, and remote-county coverage is not
complete.

Taxlane admits the official national EMS context but no candidate effect or
savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining target stays **$813.727B**, and both
analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
