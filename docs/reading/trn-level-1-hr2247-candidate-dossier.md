# TRN Level-1 H.R. 2247 candidate dossier

H.R. 2247 is selected as Taxlane's transportation cost-only modernization
candidate. The selection is conditional on enactment and appropriation and is
limited to a plain-language candidate explanation and CBO's official $18
million FY2026-FY2031 incremental outlay estimate.

The bill is not selected as a lower-cost, savings, named-fund, or receipt-rate
proposal. Candidate-specific access, authentication, equity, resilience, and
delivery protections are supported. Unrelated roadway and rate/savings gates
are reviewed as not applicable to the requested outputs.

TRN Level 1 is complete. Level 2 may rerun TRN-E with this dossier, but TRN-F
and every rate or savings output remain blocked.
