# REV Level-6 revenue-instrument dossier

The +11.0-point schedule now passes CORE-M's revenue-instrument interface. Its
assigned-rate output is tied to the same TY2026 CPS tax-unit liability base used
for scoring, the official 2026 legal rate schedule, and the CBO FY2026 fiscal
target context.

All gates required for a model planning rate card are ready. Official dynamic
scoring and balance certification are explicitly outside this release surface
and remain blocked. CORE-M therefore admits only a candidate explanation and
the assigned planning rate, allowing REV-F to start.
