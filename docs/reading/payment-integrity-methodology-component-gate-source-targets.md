evidence. If a source only repeats improper-payment totals, root-cause wording,
or broad process improvements, the field remains open and scoring remains
blocked.

## Boundary

These rows do not close fields, do not score programs, do not estimate savings,
do not identify waste, do not identify fraud, and do not claim recoverable
amounts.
