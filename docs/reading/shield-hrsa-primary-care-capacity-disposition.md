# SHIELD measures designation formulas—not the entire primary-care workforce

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's current HRSA primary-care capacity baseline. Of
7,682 current HPSA designations, 3,388 carry both physician FTE and shortage
values: all 2,838 area designations and 550 correctional facilities. Together
they record 10,635.4884 physician FTE and 12,267.0916 shortage, producing a
bounded derived need-met ratio of 46.44%.

The area formulas reproduce estimated served population within half a person
and shortage within 0.01 FTE for every designation. One record leaves a visible
one-person population rounding residual. The other 4,294 facility designations
have no FTE or shortage value and remain policy exclusions—not zeros.

This is real formula-capacity evidence, but it is not a deduplicated count of
physicians or affected people. The HRSA primary-care calculation excludes
nurse-practitioner and physician-assistant services and specified automatic or
service-based facility designations. It also does not establish CMS hospital
staffing, service-line availability, appointments, travel, quality, or
adequacy.

Taxlane therefore admits no candidate effect or savings. HLT does not reopen.
The remaining target stays **$813.727B**, and both analytical rate schedules
remain unchanged.
