## Why There Is No Peer Expected Value Yet

Federal spending is not the US transportation system. States and localities own
and finance major road, transit, airport, port, and maintenance responsibilities;
trust funds, user charges, private capital, and tax expenditures also affect the
comparison. A federal-only percentage cannot be compared directly with an
all-government international infrastructure measure.
