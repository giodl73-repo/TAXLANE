## Use Rule

Use this backlog to decide what evidence to collect next:

- Ask for reviewed sources, metrics, and outcome floors.
- Do not publish a savings estimate from these rows.
- Do not call a pressure row waste, fraud, abuse, or poor performance without a
  reviewed finding source and role-approved wording.
