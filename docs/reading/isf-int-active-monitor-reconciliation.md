# ISF and INT active-monitor reconciliation

Both active implementations remain valid monitors, but neither has crossed its
admission evidence gate.

For ISF, CDSS confirms that the relevant work and community-engagement change
began June 1, 2026. Only 61 days have elapsed. The CalFresh dashboard was
updated July 10 and STAT 47 was updated June 18, but a refreshed publication
date is not a candidate-coded recertification cohort. The replayed operations
month remains May 2026 and its error month remains February 2026. ISF therefore
waits for a complete candidate-coded cycle with service, rights, incidence,
adequacy, county-delivery, and realized-cost measures.

For INT, MCC still classifies the Nepal Compact as implementation. The amended
program is $550 million and its compact end date is September 9, 2026—39 days
after this audit. ENVOY's June 30 quarter-11 baseline records training and road
design, but not completed works, version-reconciled cost, local incidence,
safeguards, durable service, or independent outcomes. Compact closeout will be
a review trigger, not automatic outcome passage.

The result is two active monitors, zero failures, zero admissions, and no rate
change. Review occurs when qualifying owner evidence is published, not merely
because time passes.
