# SHIELD exposes EMS response tails without inventing a target result

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's calendar-2025 severity-1 response distribution. The
27,540 valid records have a 366-second median, 650-second p90, and 792-second
p95. Exactly 23,922 (86.86%) are at or below ten minutes and 3,618 are above;
borough p90s range from 593 to 698 seconds.

The official Preliminary Fiscal 2026 Mayor's Management Report separately
reports an FY2025 ambulance average of 8:49 and FY2026 target of 6:55 for
life-threatening emergencies. It also reports a combined ambulance/fire
FY2025 average of 7:45 and target of 6:00.

Those benchmarks cannot be scored against the extract. Fiscal year differs
from calendar year, Segment 1–3 differs from severity 1, call receipt differs
from incident creation, and identical arriving-unit scope is not proved. The
extract's ten-minute share is not Local Law Category 9 ALS compliance.

Taxlane therefore admits distribution and official-target context, but no
target miss, adequacy finding, candidate effect, or savings. HLT does not
reopen. The remaining target stays **$813.727B**, and both analytical rate
schedules remain unchanged.
