## Interpretation Boundary

OECD marks the U.S. estimates for caution because PISA sampling standards were not reached.
U.S. school participation was 51% before and 63%
after replacement, below PISA targets of 65% and 85%. The U.S. student-exclusion
rate was 6%, above the 5% maximum target. The 80% student response rate met its
target.

PISA Level 2 is not NAEP Proficient. The comparison cannot be used to splice
PISA percentages into the NAEP 2024 distribution. Nor is the OECD average an
automatic target: this record does not match education-system spending, prove
causality, or establish an expected spending level, waste, fraud, or savings.
The [OECD Resource Comparison](k12-oecd-resource-comparison.md) adds a matched
2022 institutional-resource peer, but annual resources still are not assigned
to the tested students or their cumulative prior inputs.
