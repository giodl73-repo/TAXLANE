# Local Law 119 requires borough reporting—not community-district reporting

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's enacted-law audit and corrects the prior deferred
boundary. Local Law 119 requires monthly and yearly reports to the Council and
Mayor, website posting, citywide results for each category, and disaggregation
by borough. It does not require community-district or division reporting.

The official website and citywide/borough report surface are observable. The
public semantic model contains 147 monthly labels from April 2014 through June
2026. This establishes meaningful publication custody, but not every statutory
duty: formal submission to the Council and Mayor was not verified, and the
audit did not locate a discrete yearly report.

Full statutory compliance therefore remains held for the corrected reason.
The law also specifies no minimum acceptable Category 9 share, so there is
still no legal or service pass/fail. No operational cause, outcome, adequacy,
candidate, cost, or savings follows. HLT does not reopen; the target remains
**$813.727B**, and both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
