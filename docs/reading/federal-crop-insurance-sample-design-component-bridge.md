# Federal Crop Insurance Sample-Design Component Bridge

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/federal_crop_insurance_sample_design_component_bridge.fy2024.v1.draft.json`.

This bridge closes one narrow internal component: the sampling-governance and
design attributes that official sources actually disclose. It does not close
the full `sample design` methodology field.

## Disclosed Component

RMA COM-23-001 says the FY2024 reporting-period review used 326 policies from
reinsurance year 2022. It describes the sample as statistically selected and
statistically valid and says selection considered the Approved Insurance
Provider servicing each policy.

FCIC/RMA's FY2024 financial report adds that payments were separated by AIP
into high, medium, and low payment tiers before an annual statistically valid
sample was pulled and reviewed.

The independent FY2024 PIIA performance audit identifies FCIC as a Phase 2 and
high-priority program, reports FCIC compliant with all six tested PIIA
criteria, and says the auditors reviewed USDA samples and the underlying
sampling methodologies for programs deemed susceptible to improper payments.
The relevant audit scope appears on printed pages 3-5 and 10, corresponding to
PDF file pages 7-9 and 14.

## Narrow Decision

Together, the official sources support an internal-only closure of the
disclosed sampling-governance/design component: 326 policies, reinsurance year
2022, AIP-aware selection, high/medium/low tiers, statistical-validity
designation, and independent audit review.

Full sample design remains open. The public sources do not disclose frame
construction, tier allocation or tier sample counts, selection probabilities,
randomization, replacement, nonresponse or incomplete-document treatment,
weights, the projection estimator, or the variance and confidence-interval
method.

## Governance Boundary

Other Matters item 3 on printed page 23—PDF file page 27, zero-based viewer
index 26—encourages USDA's Office of the Chief Financial Officer to conduct
more detailed reviews of Sampling and Estimation Methodology Plans. That
recommendation is important limiting evidence: independent audit review and
PIIA compliance establish governance oversight, but they do not make FCIC's
sample design publicly reproducible.

No program score or public claim about fraud, waste, debt, recovery,
prevention, or savings is permitted by this component closure.
