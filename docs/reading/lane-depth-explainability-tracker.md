# Lane depth and explainability tracker

Record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/lane_depth_explainability_tracker.v1.draft.json`

Plain-English answer: we are not done filling out the depth of each lane or the public explainability layer.

This tracker separates three things:

- lane depth: the evidence needed to understand a lane;
- public explainability: the plain-language packet taxpayers can read;
- solver readiness: the audited annual values needed before rates or budget balance can be calculated.

Transportation is the deepest pilot. Several lanes have partial depth cards.
Every lane still has missing baseline, source-custody, scenario, outcome-floor,
modernization-transition, public-explainer, or solver-mapping work.

Every lane must eventually answer these public questions:

- What does this lane do?
- What are taxpayers paying now?
- Who is served or protected?
- What outcomes matter before any lower-cost scenario is admissible?
- What would count as overspending, underfunding, or only a review-needed signal?
- What can technology change, and what transition risks must be paid for?
- What evidence is still missing?
- What claims are blocked?

This is not a solver run, not target-cost selection, not rate calculation, not a public rate card, not a tax proposal, not a savings estimate, not a waste finding, not a fraud finding, not a department-cut instruction, not a technology-savings claim, and not a balanced-budget claim.
