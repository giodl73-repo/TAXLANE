# Taxlane internal rate-analysis completion

Taxlane tested fourteen uniform ordinary-rate uplifts with three taxable-income
response cases each. The preferred central schedule is 21/23/33/35/43/46/48.
It produces an $819.220 billion first-year cash proxy and a $5.416 billion gap
after subtracting the $77 million administration ceiling, against the frozen
$813.727 billion target.

The 22/24/34/36/44/47/49 schedule is the smallest tested schedule covering all
three behavior cases without additional macro stress. A +12.6-point schedule
is the first to cover all nine combinations of behavior and the deliberately
adverse 0%, -2.5%, and -5% internal macro stresses. That last schedule is a
stress ceiling, not the baseline recommendation.

PAY remains non-additive, NET remains endogenous, and no spending savings were
added. REV and NET are the only tracks with changed analysis; the other thirteen
track results remain valid. These are Taxlane analytical outputs only.
