# VA PLTSS Documentation and Recoverability Boundary

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/va_pltss_documentation_recoverability_boundary.fy2025.v1.draft.json`.

## Result

Current VA policy supplies a clear agencywide classification boundary. Missing
documentation that prevents determining the right recipient or right amount is
an unknown payment, reported separately. A documentation or agency-procedure
failure may still be proper when sufficient evidence establishes the right
recipient, right amount, and legal compliance. Monetary loss is an overpayment
that in theory could or should be recovered; a technically improper payment is
non-monetary. Recovery occurs only when a monetary-loss payment is returned.

VA's recovery-audit policy sharpens the cash boundary: contractor compensation
is based on actual funds certified recovered, credited, or realized, and VA
records the related service obligation only after collection is certified.
Estimated, identified, billed, or outstanding amounts are not recovered cash.

## PLTSS limit

The FY2025 AFR identifies PLTSS causes and says incorrect-amount claims may lead
to additional payments or bills of collection. It does not map Basic Ordering
Agreement, late-claim, signature, reconciliation, authorization, payment, or
level-of-care documentation defects to proper, unknown, technical/non-monetary,
monetary-loss, established-debt, appeal, write-off, collection, or recovery
outcomes.

The two policies were approved after the payments tested for the FY2025 report.
They are current governance and interpretive support, not proof of how each
historical PLTSS sampled claim was classified or disposed.

## Decision

The agency-policy classification and certified-recovery boundary is recorded as
a narrow internal component. The full documentation-defect-versus-recoverable-
overpayment field remains open, so PLTSS remains two fields closed and six open.
No score or public claim about debt, collectibility, recovery, fraud, waste, or
savings is allowed.
