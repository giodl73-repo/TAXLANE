# REV Level 7 integrated certification and score handoff

REV Level 7 completes TAXLANE's internal certification work. The all-15 package
is complete, dependency-correct, reproducible, and ready to hand to an official
scorer. The handoff now includes a filing-status-complete substantive policy
specification, a ten-year annual score-request workbook, and a nonofficial
discussion draft keyed to the current §1(j) structure. The discussion draft
reduces preparation friction but does not close the Legislative Counsel gate
and is not statutory text. The handoff carries the
21/23/33/35/43/46/48 planning schedule,
unchanged thresholds, the $813.727 billion FY2026 target, the $819.220 billion
model cash proxy, zero admitted FY2026 spending reduction, and the conditional
TRN cost note outside the fiscal package.

That is not the same as formal fiscal certification. A certified rate or
balance requires Legislative Counsel conforming text, authorized submission,
and official CBO/JCT conventional,
spending, interaction, macro, compliance, distribution, debt-service, and
timing work. Those outputs have not been received and remain null. The public
rate card therefore stays a model planning surface, and no lower schedule is
supported by admitted spending.
