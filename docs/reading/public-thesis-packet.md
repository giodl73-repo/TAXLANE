# Public Thesis Packet

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/public_thesis_packet.v1.draft.json`.

TAXLANE can build a transparent fiscal operating model that calculates effective funding rates only after assigned bases, behavior, incidence,
distribution, administration, interactions, outcome floors, fund accounting,
reserves, emergencies, and endogenous net interest reconcile.

Before those gates pass, the system can publish what is known, what is blocked,
and what evidence is still needed.

This is a role-reviewed public thesis packet, not a statutory-rate proposal,
effective-rate publication, public rate card, tax proposal, savings estimate,
waste finding, fraud finding, department-cut instruction, technology-savings
claim, solver result, pilot selection, or balanced-budget claim.

Public language must say "overspending risk" rather than unsupported "waste."
An improper-payment or methodology gap is not fraud. A benchmark gap is not
recoverable savings.

Technology is a transition path, not an automatic cut. Modernization starts
with implementation cost, training cost, cybersecurity, privacy, fallback
operations, service risk, and stress cases. Lower funding may be recognized
only after measured productivity and service floors pass.

Blocked rates and not-calculated values are first-class public outcomes. They
mean the model is naming a blocker instead of filling a missing value with zero
or publishing an unsupported rate.

Fairness requires burden and distribution analysis: legal perimeter, economic
incidence, taxpayer burden, employer burden, agency burden, compliance burden,
distribution by income, and beneficiary or service-floor protection.

The balanced-budget claim remains blocked until every budget row, fund, offset,
reserve, emergency rule, interaction score, distribution score, macro feedback,
endogenous interest path, and unrounded deficit gap reconcile.

The eight-role review passes this packet only as explanatory design with P1
blockers. It does not approve rates, tax proposals, savings estimates, waste or
fraud findings, department cuts, technology-savings claims, pilot selection, or
balanced-budget language.

Every output placeholder remains null. Every claim boolean remains false.
