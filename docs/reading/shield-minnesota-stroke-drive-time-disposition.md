# SHIELD adds modeled stroke access—not observed ambulance travel

Taxlane replayed SHIELD's bounded July 2026 Minnesota stroke-system drive-time
baseline. Minnesota Department of Health reports 123 designated stroke
hospitals, with 97% of Minnesotans living within a modeled 30-minute drive and
99% within a modeled 60-minute drive.

This is SHIELD's first current, emergency-service-specific drive-time coverage
benchmark. It establishes a statewide population-access result for a time-
sensitive condition. It does not observe an ambulance trip, patient origin and
destination, response time, scene-to-hospital duration, or county estimate.
The published summary provides neither machine-readable substate geography nor
an exact count of residents outside the bands.

Hospital designation also does not prove current staffing, readiness,
throughput, transfer acceptance, outcomes, affordability, unmet need, or
adequacy. Minnesota cannot stand in for other conditions, states, or the
nation.

Taxlane therefore admits the official modeled statewide access context but no
candidate effect or savings. HLT does not reopen. The remaining target stays
**$813.727B**, and both analytical rate schedules remain unchanged.
