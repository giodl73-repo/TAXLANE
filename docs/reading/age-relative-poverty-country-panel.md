Values are the percentage of the age group living in households below 50
percent of national median equivalised disposable income. Singapore is absent.

There is no common observation year for all 11 peers. The older-person panel
therefore exposes actual country years from 2020-2024. The child panel uses 2021
except for Australia's 2020 fallback. These are relative national thresholds,
not one shared absolute living standard, and they exclude household wealth.

The outcomes remain separate from 2022 SOCX spending and the forward-looking
2024 pension replacement model. No spending/output efficiency rank is made.
