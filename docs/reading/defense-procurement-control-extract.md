# Defense Procurement Control Extract

## Purpose

This packet records the first GAO Weapon Systems Annual Assessment probe for the
defense procurement-control queue item.

Machine rows:
`data/derived/efficiency_pressure/extracts/defense_procurement_control_first_pass.jsonl`.

## Extracted Probe

The first pass locks 6 rows from GAO-25-107569. It captures portfolio cost
growth for Major Defense Acquisition Programs, the Sentinel and T-AO 205
program cost-growth signals, the 20 most expensive middle-tier acquisition
programs, leading-practice gaps, and schedule-risk context.

## Boundary

These rows are procurement-control probes only. They are not readiness
judgments, waste findings, program-cancellation recommendations, or savings
estimates. The next extract must attach program baselines, current estimates,
schedule variance, root-cause or corrective-action evidence, and mission or
readiness floor evidence before any scored cost-down claim.
