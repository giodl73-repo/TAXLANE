# REV Level-4 first-year cash-timing bridge

The December 2024 CBO/JCT option took effect in January and scored $82.3
billion in its first fiscal year versus $106.3 billion in the next. Their ratio,
77.423 percent, provides an official first-year realization proxy.

Applying that ratio to the behavioral tax-unit model requires $1.051023 trillion
of full-year liability change to meet the $813.727 billion FY2026 cash target.
A +10.922-point central run produces $1.051017 trillion, or a first-year cash
proxy of $813.722 billion. The approximate elasticity range is +10.159 to
+11.853 points.

Because the timing anchor predates current law and does not directly score this
schedule, this is a fiscal-timing proxy rather than an official cash score.
