H.R. 1702 is not enacted and is a net cost rather than a spending reduction.
CBO does not estimate case delay, aged pending workload, counsel, liberty,
victim notice, evidence integrity, disparity, durable resolution, or savings.
The pack also cannot close the distinct program and beneficiary floors on
Taxlane's existing selected-DOJ-grant reduction.

Taxlane therefore custodies the identity but admits no FY2026 reduction. The
$813.727B target and 21/23/33/35/43/46/48 planning schedule do not change.
