# HLT site-neutral imaging current-law dependency audit

The selected all-off-campus imaging option is not current law as of August 1,
2026. Its January 2026 effective date is an assumption in CBO's December 2024
budget option, not evidence of enactment.

CMS did implement the adjacent drug-administration alternative for CY2026. For
CY2027, CMS has proposed a narrower policy covering imaging without contrast at
excepted off-campus provider-based departments. The proposal would exempt rural
Sole Community Hospitals and CMS estimates lower Medicare spending, premiums,
and beneficiary cost sharing. Those are useful implementation and protection
design inputs, but the rule is not final, its scope is not all imaging, and its
estimates are neither observed results nor a rescore of CBO option 60908.

HLT therefore closes the current-law dependency question and moves to
publication-triggered monitoring. The candidate remains at zero admitted
FY2026 savings. It can reopen when CMS finalizes an imaging policy, CBO publishes
a matching current-baseline score, or candidate-coded service-floor outcomes
become available. The active dependency audit moves to DEF.
