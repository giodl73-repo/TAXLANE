# DEF Level-3 defense current-law scale bridge

DEF now has a usable timing envelope, but not a current-law rescore. CBO's
December 2024 option reduces DoD outlays by $959 billion from FY2026 through
FY2034: $15 billion in FY2026, rising to $167 billion in FY2034.

The February 2026 baseline shows $8.852 trillion of broader national-defense
outlays over the same years. The official option equals 10.834 percent of that
broader denominator, but that percentage is diagnostic only: the option covers
DoD, whereas the baseline series includes all Function 050 discretionary and
mandatory defense outlays. No option value was rescaled.

The mismatch matters more after the 2025 reconciliation act. CBO's current
baseline includes $156 billion of added mandatory-defense outlays through 2035,
and the FY2026 DoD request routes $113 billion of reconciliation funding through
a request whose enduring acquisition cost remains uncertain. Those amounts
cannot be silently combined with, or subtracted from, the older option.

Use the annual option path only to judge fiscal scale and timing. Do not call it
a force plan, a 2026 score, admitted savings, or a solver input.
