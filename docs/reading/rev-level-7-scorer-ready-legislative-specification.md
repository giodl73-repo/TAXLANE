# REV Level 7 scorer-ready legislative specification

The planning card is now specified as a permanent scoring case beginning with
tax year 2026. It changes only the seven ordinary individual-income marginal
rates to 21, 23, 33, 35, 43, 46, and 48 percent. All current-law filing-status
breakpoints, later-year inflation adjustments, income definitions, deductions,
credits, preferential capital-gain and dividend rates, AMT, payroll taxes, and
other federal taxes remain unchanged.

The packet states all 2026 breakpoints for single, joint/surviving-spouse,
separate, and head-of-household filers; the FY2026–FY2035 score window; the
January 14, 2026 baseline cutoff; withholding, estimated-tax, return, software,
and late-enactment transition instructions; and the exact seven Tax-Calculator
parameters used by the model. No spending instrument is included.

This closes the substantive policy-specification gap. It is not Legislative
Counsel bill text, enacted law, or an official score.
