# Social Security scenario gate

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/social_security_scenario_gate.v1.draft.json`.

This is a readiness gate, not a Social Security reform plan.

The current FY2025 context records $1,580.673B of Social Security cost,
$1,283.736B of dedicated OASDI receipts, and a $296.937B residual trust-fund-drawdown or general-pool gap. That gap is not a savings target and does not by itself choose a tax or benefit policy.

Before a central or stress scenario can be solver-eligible, Taxlane still needs
a demographic path, a 75-year OASDI trust-fund path, explicit wage-base, rate,
eligibility-age and benefit-formula levers, administration and transition cost,
distribution by income and birth cohort, and passed adequacy and old-age-poverty
floors.

Public old-age spending comparisons are context, not a Social Security target.
Modeled pension replacement rates are adequacy context, not a spending-efficiency
score. Old-age poverty is an outcome floor, not a spending cut rule.

Until those inputs exist, central and stress Social Security scenarios remain unscored and solver-ineligible.
