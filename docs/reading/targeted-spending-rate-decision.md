# Targeted spending-and-rate decision

Taxlane tested the two most concrete large-lane spending candidates against the
completed internal rate analysis.

The HLT candidate applies site-neutral payment to imaging in all off-campus
hospital outpatient departments. CBO's December 2024 option reports $0.4
billion in FY2026 and $7.6 billion over FY2025-FY2034, but only one of seven
required gates is ready. Imaging-specific beneficiary, access, rural, quality,
behavior, and CY2026 overlap evidence remains unresolved.

The DEF candidate gives the earlier topline option a specific proportional
active-force strategy while preserving reserve units. Its headline FY2026
amount is $15 billion, but six gates remain unresolved: current FYDP allocation,
readiness stress, transition costs, program schedules, allied posture, and a
current-law rescore.

Neither headline amount enters the package. Admitted FY2026 primary reduction
is therefore zero; PAY contributes zero as a non-additive overlay, and NET's
endogenous zero-input result remains zero. The PAY-NET-REV target stays
$813.727 billion.

Consequently, the preferred central Taxlane schedule remains
21/23/33/35/43/46/48. This is a completed, evidence-bounded rate-down decision,
not a claim that spending reforms can never lower the required rate.
