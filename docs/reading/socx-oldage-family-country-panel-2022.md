the Netherlands, Poland, and Singapore have no 2022 observation in the bounded

Old-age and survivors spending is broader than United States Social Security,
while public spending alone is incomplete where mandatory occupational or
private pensions are important. The family panel separates cash and services;
it does not splice in tax breaks from a different-year source.

These values describe resource levels and composition. They do not rank benefit
adequacy, old-age poverty, childcare access, child outcomes, efficiency, or
savings.
