## Interpretation firewall

Administrative events are not substantive evidence. Acknowledgment and fee
handling do not prove responsive records exist. A no-records response reports
the result of the described search, not zero findings, appeals, debts, or
collections. A denial or redaction does not establish nonexistence. Produced
files cannot support field review until custody, privacy, cohort, state-
transition, and weighted-projection gates pass.

## Timing and appeal

The intake records the ordinary 20-working-day determination target, unusual-
circumstances notices and narrowing opportunities, the 90-calendar-day
administrative-appeal deadline, and the ordinary 20-working-day appeal-decision
target. Receipt by the proper component, routing, clarification and fee tolls,
and determination versus production are recorded separately. Liaison or OGIS
contact does not replace or automatically toll a timely administrative appeal.
These fields support monitoring; they are not legal conclusions.
