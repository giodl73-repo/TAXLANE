# PAY Level-4 full-DMF accounting and floor audit

Two of eight gates pass: the control has a bounded causal attribution method,
and its reported benefit is net of stated implementation and operating costs.
That is enough to call the candidate promising, but not enough to count savings.

Six gates remain blocked:

- prevention, identification, and recovery are not separated;
- owning program components and lane overlaps are unknown;
- appropriation, fund, and Treasury cash disposition are unknown;
- calendar-2024 evidence is not a recurring FY2026 score;
- notice, appeal, false-positive, and remediation outcomes are unquantified;
- access, distribution, and service-continuity effects are unquantified.

PAY therefore remains a non-additive overlay. The next evidence target is the
same transaction cohort with benefit type, program ownership, cash disposition,
fiscal timing, appeal, false-positive, and distribution fields.
