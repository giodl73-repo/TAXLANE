## Result

Official RMA sources publish the governance path after a Final Finding. RMA
uploads the finding to CARS, which starts a 45-day period for an AIP to request a
final administrative determination. The AIP must identify the disputed
provisions, explain the asserted error, and submit rebuttal evidence. The 2022
Standard
Reinsurance Agreement requires a final administrative decision before judicial
review.

When FCIC can determine the correct amount, the SRA provides for corrected
reporting, payment of underpaid policyholders, and Company payment to FCIC for
overpaid indemnities, underpaid premiums, and excess subsidy. But FCIC may waive, reduce, or delay
repayment for continued program delivery. FCIC may use setoff
for unpaid amounts across reinsurance years with interest, while the Company
retains administrative appeal rights over the indebtedness.

The SRA also requires timely written notice when a review reveals that the
Company owes FCIC a debt. Interest on a written specific amount can accrue while
an appeal is pending. That obligation mechanic still does not establish final
collectibility or cash collection.
