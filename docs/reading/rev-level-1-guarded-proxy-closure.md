# REV Level-1 guarded proxy closure

REV Level 1 is complete for the bounded planning objective. The selected case
adds a uniform **2.760 percentage points** to the 2026 individual-income-tax
schedule after assuming, rather than scoring, a $500 billion annual primary
spending correction. The resulting proxy schedule is 12.760, 14.760, 24.760,
26.760, 34.760, 37.760, and 39.760 percent.

This is the useful numerical answer the present evidence supports. It is not an
assigned statutory rate or an official JCT/CBO score. The proxy rescales a
December 2024 option after Public Law 119-21 and does not model nonlinear
behavior, tax-unit distribution, administration burden, macro feedback, or a
complete tax-year/fiscal-year match. Formal REV-F and the CORE-N rate-card
surface remain blocked.
