# Batch 3 TRN and REV two-level closure

TRN and REV have completed the final two internal advancement levels. CBO's
current H.R. 2247 estimate still reports $18 million of 2026-2031 outlays
subject to appropriation, with less than $0.5 million in FY2026. The bill
remains introduced rather than enacted, so Taxlane retains the conditional
cost note but admits neither an implementation path nor savings.

REV's legislative specification, ten-year workbook, and nonofficial discussion
draft are internally complete. Authorized submission, Legislative Counsel
text, and an official CBO/JCT response remain external. The 21/23/33/35/43/46/
48 schedule therefore remains planning-only. This closes the internal
all-fifteen two-level wave without claiming the proper rate or a balanced
budget; the integrated PAY-NET-REV reconciliation is next.
