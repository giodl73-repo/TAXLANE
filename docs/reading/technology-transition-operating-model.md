# Technology Transition Operating Model

Machine record:
`data/derived/breadth_benchmark_matrix/technology_transition_operating_model.v1.draft.json`.

This is a technology transition operating model, not a technology-savings claim,
department-cut instruction, target-cost reduction, rate reduction, procurement
recommendation, vendor recommendation, budget score, or balanced-budget claim.
Technology is a transition path, not an automatic cut.

The model requires each modernization scenario to name implementation cost,
training and change-management cost, cybersecurity constraints, privacy
constraints, fallback and resilience planning, service-quality and access risk,
annual phase-in, measured productivity, and a stress case.

The four required phases are:

- baseline;
- transition;
- measured productivity;
- stress.

Year 0 and early transition years may increase outlays because implementation,
training, fallback operations, and remediation costs are real costs. Years 1-3
productivity remains provisional unless measured. Years 3-10 may recognize a
lower funding requirement only if access, quality, equity, adequacy/resilience,
delivery-feasibility, lane-specific floors, and score provenance pass.

All technology-savings, target-cost-reduction, rate-reduction, budget-score,
department-cut, procurement-recommendation, and solver cash-flow outputs remain
null.

Every claim boolean remains false.
