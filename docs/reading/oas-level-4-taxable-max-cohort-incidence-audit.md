# OAS Level-4 taxable-maximum cohort and incidence audit

Two gates pass: the instrument and annual aggregate score are official, and the
current 2026 trust-fund and taxable-payroll context is available.

Six gates remain blocked:

- benefit-formula and lifetime effects by earnings and birth cohort;
- worker, employer, and self-employed economic incidence;
- poverty, adequacy, survivor, and disability policy/stress floors;
- payroll administration, compliance, behavior, and transition cost;
- tax-unit allocation and joint distribution with REV;
- a 2026-baseline score and post-2034 actuarial result.

The option remains a serious solvency candidate, but neither a complete
solvency package nor spending savings. Its aggregate income-tax offset is
already embedded in the CBO/JCT score, so adding the option to REV without a
joint tax-unit bridge would double count interactions.
