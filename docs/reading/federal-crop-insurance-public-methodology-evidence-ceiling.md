# Federal Crop Insurance Public Methodology Evidence Ceiling

Machine record:

## Result

OMB M-21-19 requires statistically valid payment-integrity estimates to produce
point estimates and confidence intervals, then directs agencies to upload the
Sampling and Estimation Methodology Plan and checklist to the agency's secure MAX page.
That establishes the controlled governance channel; it does not make
FCIC's plan publicly reproducible.

The FY2025 FCIC/RMA report supplies a later-period public continuity check. Its
unaudited Management's Discussion and Analysis repeats premium subsidy,
Administrative and Operating expense, and indemnities; high-, medium-, and
low-payment AIP tiers; annual statistically valid samples; and actual improper-
payment rates of 2.43 percent for 2024 and 3.29 percent for 2025.

## Decision

This closes zero current methodology fields. FCIC remains at four closed fields
and four open fields: sample design, estimation method, exclusion rules, and
recoverable-savings basis.

The public sources still do not provide the FCIC rate formula, dollar-projection
estimator, weights or tier adjustments, finite-population treatment, variance
and confidence-interval computation, nonresponse treatment, aggregation rules,
or detailed inclusion and exclusion treatment. The repeated FY2025 description
does not prove that undisclosed FY2024 and FY2025 mechanics were identical.

The accounting discussion of estimated insurance losses is not the improper-
payment estimator. Likewise, Harvest Price Exclusion is an insurance option,
not a sampling exclusion rule.

## Claim firewall

No program score or public claim about fraud, waste, identified debt,
collectibility, recovery, prevention, control cost, or savings is allowed. A
future closure requires authorized access to the actual FCIC plan or an
equivalent public program-specific disclosure.
